# Sierra Leone (SLE) — research log

Built from scratch on 2026-06-19. Web tools (WebSearch/WebFetch) loaded and used throughout. No seed file existed.

## Shape of the ledgers
Asymmetric by design (3 victim, 1 perpetrator), and the asymmetry is honest. Sierra Leone's deepest victimhood is the Atlantic slave trade and colonial conquest; its perpetrator record is real but proportionally smaller and concentrated in the same 1991-2002 civil war that dominates the victim ledger. The dual lens is unusually literal here: the civil war was overwhelmingly intra-Sierra-Leonean, so the same people appear as victims (sle-3) and perpetrators (sle-4) in the same conflict. Nothing was invented to balance; the lighter perpetrator column is left standing and explained in the country note.

## Events
- **sle-1 (victim, STR, UNVERIFIED)** — Bunce Island / Atlantic slave trade, c.1670-1808. Largest British slave castle on the Rice Coast. Operators: Royal African Company → Grant, Sargent & Oswald (Richard Oswald) → John & Alexander Anderson. Captives shipped chiefly to South Carolina/Georgia rice plantations. Toll 30,000-50,000 PERSONS TRAFFICKED (not a death count — unit caveat is explicit in toll.note). Low ~30,000 associated with Joseph Opala's research (leading scholarly authority); high ~50,000 from popular/heritage sources. Documented sub-figure: Grant, Oswald & Co. exported >12,500 from Bunce Island 1749-1773. Tiered STR (structural mass-death/dispossession system, not war/political-purge/disease). Marked UNVERIFIED because the 30k/50k totals are relayed via blog/press, not traced directly to Opala's published scholarship, and the unit is enslaved-persons not deaths.
- **sle-2 (victim, WAR, UNVERIFIED)** — Hut Tax War of 1898. Britain (Gov. Frederic Cardew) imposed a hut tax; northern Temne rising under Bai Bureh + southern Mende rising; British colonial troops suppressed both. Firm figure: ~96 rebels executed afterward. Total war dead not aggregated in sources → high left null. Bai Bureh now a national anti-colonial hero. Marked UNVERIFIED: single tertiary source (Wikipedia), and late-20th-c. scholarship revises Bai Bureh's "instigator" framing.
- **sle-3 (victim, WAR, RESEARCHED)** — Civilians in the Sierra Leone Civil War, 1991-2002. RUF (Foday Sankoh, backed by Charles Taylor's Liberia) + AFRC. Signature atrocity: machete limb amputation ("long sleeve / short sleeve"); mass killing; systematic rape. Jan 1999 Freetown assault ("Operation No Living Thing"): commonly cited ~6,000-7,000 dead, mostly civilians; HRW documented several hundred killed/maimed by amputation, 26 double arm amputations, mosque massacre of 66 on 22 Jan, ~51,000 left homeless. Toll: ~50,000-70,000 dead (best-supported); ~2-2.5M displaced. The higher ~200,000 figure seen in some press conflates death with displacement — flagged disputed, NOT adopted.
- **sle-4 (perpetrator, WAR, RESEARCHED)** — State forces (SLA + NPRC junta) and state-allied CDF/Kamajor militia atrocities in the civil war. SLA/NPRC: extrajudicial executions of suspected rebels (incl. 26 alleged plotters shot without trial Dec 1992 per US State Dept 1997 report), "sobel" (soldier-by-day/rebel-by-night) predation; >180 summary executions of RUF prisoners by govt/ECOMOG forces in Jan 1999 Freetown fighting (HRW). CDF/Kamajors: summary killings, looting, cruel treatment of suspected RUF collaborators. Special Court for Sierra Leone convicted CDF leaders Fofana & Kondewa (2007; on appeal May 2008 crimes-against-humanity convictions entered, sentences raised to 15 yrs Fofana / 20 yrs Kondewa); Sam Hinga Norman died 2007 before judgment. TRC statistical record: 40,242 violations / 14,995 victims — RUF ~59%, AFRC ~9.8%, SLA ~6.8% (2,724), CDF ~6% (2,419); SLA+CDF together ~13% (~5,000+ violations). Toll left NULL (no clean state-forces death count); proportional share documented instead.

## Contested / could not be verified
- Bunce Island lifetime total: genuinely uncertain (30k vs 50k); the 30k figure traces to Joseph Opala but was reached via secondary summaries, not his original publication; unit is enslaved-persons, not deaths. → UNVERIFIED.
- Wider Sierra Leone region slave-export total: not cleanly aggregated in sources consulted (SlaveVoyages groups Bunce/Banana Islands under "Sierra Leone" embarkation but no single regional total surfaced). Bunce Island used as best-documented node.
- Hut Tax War total death toll: only the ~96 executions are quantified; combat dead and the several-hundred Krio/colonial deaths in the Mende rising are not aggregated → high null. Bai Bureh "instigator" framing contested by modern scholarship.
- Civil war total dead: ~50,000-70,000 is best-supported; ~75,000 and up to ~200,000 appear in press but the high figures conflate deaths with the ~2-2.5M displaced. Adopted 50k-70k, flagged disputed.
- State-forces specific death toll: does not exist as a clean number → null; proportional TRC share used instead.
- HRDAG/Benetech PDF (Truth and Myth in Sierra Leone) returned as binary/corrupted on fetch; the TRC statistical breakdown was instead taken from the Sierra Leone TRC Appendix 1 fetch and corroborated against the search summary of the HRDAG paper (RUF 60.5%/59.2%, AFRC 9.8%, SLA 6.8%, CDF 6%; small variances between the two reflect rounding/version).

Joseph Opala confirmed as a real historian and the recognized Bunce Island authority; the ~30,000 (1670-1807) figure is widely attributed to his research and corroborated across secondary sources; the ~50,000 upper bound is corroborated (Face2Face Africa, TalkAfricana, CNN-derived). FALSIFICATION CAUGHT: the RHS-blog cite claimed it carried the ">12,500 exported by Grant, Oswald & Co. 1749-1773" figure — it does NOT. That figure is real but lives in the Understanding Slavery Initiative (understandingslavery.com) and the University of Edinburgh "Managing Imperial Legacies" blog; I verified it there and added a separate, correctly-attributed source for it. CNN URL is genuine but geo-blocked (HTTP 451) on direct fetch — existence/content confirmed via its visitsierraleone.org reproduction. STR tier CORRECT (structural slave-trade engine of mass death/dispossession, not war/purge/disease). Kept UNVERIFIED for two standing reasons, now stated in toll.note as a quarantine: (a) the ~30k figure was reached via secondary summaries, not an Opala primary publication, and (b) the unit is enslaved-persons, not deaths.
- **sle-2 (Hut Tax War 1898, WAR) — VERIFIED.** Wikipedia source verified; Gov. - **sle-3 (Civil-war civilians, WAR) — VERIFIED (upgraded from RESEARCHED).** HRW "Shocking War Crimes" (24 June 1999) verified at the cited URL; every attached figure confirmed verbatim (26 double amputations, mosque massacre of 66 on 22 Jan, ~51,000 homeless, >50,000 dead, >180 govt/ECOMOG summary executions).
