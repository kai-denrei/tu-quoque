# Sudan (SDN) — research log

Researched 2026-06-18 from scratch (no prior seed). SDN = Republic of Sudan (Khartoum state). South Sudan (SSD) is a separate entry from 2011; intra-southern atrocities (e.g. the 1991 Bor massacre) belong there, not here, and were deliberately excluded.

## Ledger shape

- **Done to them (victim):** 2 events — both pre-20th-century.
  - sdn-1 Mahdist-era demographic collapse (1883–1898), STR, status UNVERIFIED.
  - sdn-2 Anglo-Egyptian reconquest / Battle of Omdurman (1896–1899), WAR, status RESEARCHED.
- **In their name (perpetrator):** 3 events — all modern/contemporary, all authored by the Khartoum state.
  - sdn-3 Second Sudanese Civil War: engineered famine, slave raids, scorched earth (1983–2005), POL.
  - sdn-4 Nuba Mountains campaign (1992–93), EXT.
  - sdn-5 Darfur genocide (2003–2005+), EXT.

The asymmetry (victim events sit in the 19th century; the heavy modern record is perpetrator-side) is real and is stated in the country note. No event was invented to balance the ledgers.

## What is well established

- **Second Civil War ~2 million dead / ~4 million displaced** — widely cited aggregate (war + famine + disease). Confirmed via Wikipedia (Second Sudanese Civil War) and a CFR/Britannica-consistent figure surfaced in search. Treated as an aggregate range 1–2M because most deaths were indirect and uncountable.
- **1998 Bahr el-Ghazal famine** — HRW report "Famine in Sudan, 1998" documents Khartoum's relief-flight ban as deliberate starvation; >70,000 dead, possibly several hundred thousand. Used to anchor the "engineered famine" claim in sdn-3.
- **Oilfield scorched earth** — HRW "Sudan, Oil, and Human Rights" (2003) documents government/militia clearance of oilfield civilians. Anchors the scorched-earth claim in sdn-3.
- **Nuba Mountains ~100,000 dead 1992–93** — USHMM page cites a 1998 US Committee for Refugees estimate (100,000 of ~1M population); African Rights/de Waal "Facing Genocide" gives 60,000–70,000. Both ends sourced.
- **Darfur** — US Powell 2004 genocide finding; ICC arrest warrants 2009 (war crimes/crimes against humanity) and 2010 (added 3 genocide counts vs Fur, Masalit, Zaghawa) — first ICC genocide charges and first against a sitting head of state. ICC case page (icc-cpi.int/darfur/albashir) used as a primary-kind source.
- **Omdurman** — ~10,000 Mahdist dead vs ~48 Anglo-Egyptian dead, 2 Sept 1898 (Wikipedia Battle of Omdurman; Britannica). Lopsided colonial killing; clean WAR victim event.

## What is contested / soft

- **Mahdist population decline** — figures range from a pre-revolt ~7–8M down to ~2–3M (Metelits, Inside Insurgency 2009, cited in the Mahdist State article). This is a population-DECLINE estimate, not a death count, and rests on pre-census approximations plus a colonial-era census of a just-devastated population. Marked status UNVERIFIED and flagged heavily in toll.note; tier STR (demographic collapse, not deliberate group destruction). The decline range (~3.5–5.5M) is my own arithmetic on the cited endpoints, labelled as such.
- **Darfur toll** — spans ~98,000/100,000 to ~400,000 depending on period (2003–05 vs to 2008) and whether famine/disease deaths are included. GAO-07-24 explicitly flags methodological divergence. UN commission of inquiry found atrocity but disputed genocidal intent; US called it genocide; ICC charged genocide. Noted in toll and tier_reasoning.
- **Civil-war toll** — the ~2M number is an aggregate, mostly indirect; not enumerable. Range given as 1–2M.

## Could NOT verify / deliberately omitted

- No precise death count exists for the 1888–90 Mahdist famine alone beyond "hundreds of thousands"; left in summary, not converted to a toll figure.
- Campaign-wide Anglo-Egyptian reconquest deaths (beyond Omdurman) are not precisely fixed; toll restricted to the Omdurman battle figure with a note that the campaign total was higher.
- Did not attempt a separate slave-trade / Turco-Egyptian-era victim event — the pre-Mahdist slave economy is real but the SDN/SSD split and sourcing thinness made a clean, non-overlapping event hard to land within scope; the muraheleen slave raids are captured inside sdn-3 as perpetrator conduct.

## Audit

Adversarial source-integrity + tier audit, 2026-06-18. Assumption: every citation fake until proven otherwise. Each source was attacked to falsify it — does the work/author exist, and does it support the *specific* claim (toll endpoint / tier / summary) it is attached to.

### Verdict
- **Country status: UNVERIFIED** (4 of 5 events VERIFIED; sdn-1 remains UNVERIFIED, so the country cannot be VERIFIED).
- sdn-1 Mahdist demographic collapse — **UNVERIFIED** (quarantined; see misattribution below + inherent pre-census softness).
- sdn-2 Omdurman / reconquest — **VERIFIED**.
- sdn-3 Second Civil War — **VERIFIED**.
- sdn-4 Nuba Mountains — **VERIFIED**.

### Source integrity — what was independently confirmed
- **sdn-1**: Claire Metelits, *Inside Insurgency* (NYU Press, 2009, p.37) is a real work and IS the citation on the Mahdist State article ("Before the revolt, roughly 8 million people lived in Sudan; an Egyptian census afterwards recorded barely 2.5 million"). Population fell ~7–8.5M → ~2–3.5M, "more than half." 1888–90 famine (hundreds of thousands dead) and the Khalifa Abdallahi massacre of the grain-farming Juhaina both confirmed (Mahdist War article).
- **sdn-2**: Omdurman ~10,000 Mahdist dead, ~10,000 wounded, 5,000 captured vs ~48 Anglo-Egyptian dead / ~434 wounded, 2 Sept 1898, Kitchener — confirmed (Wikipedia, Britannica). Anglo-Egyptian conquest article supports the reconquest/tier context.
- **sdn-3**: ~2M dead (war/famine/disease) + ~4M displaced, 1983–2005 — confirmed (Wikipedia). HRW "Famine in Sudan, 1998" confirmed: >70,000 dead Bahr el-Ghazal, Feb 4–Apr 2 1998 relief-flight ban, "starvation as a method of combat." HRW "Sudan, Oil, and Human Rights" (2003) confirmed: scorched-earth clearance of hundreds of thousands from oilfields. Muraheleen slave raids confirmed exactly: Feb 1986 ~2,000 and Feb 1987 ~1,000 women/children taken.
- **sdn-4**: USHMM page + 1998 Milton Burr report for US Committee for Refugees ("Quantifying Genocide in Southern Sudan") confirmed: ~100,000 of ~1M Nuba dead 1992–93 (toll.high). African Rights / Alex de Waal, *Facing Genocide: The Nuba of Sudan* (July 1995) confirmed real: jihad, 1993 fatwa, "peace camps," 60,000–70,000 estimate (toll.low).
- **sdn-5**: USHMM Holocaust Encyclopedia confirmed: "at least 200,000" dead, 2M displaced, Powell 9 Sept 2004 genocide finding (toll.low). GAO-07-24 confirmed: State Dept 98,000–181,000, other studies up to ~400,000, methodological divergence flagged (toll.high). ICC confirmed precisely: 4 Mar 2009 warrant (war crimes, crimes against humanity), 12 Jul 2010 second warrant adding 3 genocide counts vs Fur/Masalit/Zaghawa — first ICC genocide charges, first sitting head of state.

### Falsification HIT — citation corrected
- **sdn-1 toll.low MISATTRIBUTION**: the ~2.5 million post-collapse census figure was cited to the **Anglo-Egyptian conquest of Sudan** article (en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anglo-Egyptian_conquest_of_Sudan), which does **not** contain that census figure (verified via direct fetch). The figure is real but lives on the **Mahdist State** article (Metelits, p.37). The toll.low citation URL and cite text were corrected to point there, with a caveat documenting the fix. The figure itself stands; the citation as originally written did not.
