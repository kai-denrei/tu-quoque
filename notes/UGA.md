# Uganda (UGA) — research log

Built from scratch on 2026-06-18. Status: RESEARCHED.

## Ledger shape

- **Done to them (victim): 1 event** — LRA insurgency (uga-1).
- **In their name (perpetrator): 3 events** — Amin's terror (uga-2), Luwero Triangle / Obote-UNLA (uga-3), UPDF in the DRC (uga-4).

The asymmetry is real and deliberate, not a gap. Uganda's largest mass killings
(Amin's State Research Bureau; Obote's UNLA in Luwero) were Ugandan state forces
killing Ugandan civilians — so the *same* events are perpetrator entries even though
the dead were Uganda's own people. The cleanest non-state "done to them" case is the
LRA, but the LRA was itself a Ugandan Acholi insurgency that mostly killed Acholi.
The only outward-facing perpetrator event is the UPDF in Congo. This is exactly the
dual-lens point and is explained in the country `note`.

## What is solid

- **Amin toll**: International Commission of Jurists — no fewer than 80,000, more likely
  ~300,000; Amnesty/exile estimates up to ~500,000. Perpetrating organs (State Research
  Bureau, Public Safety Unit, Military Police) are well attested (Springer chapter,
  multiple sources). Asian expulsion 1972: ~60,000.
- **Luwero**: UNLA killed an estimated 100,000–200,000 civilians in the Triangle; ICRC/
  Amnesty figure of 300,000+ civilian deaths under Obote across Uganda by July 1985;
  forced removal of ~750,000 mostly Baganda. Memorial skull displays are documented.
- **LRA**: ~100,000 civilians killed (UN-echoed) by 2004 at the high end; U.S. govt low
  estimate ~12,000 directly killed; 20,000–60,000+ children abducted; up to ~1.8–2.5M
  displaced. Atiak massacre (20 Apr 1995): 200–300 civilians shot.
- **UPDF in DRC**: ICJ 2005 judgment is a binding primary source — found killings, torture,
  incitement of Hema–Lendu conflict, child-soldier training, plunder of gold/diamonds in
  occupied Ituri. Kisangani Six-Day War (June 2000): 760+ civilians killed, ~1,700 wounded,
  4,000+ houses damaged, 69 schools shelled (ICRC casebook). $325M reparations ordered
  (2005 liability; 2022 reparations judgment), incl. $225M for loss of life.

## What is contested / could NOT be verified

- **Amin and Obote tolls are genuinely uncertain.** Killings were undocumented; every figure
  is extrapolation. Amin: ICJ/Amnesty band (80,000–500,000) vs. a low scholarly outlier
  (Jorgensen, 12,000–30,000). I used the institutional band for low/high and noted the
  outlier in the toll note rather than adopting it.
- **Luwero figures overlap with general Bush War mortality** (~100,000–500,000 incl.
  combatants). I scoped the range to civilian deaths in the counter-insurgency and flagged
  the overlap.
- **LRA death count spans an order of magnitude** (12,000 vs 100,000) because much of the
  harm was abduction and camp deaths, not battlefield killing. Both ends sourced.
- **DRC/Ituri**: the ICJ did not fix a total death figure for the broader Ituri abuses, so
  uga-4 high is null; only the Kisangani 760+ is a hard sourced number (toll.low).

### Source existence — all confirmed real

- **uga-1 LRA.** Wikipedia "Lord's Resistance Army insurgency" exists and carries the cited
  figures verbatim: "killed an estimated 100,000 civilians" and "displacement of 1.5 million"
  by 2004, U.S. government "up to 12,000 people have been killed," and the triangulated
  "24,000 to 38,000 children" abduction estimate. HHI "Examining the Regional Impact of the
  Lord's Resistance Army" exists at the cited URL and carries the 60,000–100,000 children
  and up-to-2.5M displaced figures. Wikipedia "Atiak massacre" exists and confirms 200–300
  killed by the LRA on 20 April 1995 (corroborated by Nile Post, Daily Monitor, Independent).
- **uga-2 Amin.** Wikipedia "Idi Amin" carries ICJ no-fewer-than-80,000 / ~300,000,
  Amnesty/exile ~500,000, and the 1972 expulsion of ~60,000 Asians. The Springer chapter
  is real: "State Terrorism and Death Squads in Uganda (1971–79)" by **Edward Kannyo**, in
  *Death Squads in Global Perspective: Murder with Deniability* (Palgrave Macmillan), and it
  does attribute the killings to the State Research Bureau, Public Safety Unit and Military
  Police. The Jorgensen low outlier is real — **Jan J. Jorgensen**, 12,000–30,000 — carried
  in the Wikipedia "Idi Amin" article and the cited Daily Monitor retrospective (the Monitor
  URL 403s to automated fetch but is confirmed live and correctly described via search +
  Wikipedia's own footnoting).
- **uga-3 Luwero.** Wikipedia "Ugandan Bush War" carries the ICRC 300,000+ civilian-deaths
  (July 1985), the 750,000 forced removal, and the 100,000–500,000 overall band. Wikipedia
  "Luwero Triangle" exists and confirms the Buganda/Baganda victim community and the skull
  memorialization (incl. the "war skulls stolen" episode).
- **uga-4 UPDF/DRC.** ICJ Summary of Judgment of 19 Dec 2005 (node 103924) is live and its
  Operative Paragraph 3 matches the summary almost word-for-word (killing, torture, inhumane
  treatment, destruction of villages, failure to distinguish targets, child-soldier training,
  incitement of ethnic conflict, plunder of resources, occupying-power status in Ituri). The
  ICRC casebook page is live and gives the Kisangani figures exactly: "Over 760 civilians
  were killed, and an estimated 1,700 wounded… More than 4,000 houses… Sixty-nine schools
  were shelled." The EastAfrican article exists; its $325M / $225M-loss-of-life figures are
  corroborated by the 2022 ICJ reparations judgment (nodes 106106/106108) and JusticeInfo.

### Correction made (uga-3 toll.note)

The low end's attribution was **wrong and is now fixed.** The note claimed "local officials
estimated the UNLA killed 100,000–200,000 civilians in the Luwero Triangle itself." The
100,000–200,000 figure is real but originates with the **U.S. State Department — the
Assistant Secretary of State for Human Rights, ~1984, informed by mass-grave discoveries
reported to Amnesty International** (1981–1984 killings or deliberate starvation), as carried
by the cited Wikipedia "Ugandan Bush War" / "Luwero Triangle" articles and HRW reporting.
Not a fabricated source — a misattributed but verifiable figure. The note now states the
correct provenance. No status downgrade warranted: the figure is real and sourced.
