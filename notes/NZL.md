Built from scratch on 2026-06-19. Web tools (WebSearch/WebFetch) available. Note: NZ
government sites (teara.govt.nz, nzhistory.govt.nz) return HTTP 403 to WebFetch, so their

## Framing decision (the core call)

The single largest mass death in NZ history is the **post-contact Māori population
collapse** (~100,000 in 1769 → ~42,000 by 1896). Because the dominant cause was
**introduced disease** (no single human author), it sits in the VICTIM ledger, tiered
**STR**, with toll framed as NET POPULATION DECLINE, not a counted death toll. This mirrors
the Australia entry's logic but reverses the placement: Australia put its indigenous
catastrophe in the *perpetrator* ledger because the principal agent there was the
British/colonial state (frontier killing + dispossession); for NZ the disease-driven
component is large enough and author-less enough that it reads as victimisation, with the
war/dispossession components broken out and placed separately in the perpetrator ledger.

Perpetrator ledger holds three events with plain human agency:
1. **Moriori genocide (1835–1863)** — committed by mainland Māori iwi, so "NZ's people as
   perpetrator" *before* the colonial state. EXT.
2. **New Zealand Wars + raupatu (1845–1872)** — the colonial Crown's conquest and land
   confiscation. POL.
3. **1918 Samoa influenza catastrophe** — NZ administration's negligence killed ~22% of
   Western Samoa. STR.

The intra-Māori **Musket Wars** (c.1807–1845, ~20,000–40,000 dead) are deliberately NOT
given their own ledger line: Māori-vs-Māori, neither external victimisation nor an act of
the NZ state. Noted inside the disease-collapse event (nzl-1) for completeness.

## What is well-established

- **Māori population:** ~100,000 (1769, Cook; King suggests ~110,000) → 70,000–90,000
  (1840) → recorded low **42,113 in the 1896 census**. Te Ara + Wikipedia agree.
- **Disease as dominant driver:** Te Ara — deaths from all causes 1810–40 ~120,000
  (~4,000/yr), of which ~700/yr from warfare. Wikipedia: introduced disease killed an
  estimated 10–50% of Māori.
- **Moriori:** population ~1,650–1,700 in 1835; ~300 killed outright; **1,561 dead by
  1863**; ~101 left by 1862, ~100 by 1870. Perpetrators **Ngāti Mutunga and Ngāti Tama**
  (from Taranaki, displaced by the Musket Wars). Moriori bound by pacifist Nunuku's Law.
  Wikipedia + Te Ara consistent.
- **Samoa 1918:** SS Talune arrives Apia **7 Nov 1918**, no quarantine despite Fiji having
  quarantined it; **~8,500 dead ≈ 22%** of population; Administrator **Lt-Col Robert Logan**
  refused American Samoa's offer of aid; American Samoa (strict quarantine, 50km away) had
  zero flu deaths; 1919 Royal Commission found neglect; NZ apologised 2002. NZHistory + RNZ
  consistent.

## What is contested / uncertain

- **New Zealand Wars casualties:** Te Ara states plainly there are "no accurate casualty
  figures." Cowan: >500 British/colonial + ~250 kūpapa + ~2,000 Māori dead. Wikipedia
  tabulation: ~745 Crown-side + ~2,154 Māori, total ~2,900–3,000. Toll range set 2,000
  (Māori dead) → 3,000 (all sides). Marked disputed.
- **Raupatu acreage:** Te Ara snippet says ~1 million hectares (incl. later returned);
  Wikipedia says ~1.5 million hectares (~15% of Māori land), with ~12,000 km² in 1865 alone.
  Used ~1.5 million as the headline with the caveat.
- **Māori 1769 baseline** is itself an estimate (100,000 vs King's 110,000), which feeds the
  net-decline range (50,000–70,000) — explicitly NOT presented as a death count.
- **Musket Wars toll** (20,000–40,000) — wide because no records; only referenced, not a
  standalone event.
- **Black Saturday (28/29 Dec 1929, ~11 Mau protesters killed by NZ police incl. paramount
  chief Tupua Tamasese Lealofi III)** was researched and verified but NOT given its own
  event — it is smaller in scale than the 1918 Samoa catastrophe and belongs to the same
  NZ-colonial-rule-of-Samoa thread; could be added as a fifth event if the atlas wants the
  Samoa relationship represented by a deliberate-killing as well as a negligence event.

## What could NOT be verified / left out

- No fabricated figures. Disease mortality percentage (10–50% per Wikipedia) is genuinely
  wide and was not pinned to a single number — handled via the net-decline framing.
- Did not assign a hard "deaths" number to the population-collapse event (births offset
  deaths over 127 yrs); used net decline with an explicit UNIT CAVEAT.

every citation FAKE until proven otherwise. Result: **all 8 sources across 4 events

### Source falsification attempts (all survived)

- **nzl-1 (Māori collapse, STR).** Te Ara "Taupori Māori – Māori population change"
  page-1 — real; carries ~100,000 (1769) → 70,000–90,000 (1840) → ~42,000 (1896) and
  disease as key driver (note: page-1 is titled "Population changes, 1769–1840", which is
  the correct anchor for these figures). Te Ara "Te hauora Māori i mua" page-2 (toll.low)
  — real; carries the exact ~120,000 deaths 1810–40 / ~4,000 a year / ~700 a year from
  warfare, disease dominant. Wikipedia "Māori people" (toll.high) — real; 42,113 in 1896
  census, ~100,000 (Cook) / ~110,000 (King), disease killing est. 10–50%. All verbatim.
- **nzl-2 (Moriori genocide, EXT).** Wikipedia "Moriori genocide" (toll.high) — real;
  every figure matched verbatim (Ngāti Tama + Ngāti Mutunga, 1835–1863, ~1,650 at
  invasion, ~300 killed, 1,561 dead by 1863, 101 in 1862, ~100 by 1870, Nunuku's Law;
  uses "genocide", "enslavement", "cannibalisation"). Te Ara "Moriori" (summary) — real;
  Rodney seizure, ~470 + ~430 invaders, Moriori outnumbered them ~2:1 but obeyed Nunuku's
  Law, slavery to 1863.
- **nzl-3 (NZ Wars + raupatu, POL).** Wikipedia "New Zealand Wars" (toll.high) — real;
  Crown ~745 dead, Māori ~2,154 dead, 1863 Waikato ~14,000 vs ~4,000, ~1.5M ha confiscated
  (~15% of Māori land); figures flagged uncertain by the article itself. Te Ara "New
  Zealand wars" (toll.low) — real; "no accurate casualty figures", Cowan >500 + ~250 kūpapa
  + ~2,000 Māori (with Te Ara's own caveat that Cowan overstated Māori dead), raupatu ~1M
  ha incl. later-returned land. The land-figure split (Te Ara ~1M vs Wikipedia ~1.5M) is
  honestly disclosed in the toll note — not a fabrication.
- **nzl-4 (Samoa 1918 flu, STR).** NZHistory "Influenza in Samoa" (toll.high) — real;
  Talune 7 Nov 1918, no quarantine despite Fiji, ~8,500 = 22%, Logan blamed for quarantine
  failure + rejecting American Samoa aid, Royal Commission found neglect. RNZ "How NZ took
  influenza to Samoa" (summary) — real; ~8,500 = a fifth, Logan rejected medics, zero
  deaths in American Samoa. MINOR (not a falsification): JSON cites the legacy NZHistory
  path `/culture/1918-influenza-pandemic/samoa`; canonical current path is
  `/page/influenza-samoa` — same official content. Left as-is.

### Tier re-derivation (independent, before reading assigned)

- nzl-1 → **STR.** Disease-driven demographic collapse, no single human author. Correctly
  NOT EXT — applies the disease-driven=STR rule. War/dispossession kept separable, not
  merged into a kill count. MATCH.
- nzl-2 → **EXT.** Deliberate destruction of a distinct people (kill / enslave / forbid
  reproduction); the organising act is deliberate, not disease. disease-driven=STR rule
  does NOT apply here. MATCH.
- nzl-3 → **POL.** State war of conquest + statutory mass confiscation; organising act is
  state policy/force, killing in WAR territory and noted as such. MATCH.
- nzl-4 → **STR.** The key STR-vs-EXT trap: deaths from an influenza pandemic via
  governing-power negligence, NOT deliberate killing. Correctly STR, not EXT/POL. MATCH.
