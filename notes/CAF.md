Built from scratch 2026-06-19. Five events: 3 victim, 2 perpetrator. Wrapper status UNVERIFIED (two colonial-era tolls genuinely unknowable).

## Ledger structure

**Done to them (victim):**

## What is solid

- **2013-2014 civil war toll (3,000-6,000):** firmly attributed to the 2014 UN Commission of Inquiry, relayed by USHMM and corroborated by Amnesty. Explicitly a conservative floor — many remote deaths uncounted. ~1,000 dead in Bangui alone in Dec 2013 (Amnesty). Displacement ~554,800 IDP + ~360,000 refugees by May 2014, mostly Muslim.
- **Anti-balaka ethnic cleansing:** the "ethnic cleansing of the country's Muslim community" characterization is the UN Commission of Inquiry's own finding (via USHMM), not the atlas's editorializing. Amnesty documented the massacres, mosque-burning, convoy attacks, and the stated aim of ridding the country of Muslims. This is why caf-5 is tiered EXT while the broader war (caf-3) is WAR.
- **Ngaragba 1979 (~100 children):** multiple independent accounts converge on ~100 students/children killed in April 1979, with Bokassa's personal participation reported (beating boys with his cane, ordering guards to continue). Confirmed via Wikipedia (citing NYT/Guardian/WaPo) and corroborated by Britannica's Bokassa entry, which ties the child-killings to France's decision to depose him in Sept 1979. Amnesty International's contemporaneous account (cited in secondary sources) gave victim ages ~8-16 and a near-100 figure.

## What is contested / could NOT be verified

- **Concession-era death toll (caf-1):** the comprehensive number of dead in Ubangi-Shari under the French concession system is, per the scholarship summarized by Wikipedia, UNKNOWN and was never counted. Only a documented floor exists — 1,500 murders on the Mpoko concession (near Bangui) 1903-1905, recorded by the administration (Wikipedia 'French Equatorial Africa'). Adam Hochschild's estimate that the population of French Congo + Gabon fell by ~half is for the WIDER region and mixes killing with disease (sleeping sickness), so it is NOT used as a CAR death count — only mentioned in the toll note. High left null. Tier STR (structural/demographic collapse), per the atlas rule that disease-driven decline = STR; the deliberate killings (mutilations, executions per Gide) are noted within it.
- **Kongo-Wara toll (caf-2):** the "10,000+" figure appears in the Wikipedia infobox WITHOUT a detailed in-text citation. The rebellion is described as deliberately concealed by France and one of the least-documented interwar uprisings, so the number must be treated as a weak estimate, not an established count. A scholarly monograph exists — Raphaël Nzabakomada-Yakoma, *La guerre de Kongo-Wara 1928-1931* (1986) — but I could not confirm its specific casualty figure in this session (WorldCat record found, full text not accessible via web tools). Low left null; high carried as weakly attributed 10,000. Status UNVERIFIED.
- **Anti-balaka death count (caf-5):** no clean death count attributable specifically to the anti-balaka anti-Muslim campaign exists separate from the war's overall 3,000-6,000 toll; Amnesty documented "many hundreds" killed in massacres but the deaths are folded into the wider figure. Both toll ends left null; displacement (tens of thousands; ~50,000 flown out of Bangui airport per Amnesty) is the firmly documented metric.

## Asymmetry note

The two deepest victim wounds (concession terror, Kongo-Wara) were inflicted by France, an external colonial power; both have unknowable tolls. The perpetrator ledger turns inward — Bokassa's state against its own children, and a Central African militia (anti-balaka) against the Muslim minority. The 2013-2014 war deliberately appears on BOTH ledgers (caf-3 victim / caf-5 perpetrator) — Central Africans of all faiths as victims, Central African militias as perpetrators — which is the atlas's dual lens collapsed into a single conflict. Nothing was invented to balance the columns; the colonial tolls are shown as floors or null and the asymmetry is explained in the country note.

## Audit

Adversarial source-integrity + tier audit, 2026-06-19. Every citation treated as fake until proven. All five events' sources were independently re-fetched/searched. Net result: 3 VERIFIED, 2 UNVERIFIED; country wrapper UNVERIFIED. All five tiers independently re-derived and confirmed correct — no tier changes.

**Verified real and supporting their claims:**
- Wikipedia "Ubangi-Shari" (caf-1 summary) — concession system "similar to the Congo Free State," Gide's account of mutilations/dismemberments/executions/burning of children, and the explicit "number of victims… remains unknown." Confirmed verbatim.
- USHMM "Political and Ethnic Violence in CAR" (caf-3 toll.high, caf-5 tier) — UN CoI 3,000-6,000 (Jan 2013-Dec 2014), Séléka/anti-balaka attribution, the "ethnic cleansing of the country's Muslim community" finding, and May 2014 displacement (554,800 IDP; 359,834 refugees, mostly Muslim). Fully confirmed.
- Amnesty Apr 2014 Q&A (caf-3 summary) — confirmed via search (direct fetch 403). ~1,000 killed in Bangui over two days in Dec 2013 (Amnesty frames this as ex-Séléka retaliation against Christians; anti-balaka killed ~60 Muslims in the trigger attack). Exodus and atrocity claims supported.
- Amnesty Feb 2014 "Ethnic cleansing and sectarian killings" (caf-5 summary) — confirmed via search. Anti-balaka massacres of Muslims ("many hundreds"), mosque/home burning, convoy attacks, stated aim to rid the country of Muslims, tens-of-thousands exodus = ethnic cleansing. Supported.
- Wikipedia "1979 Ngaragba Prison massacre" (caf-4 toll.high) — ~100 / "at least 100 students beaten to death," Bokassa's personal participation (ivory-encrusted ebony cane, "carry on" order), uniform-protest trigger, NYT/Guardian/WaPo citations. Fully confirmed.
- Wikipedia "Kongo-Wara rebellion" (caf-2 toll.high) — exists and is accurate on participants (350,000+ adherents, ~60,000 warriors), Karnou's death (11 Dec 1928), and the 1931 "war of the caves."

**Falsification hits (corrections applied to JSON):**
1. **caf-1 toll.low MISATTRIBUTED.** The 1,500-murders-on-Mpoko figure (with "40 European managers + 400 armed guards shot on sight any African not collecting rubber") is genuine but does NOT appear on the cited Wikipedia "French Equatorial Africa" page (re-fetched twice — that page carries only Gide + the Hochschild half-decline estimate). The figure lives verbatim in the scholarly reference "France in Tropical Africa" (encyclopedia.com) and in Wikipedia "Ubangi-Shari." Source re-pointed to encyclopedia.com; cite, url, caveat, and toll.note corrected. Claim is true; attribution was false. Status stays UNVERIFIED (region-wide total unknowable, single floor).
2. **caf-2 "deliberate concealment" OVERSTATED.** The summary, toll.note, and both source caveats asserted France "deliberately concealed"/"carefully hidden" the rebellion's scale. The sources (Wikipedia + Britannica) say only that it was "relegated to relative obscurity amongst the public in France" / limited media coverage — obscurity, not documented state concealment. Wording softened in all four places. Separately confirmed the "10,000+" toll appears ONLY in the Wikipedia infobox with no inline citation — correctly carried as a weak estimate. Status stays UNVERIFIED.
3. **caf-2 Britannica cite tightened.** Direct fetch 403; confirmed via search. Britannica corroborates the rebellion, leader executions, and forced relocations but does NOT say it was hidden — cite/caveat corrected to match.
4. **caf-4 Britannica caveat INACCURATE.** Original caveat claimed Britannica "does not supply an independent death toll." It does: "more than 50 schoolchildren," LOWER than the ~100 carried. Caveat corrected; the 50+/~100 spread now expressly justifies the disputed flag. Britannica also attributes the killings to the "imperial guard" and does not confirm in searchable text the uniform trigger or Bokassa's hands-on role (those rest on the dedicated WP article + press). Event still VERIFIED — the ~100 high is well-corroborated independently.
