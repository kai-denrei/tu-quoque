# Kyrgyzstan (KGZ) — research log

Built from scratch (no seed). Web research conducted 2026-06-19 via WebSearch/WebFetch.

## Ledger structure
- **Victim (2):** kgz-1 Great Urkun (1916); kgz-2 Stalin's Great Terror in the Kyrgyz SSR / Ata-Beyit (1937-38).
- **Perpetrator (1):** kgz-3 June 2010 Osh ethnic violence (Kyrgyz mobs + complicit security forces vs Uzbeks).

The record is asymmetric — Kyrgyzstan is overwhelmingly a victim nation (Tsarist then Soviet subjugation). The perpetrator side is real but thin; explained in the country note.

## What I found
### kgz-1 — Great Urkun, 1916
- Trigger: Tsar Nicholas II's June 1916 conscription order for Central Asian Muslims → revolt → Tsarist + settler reprisal massacres and a deadly flight over the Tien Shan to China.
- Kyrgyz death range: low ~40,000 (Shayyrkul Batyrbaeva, population tally); high ~270,000 (Edward Dennis Sokol, region-wide; widely applied to Kyrgyz). Toynbee floated ~500,000 (self-described speculative). "Perhaps ~40% of the Kyrgyz population" died — population-decline framing, not a clean death count.
- Perpetrator dimension within the same event: rebels killed ~2,325 Russians (+1,384 missing) before the larger reprisal. Folded into kgz-1's relevance line rather than made a separate perpetrator event, since the reprisal/flight deaths dwarf it.
- Memory: now strongly commemorated in Kyrgyzstan (received_lens: memorial); suppressed under the USSR; Russia has framed centenary commemoration as a "Western plot."

### kgz-2 — Great Terror / Ata-Beyit, 1937-38
- 137 condemned (mostly Kyrgyz political/intellectual elite, "19 nationalities") shot Nov 5 & 8, 1938, secretly buried at NKVD dacha near Chong-Tash; grave revealed 1991 by a former guard's daughter; reburied at Ata-Beyit memorial (built 2000, Akayev initiative).
- Broader Kyrgyz SSR repression: 24,000+ repressed by mid-1938 (Book of Victims of Political Repressions 1920-1953); 591 documented dead in prison; executions in the hundreds. Archives acknowledged incomplete.
- received_lens: memorial; live contest with modern Russia's attempts to justify the Terror.

### kgz-3 — June 2010 Osh ethnic violence
- Kyrgyz mobs from surrounding villages attacked Uzbek neighborhoods in Osh and Jalal-Abad starting June 10, 2010; >2,600 mostly Uzbek-owned homes burned.
- Toll: official Kyrgyz National Commission (Jan 2011) = 426 (276 Uzbek, 105 Kyrgyz). HRW: Ministry of Health 371, "likely higher," one official suggested ~900. Local Uzbek sources: 1,526-2,000+. Majority of dead were Uzbek.
- State complicity: HRW + KIC documented camouflaged armored vehicles clearing Uzbek barricades ahead of attackers; security forces disarmed Uzbeks selectively; KIC concluded some attacks "would amount to crimes against humanity." Post-violence prosecutions disproportionately targeted Uzbeks (→ received_lens: denied).

## Contested / uncertain
- Urkun toll is genuinely disputed (40k vs 270k+); ranges are population-decline estimates blending massacre, starvation, exposure, disease. Sokol's high figure is region-wide, not strictly Kyrgyz.
- 2010 Osh toll: official ~426 vs unofficial 2,000+; ethnic breakdown not officially released per HRW, though Uzbek-majority victimhood is consistent across sources.
- Agent for Osh is plural (mobs + complicit state), not a single state-directed campaign — reflected in the "agent" field.

## NOT verified / excluded
- **1930s collectivization famine in Kyrgyzstan:** real, but Kyrgyz mortality (~4%) was far below the Kazakh catastrophe (40-60%), and I found NO firm sourceable Kyrgyz-specific absolute death count. Left OUT as an event (no invented toll) and flagged in the country note instead. The detailed source (Springer chapter "Famine in Kyrgyzstan in the 1930s and 1940s") and Pianciola's "Stalinist spatial hierarchies" were paywalled (403/redirect); only the 4% comparative rate could be confirmed via secondary search.
- 1990 Osh clashes (Kyrgyz-Uzbek, ~300+ dead) noted in passing during research but not added; 2010 is the larger, better-documented perpetrator case.

## Audit
Adversarial source-integrity + tier audit, 2026-06-19 (assume-fake posture). Country status set to **UNVERIFIED** (1 of 3 events failed verification).

### Method
Every source treated as fabricated until proven. Each citation checked for: (a) the work/author actually exists, (b) it supports the *specific* claim it is attached to (toll.low / toll.high / tier / summary), (c) bias disclosed in caveat where needed. Tiers re-derived from facts before reading the assigned tier.

### Per-event verdicts
**kgz-1 Great Urkun (1916) — VERIFIED.**
- Wikipedia "Central Asian revolt of 1916" (toll.low): confirms Batyrbaeva ~40,000 (population tally), Sokol ~270,000 (region-wide), Toynbee ~500,000 (self-described speculative), 2,325 Russians killed + 1,384 missing, ~40% Kyrgyz population loss. Both toll ends sourced.
- Sokol/Toynbee scholarly cite (toll.high): confirmed; caveat correctly flags Sokol's figure as region-wide not Kyrgyz-specific.
- RFE/RL "Remembering The Great Urkun 100 Years Later" (Bruce Pannier, Apr 2016) and The Diplomat "Memorializing (and Avoiding) the Great Urkun" (Sept 2016): both 403 on direct fetch (anti-bot), CONFIRMED REAL via search with matching titles/content. Not fabricated.
- Tier POL re-derived independently: deliberate massacre + state-authorized driven exodus/expulsion of an ethnic group. Correct. (Most deaths from exposure/starvation in flight, but the *driver* is expulsion, not disease — POL, not STR/EXT.)

**kgz-2 Stalin's Great Terror / Ata-Beyit (1937-38) — UNVERIFIED (toll.high quarantine).**
- 137 figure (toll.low): CONFIRMED by Wikipedia and openDemocracy "Uncovering Stalin's terror in Kyrgyzstan" (real, reachable). Chong-Tash grave, 1991 discovery via KGB caretaker→daughter (Bubyira Kydyraliyeva), NKVD/Great Terror context all confirmed.
- 591 prison-death figure (in toll.note): CONFIRMED verbatim in the cited NomadIT paper (Abdalieva, "The plight of political prisoners in prisons of the USSR (1920-1953)").
- **FAILURE — toll.high 24,000:** could NOT be independently verified. Searches surfaced "several tens of thousands repressed" and "~10,000 elites executed" but NO source confirming the specific 24,000 number. It traces to the Book of Victims of Political Repressions (primary work, not directly accessible in audit). High end is directionally supported (tens of thousands repressed, republic-wide, not pure deaths) but the exact figure is single-sourced and unconfirmed → quarantined in toll.note.
- **Cite correction:** the NomadIT URL (conference/102/p/12049) resolves to a conference PANEL (HIS11), not a single paper. Cite was mislabeling the panel name as a paper title; corrected to name both the panel and the actual paper (Abdalieva), and caveat now states the panel abstract supports only "several tens of thousands," not 24,000.
- Tier POL re-derived: targeted political execution of national intelligentsia by NKVD on fabricated charges. Correct.

**kgz-3 June 2010 Osh ethnic violence — VERIFIED.**
- HRW "Where is the Justice?" (2010, summary): fully confirmed — June 10-14 violence, Kyrgyz attacks on Uzbek neighborhoods, camouflaged armored vehicles clearing barricades ahead of mobs, security forces disarming Uzbeks selectively, 2,600 homes destroyed (mostly Uzbek-owned), Ministry of Health 371 deaths "likely higher"/one official ~900.
- Wikipedia "2010 South Kyrgyzstan ethnic clashes" (toll.low): confirms National Commission Jan 2011 = 426 (276 Uzbek, 105 Kyrgyz) AND local Uzbek sources 1,526-2,000+. Both toll ends sourced.
- KIC commission (tier): CONFIRMED real (Independent International Commission of Inquiry, chaired via Kimmo Kiljunen/OSCE PA, report published 3 May 2011). Concluded attacks on Uzbek neighborhoods in Osh 11-13 June would amount to crimes against humanity (murder, rape, sexual violence, persecution) — not war crimes or genocide. Caveat tightened with publication date and scope.
- Tier POL re-derived: organized ethnic-targeted crimes against humanity per KIC. Correct.
