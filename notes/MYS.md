# Malaysia (MYS) — research log

Built from scratch 2026-06-18. Web tools (WebSearch/WebFetch) loaded and used for all events. Five events: three victim, two perpetrator.

## Victim ledger ("Done to them")

### mys-1 — Sook Ching massacre in Malaya (1942) — EXT
- Japanese 25th Army / Kempeitai systematically killed ethnic Chinese branded "anti-Japanese," screenings ordered under Yamashita, directed in the field by Tsuji.
- **Tolls are heavily contested.** This is the central difficulty:
  - Malayan-peninsula-only: reference summaries cite ~20,000 civilians; up to ~25,000 sometimes attributed to Johor alone.
  - Singapore + Malaya combined: Japanese postwar acknowledgement ~5,000; scholarly band 25,000–55,000; Chinese/Singaporean claims 50,000–100,000; a Chinese-source figure of ~97,000 "imprisoned or killed" across both territories.
  - I set low=20,000 / high=50,000 to bracket the commonly cited Malayan range, and explicitly flagged that the larger combined figures mix in Singapore and (for the 97,000) include imprisonment. Did NOT use the combined figures as the Malayan toll, to avoid double-counting with a future SGP entry. Status UNVERIFIED because of the wide unresolved spread.

### mys-2 — Burma–Siam Railway forced labour (1942–45) — WAR
- Japanese occupation conscripted Malayans as romusha; ~73,000 sent to the Thai–Burma "Death Railway," ~25,000 died.
- Other figures (80,000–100,000 region-wide labourer deaths; ~150,000 Tamil deaths) blur Malayan, Tamil, Burmese and other Asian labourers together and overlap the Indian-labourer toll, so I used only the Malayan-specific ~25,000 and set low=high=25,000 rather than fabricate a spread. Status UNVERIFIED (single estimate, no firm Malayan-specific range).

### mys-3 — Batang Kali massacre (1948) — WAR
- British Scots Guards (2nd Bn) shot 24 unarmed men at Sungai Rimoh near Batang Kali, Selangor, 12 Dec 1948, during the Malayan Emergency. One survivor (Chong Hong) feigned death.
- Toll of 24 is NOT disputed; what is disputed is "shot while escaping" (original account) vs deliberate massacre (survivors' account). UK courts (2010s) acknowledged unlawful killing / possible war crime but refused an inquiry; 2024 UK expression of regret.
- Perpetrator here is the British colonial state, so this sits on Malaysia's VICTIM ledger. Status RESEARCHED (firm toll, well-documented).

## Perpetrator ledger ("In their name")

### mys-4 — 13 May 1969 racial violence — POL
- The hardest call. Official NOC framing: spontaneous inter-communal rioting after the 1969 election; 196 dead (143 Chinese, 25 Malay, 13 Indian, 15 others).
- What makes it a defensible PERPETRATOR (state) event: documented shoot-to-kill order (~8:30 pm); hospital records show casualties shifted from Chinese parang wounds (pre-order) to Chinese gunshot wounds (post-order); foreign correspondents reported soldiers firing into Chinese shophouses; declassified UK files note "individual instances of partiality by Malay military and police personnel against the Chinese."
- **Contested interpretation:** Kua Kia Soong (May 13, 2007, based on real UK Public Record Office declassified files) argues UMNO's "state-capitalist class," backed by police/army, instigated the violence as a coup against Tunku Abdul Rahman. Kua is opposition-aligned — flagged as partisan; the orchestration thesis is contested, not consensus. I framed the event honestly as inter-communal violence WITH a documented security-force role, not as a clean state massacre.
- Toll: low=196 (official) / high=800 (observer John Slimming, first week; Western diplomats ~600). Four-figure claims exist but are less firmly sourced. Bodies reportedly dumped in Klang River / tin mines without inquests, explaining the gap. Status RESEARCHED with disputed=true.

### mys-5 — Memali Incident (1985) — STR
- Cleanest perpetrator entry. ~200 Royal Malaysia Police, ordered by DPM/Home Minister Musa Hitam under the Mahathir government, sieged Kampung Memali (Baling, Kedah), 19 Nov 1985, to arrest Ibrahim Mahmud ("Ibrahim Libya") under the ISA. 14 villagers + 4 police killed.
- Toll of 18 not disputed. Contested: proportionality/conduct, "criminals/deviationists" (white paper) vs "martyrs" (PAS), and the long-suppressed fact (revealed by Musa in 2014) that Mahathir was in the country, not abroad. RCI repeatedly refused. Status RESEARCHED.

## Asymmetry (noted in country `note`)
The worst mass death on Malaysian soil was inflicted by OUTSIDERS — Imperial Japan (Sook Ching, romusha) and the British (Batang Kali). The independent Malaysian state's own perpetrator record is real but an order of magnitude smaller (dozens to a few hundred, and in 1969 partly contested). I did NOT invent perpetrator events to balance the ledgers; the asymmetry is stated plainly.

## Could NOT verify / open questions
- A precise Malayan-only Sook Ching death count — genuinely unknowable; bracketed range only.
- A firm Malayan-specific RANGE for railway deaths — only a single ~25,000 estimate found; low=high.
- The 1969 orchestration thesis (Kua) — based on real declassified files but interpretively contested; not asserted as fact.
- A total occupation-wide Malayan death figure (~100,000 is cited loosely but consolidates massacres + labour + famine and is not cleanly separable) — not used as a standalone toll.

## Considered and rejected
- Sandakan Death Marches / Borneo POW deaths — victims were overwhelmingly Australian/British POWs, not Malaysians; better placed on AUS/GBR/JPN ledgers.
- Malayan Emergency Communist insurgency generally — the lethal counter-insurgency (Briggs Plan, resettlement) was a British colonial operation; Batang Kali captures the sharpest atrocity. Post-independence Malaysia's own conduct against the MNLA did not surface a single well-documented mass-atrocity event of comparable standing.
