# Luxembourg (LUX) — research log

Built from scratch on 2026-06-19. Status: RESEARCHED. 4 events (2 victim, 2 perpetrator).

## Summary of the ledger

Luxembourg's dual ledger is genuinely asymmetric. Victim side is heavy and very
well documented (Nazi annexation + Holocaust). Perpetrator side is real but
consists of COMPLICITY, not direct mass killing — so both perpetrator entries
carry null or non-isolable tolls. The asymmetry is explained in the country note;
no event, toll, or source was invented to balance the books.

## Victim ledger

- **lux-1 — The Holocaust in Luxembourg (EXT).** Pre-war Jewish pop ~3,500
  (1,000+ German refugees). ~2,500 fled to unoccupied France before deportations.
  ~800 interned at Fuenfbrunnen transit camp; 674 deported in 8 transports
  (Oct 1941–Apr 1943) to Lodz/Theresienstadt/Auschwitz; only ~36 deportees
  survived. **Total murdered: 1,000–2,500** (USHMM + Wikipedia both give this
  range — the spread exists because many Luxembourg Jews were caught/killed
  AFTER fleeing abroad, esp. from Vichy France, so total killed > those deported
  directly from Luxembourg). Toll marked disputed:true.

- **lux-2 — Nazi annexation, forced conscription, 1942 general strike (WAR).**
  ~12,000 men conscripted into the Wehrmacht; >3,500 deserted; >1,100 fled
  abroad; **>2,800 killed or missing in action** (the clean quantified figure,
  used as low=high=2800). Aug–Sep 1942 general strike against conscription:
  21 strikers executed (20 by Standgericht at Hinzert + 1 decapitated at
  Cologne), ~2,000 arrested, ~290 schoolchildren/young workers sent to
  re-education camps, several thousand forcibly resettled east. Strike-execution
  and resettlement deaths documented but not isolable as a single total.

## Perpetrator ledger

- **lux-3 — Administrative commission's complicity in the Holocaust (EXT).**
  THE sharpest dual-lens case: Luxembourg's own wartime replacement
  administration helped persecute Jews. Vincent Artuso's 2015 official report
  ("La « question juive » au Luxembourg") found officials identified Jews by
  German criteria, expelled them from professions/schools, organized property
  theft, and ordered that fled Jews could not return — "with diligence, even
  zeal." Led to PM Bettel's 2015 parliamentary apology. **Toll = null** to avoid
  double-counting the lux-1 deaths; this is complicity, the deaths are the same.

- **lux-4 — Financing the Belgian Congo + apartheid South Africa (STR).**
  Luxembourg never held a colony. Under the 1921–22 economic union, Luxembourgers
  got Belgian colonial-administrator rights (~600 installed in Congo by 1957).
  From the 1950s–80s, Lux-domiciled banks (BgL/SgB, Banque Lambert, KBL) financed
  Congolese mining/infrastructure and acted as a discreet hub for apartheid SA
  (e.g. KBL ~$625M to SA state enterprises 1967–75), including sanctions-busting
  structures. **Toll = null** — the Cambridge scholarship explicitly frames this
  as financial facilitation, NOT direct perpetration.

## What is contested

- The Holocaust toll (1,000–2,500) is a genuine scholarly range, not a precision
  gap; flagged disputed:true with a note explaining the cause of the spread.
- Whether the Congo/apartheid financing belongs in a "mass atrocity" atlas at all
  is a judgment call — included as STR (structural/economic complicity) with an
  explicit null toll and a caveat, rather than overstated.

## What could NOT be verified / left null

- No separate, isolable death toll for the administrative commission's
  complicity (lux-3) — deliberately null to avoid double-counting lux-1.
- No death toll attributable to Luxembourg in the colonial/apartheid financing
  role (lux-4) — facilitation, not killing; left null per the cited scholarship.
- Exact resettlement-death and strike-execution-aftermath totals (lux-2) are
  documented qualitatively but not given as a single number; only the conscript
  war-dead figure (~2,800) is carried as the toll.
