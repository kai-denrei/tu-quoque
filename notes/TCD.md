## What I found (well-documented)

- **Habré regime** is the single strongest, best-documented case in the entry, and it is a *perpetrator* case. The 1992 Chadian Truth Commission (Commission of Inquiry, decree 29 Dec 1990, reported 7 May 1992) estimated 40,000+ killed and ~200,000 tortured. Independently, the Human Rights Data Analysis Group (HRDAG) forensically analysed recovered DDS files: 12,321 named victims, 1,208 recorded killed/died in detention, prison mortality 30–87 per 1,000, political-prisoner death rate 90–540x the national rate. Habré convicted of crimes against humanity by the Extraordinary African Chambers in Dakar, 2016 — a landmark universal-jurisdiction verdict. Ethnic targeting documented: southerners ("Black September" 1984), Hadjeraï (1987), Zaghawa (1989).
- **French colonial violence** well-covered by the Sciences Po Mass Violence and Resistance project: Ouaddai war 1909–11 (~1,320 official deaths / 1,500–2,000 per historians), Wadai population collapse 700,000→400,000 (1912–14), 1917 "massacre des coupes-coupes" (~56–150), Congo-Ocean railway levy (~10,000 Chadian/Sara deaths, ~half of Moyen-Chari recruits), 1952 Bébalem shooting (24–70 official, up to 375 per survivors).
- **Libyan invasion/occupation** documented via Wikipedia (Chadian–Libyan War) and GlobalSecurity.org: four campaigns 1978–87, Aouzou Strip annexation, N'Djamena battles (2,000–5,000 killed 1979; 3,000–4,000 in 1980), 1987 Toyota War, ICJ award of Aouzou to Chad in 1994.

## What is contested / could not be cleanly verified

- **Habré toll (1,208 vs 40,000):** an order-of-magnitude+ gap between the forensic documented floor (HRDAG) and the Truth Commission's total-death estimate. Both ends sourced; range flagged disputed. The 40,000 is an estimate, not enumerated name-by-name.
- **Colonial toll:** no unified count. Deaths blend killing, famine, drought, and epidemic disease set off by conquest. The ~10,000 railway figure is the firmest single number (low end). The ~300,000 high end is a *demographic-decline* figure for Wadai, NOT a death count — explicitly caveated. Tier kept POL (lethal exploitation/repression of a colonised population), not STR, because direct killing and forced labour are central, though disease/famine are a major share.
- **Libyan-war toll:** genuinely entangled with Chad's own civil wars (Libya was the principal external belligerent and patron of client factions). No clean Libya-only count exists. Used the documented N'Djamena capital-battle spikes as a bounded, conflict-driven range; aggregate "30,000 in the 1980s" figures are explicitly flagged as blended.

## Deliberately excluded (honesty, not omission)

- **Pre-colonial slave-raiding** by Rabih az-Zubayr (sacked Bagirmi/Bornu/Wadai in the 1890s) and by the Wadai and Bagirmi sultanates themselves — real and large, but too diffuse in agency and documentation to enter as a discrete sourced event with a defensible toll. Noted in the country `note` instead.
- Post-Habré Déby-era abuses and the 2008 N'Djamena battle were not developed into a fourth event; the entry caps at 1–3 per ledger as instructed, choosing the most significant and best-documented.

### tcd-1 French colonial conquest / Congo-Ocean railway — VERIFIED

- **Sciences Po Mass Violence (toll.low + toll.high):** source real and live. Every figure verified verbatim against the page — Ouaddai war "more than 1,320 people killed" across 22 battles ("1,500 or 2,000 dead" per historians); "General Hilaire estimated that the population of Ouaddaï declined from 700,000 inhabitants in 1912 to 400,000 in 1914"; "nearly 10,000 Sara died, which was about half of those recruited from Moyen-Chari"; "between 15,000 and 30,000 Africans died" with "at least 20,000" from Chad; 1917 massacre (~100/150 deaths); 1952 Bébalem (24 to 375); and the load-bearing caveat sentence "the disruption to economic life brought about by the French conquest caused more deaths than the fighting." Exact match — no drift.
- **Wikipedia French Chad (summary):** verified — Kousséri 1900, conquest complete by 1917 / insurgencies crushed by 1920, forced porterage "which claimed thousands of lives," 1929 cotton quotas at artificially low prices, Bébalem massacre 1952. All present.
- **Toll:** both ends sourced (low = railway ~10,000; high = Wadai demographic decline ~300,000, properly caveated as decline-not-deaths). Two distinct supporting source-anchors. OK.
- **Tier:** independently re-derived BEFORE reading assigned. Lethal exploitation/repression of a colonised population (forced labour + conquest killing + induced famine/disease). Not EXT (no defined-group extermination campaign); the disease/famine share is folded into a POL forced-labour event rather than mislabelled. **POL confirmed correct.**

### tcd-2 Gaddafi's Libya invasion/occupation — UNVERIFIED (quarantined)

- **Failure:** the GlobalSecurity cite (was supporting toll.high) attached FOUR figures that are NOT in the source: "3,000–4,000 killed in the 1980 fight for N'Djamena," "~30,000 dead in Chad's conflicts during the 1980s," "~11,000-troop airlift," and "~80 combat aircraft." The page's only "3,000" is Gaddafi's propaganda: "Qadhafi announced that his troops had killed over 3,000 of the 'enemy' while losing 300 themselves" — a claimed enemy-kill count, not a neutral toll. The "~30,000 in the 1980s" could not be located in any source via targeted search either.

- **Corrections applied:** high end 7,500 → **5,000** (the verified ceiling of the one 1979 estimate; the 7,500 was unsourced). Display + note rewritten to drop the 1980 "3,000–4,000" figure and flag the quarantine. GlobalSecurity cite re-scoped from toll.high to **summary** (Aouzou + deployment facts only, which it does support) with an explicit "does NOT support any toll" audit note. Wikipedia cite re-scoped to support **both** toll ends, with a single-source-toll flag added (audit rule 2): after falsification, the entire toll rests on one 1979 estimate, so the contested-toll two-source requirement is not met. `audit_note` field added to the event explaining the UNVERIFIED status.
- **Tier:** cross-border armed invasion/occupation, combat-driven deaths. **WAR confirmed correct.** (Status failure is sourcing, not tier.)
