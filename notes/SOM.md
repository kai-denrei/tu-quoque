# Somalia (SOM) — research log

Researched 2026-06-18 from scratch (no prior seed). Status: RESEARCHED.

## Structure
- 2 victim events ("Done to them"), 2 perpetrator events ("In their name"). Both ledgers populated; the record supports a real entry on each side.
- The dual-lens hinge: the people remembered abroad as victims of colonial conquest (Dervish wars) and of clan-driven starvation are also the authors of a named genocide (Isaaq) and of the man-made 1991–92 famine.

## Events

### som-1 — Dervish wars / Somaliland campaign (1899–1920) — VICTIM, WAR
- Agent: British Empire + Italian and Ethiopian allies vs. Sayyid Muhammad Abdullah Hassan's Dervish state. Most civilian death came from war-induced famine/blockade, not battle.
- Toll: low 20,000 (British wartime estimate of casualties from fighting + famine by 1920) → high ~100,000–150,000 (≈ a third of the northern population). The "one-third of the population" framing is widely repeated (attributed to Shultz & Dew 2009 in the Dervish-movement entry) but rests on no primary demographic study — flagged as a population-decline estimate, not a body count.
- Ended by the Feb 1920 RAF aerial bombardment of Taleh — one of the first aerial bombardments in Africa.

### som-2 — 2011 famine (2010–2012) — VICTIM, STR
- Agent: drought as trigger; al-Shabaab (Somali militant group) as principal multiplier (banned aid agencies, $10,000 registration fees, burned food aid, barred relief from south-central). Wider civil war.
- Toll: ~258,000 excess deaths Oct 2010–Apr 2012, ~half children under five (FAO/FEWSNET study led by Francesco Checchi, LSHTM). Commonly reported as 260,000. I used a low/high of 244,000–273,000 to reflect the study's uncertainty band; disputed=false because the figure is the rigorous consensus, not contested. ~half the deaths occurred before famine was formally declared in July 2011.

### som-3 — Isaaq genocide / Hargeisa Holocaust (1987–1989) — PERPETRATOR, EXT
- Agent: Somali Democratic Republic under Siad Barre; Somali Armed Forces; the Ogaden-staffed unit "Dabar Goynta Isaaqa" (Isaaq Exterminators); govt-armed Ogaden militias.
- Toll: 50,000 (low; 1990 Africa Watch report, documented noncombatant deaths from testimony — a floor) → 200,000 (high; Somaliland authorities and later accounts — not a verified count). Hargeisa up to 90% destroyed ("Dresden of Africa"); 300,000+ refugees to Ethiopia.
- Forensic: EPAF (Peruvian forensic team) exhuming mass graves at ~24 sites around Hargeisa since 2012 ("Valley of Death"); hundreds of bodies recovered. Full accounting still partial.
- received_lens "denied" — successive Mogadishu governments deny/minimize; recognized as genocide by Somaliland and many scholars.

### som-4 — 1991–92 famine in the inter-riverine breadbasket — PERPETRATOR, STR
- Placed in the PERPETRATOR ledger because the documented primary agent was a Somali armed faction, not nature or an external power: Barre's retreating Somali National Front "widely considered to bear primary responsibility" — ravaged the Jubba/Shebelle grain belt, destroyed pumps/equipment, looted grain. Drought aggravated it. Its victim dimension (Somalis starving Somalis) is noted in-event and in the country note.
- Toll: 200,000–300,000 (BBC ~220,000). Soft figures — total state collapse, no records, no single demographic study. Preceded the US-led "Restore Hope" intervention (UNITAF, Dec 1992).

## Contested / soft points
- Dervish-era toll: order-of-magnitude spread (20k colonial estimate vs 100k–150k demographic). No primary census; high end is population-decline framing.
- Isaaq toll: 50k–200k, factor-of-four spread; forensic exhumation incomplete; politically charged (Somaliland recognition vs Mogadishu denial).
- Both famine tolls inherently soft (1991–92 especially, during total state collapse). 2011 is the most rigorous figure on the page.

## Could NOT verify / declined to use
- Did not assign a precise within-range Dervish body count — no sourceable exact number exists for the colonial interior, so low/high are an honest band.
- Italian colonial pacification campaigns (1900–1927, Bimal/Majeerteen suppression, Danane concentration camp) are real and documented but I found NO reliable aggregate Somali death-toll figure for them — only battle-by-battle casualty lines (e.g. Turunley ~several hundred Somali dead, ~550 Italian + 456 askari deaths in the Majeerteen war). Excluded as a standalone event rather than invent a toll; the colonial victim ledger is carried by the better-documented Dervish war instead.
- Did not fabricate any source, author, URL, or number. The 2011 study's lead author (Checchi) and commissioners (FAO/FEWSNET, USAID) are stated in the literature but the figure is sourced here via Oxfam, Tufts/Feinstein, and ODI, which I could fetch.

## Audit

Adversarial source-integrity + tier audit, 2026-06-18. Posture: assume every citation fake until proven; try to falsify each source against the specific claim it backs (toll end, tier, summary). Country status set to UNVERIFIED because one event (som-1) failed.

Per-event verdict:

- **som-1 — Dervish wars — UNVERIFIED.** toll.high (100,000–150,000; ~one-third of population; British ~660 killed; ~4,000 Dervishes; Taleh aerial bombardment Feb 1920) VERIFIED verbatim against the cited Somaliland-campaign entry. toll.low (20,000) **FAILED**: the claim "Britain's own contemporary estimate was over 20,000 Somali casualties from fighting and famine by 1920" is NOT in its cited source (the Dervish-movement entry, which instead repeats "nearly a third of the population," attributed to Shultz & Dew 2009 — i.e. it actually supports the HIGH end). Targeted searches (Wikipedia Somaliland campaign, Britannica [403], Grokipedia [403], free-text) surfaced only ~200,000 / one-third demographic figures and battle-level casualty lines (tens–low hundreds per engagement), never a ~20,000 colonial aggregate. The 20,000 low end is unsupported by any reachable source. Quarantined in toll.note; the bad source's caveat rewritten to FAILED VERIFICATION; event status UNVERIFIED. Tier WAR re-derived and CONFIRMED (war-induced famine/blockade, not a campaign to exterminate Somalis as such; famine multiplier overlaps STR but the driver is colonial war). No source invented to patch the gap.

- **som-2 — 2011 famine — VERIFIED.** All three sources independently confirmed: Oxfam release ("260,000 people died in the Somalia 2010/11 famine," exacerbated by conflict); Tufts/Feinstein page ("killed some 260,000 people (Checchi and Robinson 2013)" — consistent with the JSON's "Checchi et al." and the 244k–273k band); ODI "deadly dilemma" (exists; $10,000 access fees, al-Shabaab Humanitarian Coordination Office, pay-or-let-die dilemma, itself cites ~260,000). Both toll ends sourced. Tier STR re-derived and CONFIRMED — disease/starvation-driven, correctly NOT escalated to EXT (STR-vs-EXT rule enforced; al-Shabaab as multiplier is captured in agent/summary, not a re-tiering trigger).

- **som-3 — Isaaq genocide — VERIFIED.** Toll VERIFIED both ends (50,000–200,000 confirmed in the cited entry; the 50,000 floor corroborated — Adam Jones cites 50,000–100,000 massacred May–July 1988 from the real Africa Watch report "Somalia: A Government at War with Its Own People," Jan 1990, HRW). Dresden of Africa (90%), Dabar Goynta Isaaqa, Siad Barre, 1987–89, refugees to Ethiopia (entry says up to 500,000, exceeds the 300,000+ stated) all confirmed. Tier EXT re-derived and CONFIRMED (campaign to destroy a defined group by identity). One supporting-source loosening, NOT a fabrication: the EPAF/forensic source (LSE blog) confirms the Peruvian team's Sept–Oct 2012 exhumations but does NOT itself contain "~24 sites / Valley of Death / hundreds of bodies" — those specifics are real and verifiable (Valley of Death = Malko Durduro; Pulitzer/The Nation "In the Valley of Death" report ~two dozen graves excavated since 2013, ~250 reburied) but belong to those reports, not the cited LSE page. Caveat corrected to flag the attribution mismatch; the toll does not rest on this source.

- **som-4 — 1991–92 famine — VERIFIED, with single-source toll flag.** The 1992-famine entry confirms verbatim "200,000–300,000 deaths," "According to the BBC 220,000 Somalis died," "The Somali National Front is widely considered to bear primary responsibility," warfare as largest factor + concurrent harsh drought. HRW World Report 1993 — Somalia confirmed real (Refworld; 403s to fetch but verified by search; documents the Aidid–Ali Mahdi fighting, freelance looting, "cycle of famine feeding war"), supporting summary/causation not the count. **FLAG:** only one source carries the numeric range and it holds BOTH ends — not independently sourced low vs high. Verified and consistent but single-source; noted in toll.note. Tier STR re-derived and CONFIRMED (starvation/collapse-driven; man-made via factions, not nature, hence correctly in the perpetrator ledger — but still STR, not EXT, as no group was targeted for destruction as such).
