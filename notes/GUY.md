# Guyana (GUY) — research log

Built from scratch 2026-06-19 via live WebSearch/WebFetch. No prior seed file.

## Final ledger (4 events)

**Victim — "Done to them"**
- `guy-1` STR (UNVERIFIED) — Destruction of the Amerindian peoples under Dutch/British colonization (c.1600-1850). Disease, enslavement, export to the islands, Dutch-armed Carib/Akawaio slave-raiding.
- `guy-2` POL (RESEARCHED) — Plantation slavery + suppression of the 1823 Demerara rebellion. British colonial army shot down rebels and executed dozens more.

**Perpetrator — "In their name"**
- `guy-3` POL (RESEARCHED) — 1962-1964 inter-ethnic violence: Wismar massacre (Afro- on Indo-Guyanese) + Son Chapman bombing (against Afro-Guyanese). Guyanese-on-Guyanese.
- `guy-4` POL (RESEARCHED) — 1980 state assassination of Walter Rodney by the Burnham regime.

## What is solid
- **Demerara 1823**: ~12,000 enslaved rose; British troops (21st Foot, 1st West India Regiment, militia under Gov. Murray / Lt-Col. Leahy) killed 200-500 in the fighting, ~100+ summary executions, 27 hanged after court-martial. Two independent reference sources agree (Wikipedia + Encyclopedia.com), cross-checked with BlackPast. Population context: ~75,000 enslaved in Demerara-Essequibo in 1823; British Caribbean enslaved population fell 776,105 (1807) -> 664,970 (1834).
- **Walter Rodney 1980**: 2014-2016 Guyanese Commission of Inquiry found a state-organized assassination with Burnham's knowledge, carried out by GDF sgt Gregory Smith. National Assembly accepted findings in 2021; death certificate changed to "assassination." Anchored on the National Security Archive briefing (June 2020). Single fatality — not disputed.
- **Son Chapman bombing, 6 July 1964**: 43 killed when a launch on the Demerara River was bombed. Consistent across Guyanese press (Kaieteur, Guyana Chronicle) and the Wikipedia massacres list.
- **1964 disturbances "at least 110 dead"**: repeated across multiple summaries incl. the Pulitzer Center report.

## What is contested / soft
- **Wismar massacre death count**: sources agree on the siege, ~3,000-3,400 Indo-Guyanese expelled, arson/rape/looting; the *number killed* is contested. S. Pasha (2020) records 5 killed, ~50 assaulted, 7+ rapes, 200+ properties destroyed. "Several brutally killed, exact number remains contested" recurs. Treated as contested.
- **Son Chapman perpetrators**: never conclusively identified at the inquest (coroner noted absence of named suspects Harry Singh and Marat). Attributed to the Indo-Guyanese/PPP-aligned camp in the communal-violence framing but not legally established.
- **1962-1964 total toll high end (~176)**: appears in some accounts spanning the full disturbances but was NOT firmly confirmed in sources I could reach; flagged in the toll note, low end (110) is the solid figure.
- **CIA role**: State Dept. archival documents + Pulitzer/Foreign Policy reporting say ~$2.08M in covert action 1962-1968, CIA/AFL-CIO accused of inciting strikes/riots. Complicates but does not erase Guyanese agency in the killings — noted in caveat.

## What could NOT be verified (left null / UNVERIFIED)
- **Amerindian death toll / pre-contact baseline for Guiana specifically**: no defensible number exists in the sources consulted. The qualitative collapse (disease, enslavement, coastal Carib decline by the 19th c.) is well attested but unquantified. Toll left null, event marked UNVERIFIED. Did NOT invent a figure.
- **Slavery-era structural mortality as a clean Guiana death count**: the lethal natural-decrease regime is well documented qualitatively (life expectancy "as short as five years" on sugar estates per one source) but no aggregate Guiana death count was sourceable; described in toll.note, not added to the number.

## Deliberate EXCLUSION
- **Jonestown (18 Nov 1978, ~909-918 dead)**: on Guyanese soil but victims overwhelmingly American Peoples Temple members killed by Jim Jones; only ~8 Guyanese children among the dead. Belongs to the USA ledger (victim + perpetrator), NOT Guyana's. Excluded and explained in the country note. This is the main trap for a Guyana entry and was avoided.

## Asymmetry note
Perpetrator side is genuinely thin and that is honest, not a gap: Guyana independent only since 1966, small state, no large-scale state mass-killing campaign. Its real perpetrator atrocities are domestic-political (1962-64 ethnic violence, Rodney). Victim side is far heavier — colonial powers did the worst of it. File reflects the imbalance rather than forcing balance.

## Audit

Adversarial source-integrity + tier audit, 2026-06-19. Every citation treated as fake until proven otherwise; each source was attacked to falsify it (does the work/author exist, does it support the specific claim attached, is contested toll sourced at both ends, is the tier correctly derived). Country status: **UNVERIFIED** (2 of 4 events verified).

**Verdict by event**

- **guy-1 (Amerindian destruction, STR) → UNVERIFIED (kept).** Tier STR re-derived and confirmed: disease + slave-raiding + displacement = structural demographic collapse, NOT a single deliberate extermination campaign, so STR not EXT — correctly enforced. Source 2 (Tipití, Trinity digital commons, "Amerindians in the Eighteenth Century Plantation System of the Guianas") is REAL, cites Whitehead 1988, and confirms Caribs/Akawaios/Manoas as Dutch slavers — supports the tier. BUT source 1 (Wikipedia "Indigenous peoples in Guyana") was fetched directly and does NOT carry the disease/enslavement/export/19th-c.-Carib-decline claims attributed to it — that content is Minority Rights Group material conflated into the citation. Caveat rewritten to flag the mis-citation; toll stays null (no Guiana-specific figure exists in any reachable source — none invented). Quarantine stands.

- **guy-2 (Demerara 1823 + plantation slavery, POL) → VERIFIED.** Tier POL re-derived and confirmed (deliberate, organized lethal suppression + court-martial executions by the colonial state; the slow-killing slavery regime is the STR layer, correctly held in the toll.note). All three sources verified: Encyclopedia.com fetched directly (12,000 slaves, 37 plantations, Leahy, Bachelor's Adventure, "over 200 killed plus dozens executed", Rev. Smith); Wikipedia fetched directly (200-500 killed, 21st Foot / 1st West India Regiment / militia, Gov. Murray, ~100 summary executions, 45 sentences/27 executed); BlackPast bot-blocked WebFetch (403) but confirmed via search and triangulated (75,000 enslaved of ~80,000 in Demerara-Essequibo; >650,000 in the British Caribbean 1823). Both toll ends carry a real source (low = Encyclopedia.com "over 200"; high = Wikipedia "200-500"). "Son Chapman" / "Demerara" / abolition-era population figures all consistent.

- **guy-3 (1962-64 inter-ethnic violence, POL) → UNVERIFIED (downgraded from RESEARCHED).** Tier POL confirmed (politically/ethnically targeted communal killing). Event facts are sound, but TWO source-integrity failures forced quarantine: (1) **fabricated/unverifiable researcher attribution** — the toll.note and one source credited the Wismar figures to "a researcher (S. Pasha, 2020)"; no such researcher could be found, the cited Kaieteur article (verified directly) does NOT mention any "Pasha", and the genuine Wismar scholarship is by Dr. Yog Mahadeo and Prof. Baytoram Ramharack. Attribution removed. (2) **mis-citation of the contested toll** — toll.low was "110, per Pulitzer Center"; the Pulitzer/Foreign Policy piece actually says "nearly 200", and "110" traces to nothing reachable. Corrected: low set to 160 (Wikipedia "Freedom House bombing": at least ~160 fatalities by late July 1964 excl. Son Chapman), high to 176 (the firmly attested "176 killed, 920 injured" 1964 GAWU-strike riot total) — both ends now sourced. Wismar (5 killed, ~3,400 expelled, 200+ properties) and Son Chapman (43, some accounts 36) re-verified against the Wikipedia massacres list and Guyanese press.

- **guy-4 (Walter Rodney assassination, POL) → VERIFIED.** Tier POL confirmed (targeted state political murder). National Security Archive briefing fetched directly and fully corroborates: 2016 COI found "a State organized assassination with the knowledge of Prime Minister Burnham", carried out by GDF sgt Gregory Smith "acting as an agent of the State"; National Assembly Resolution No. 23 (Aug 2021) adopted the findings; death reclassified to "assassination". Single fatality, not disputed.
