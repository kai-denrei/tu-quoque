# Poland (POL) — research notes

## Victim ledger ("Done to them")

- **pol-1 The Holocaust of Polish Jewry (1939–45, EXT).** ~2.8–3M of pre-war Poland's
  ~3.3M Jews murdered (~90%), most in the six extermination camps Germany built on
  Polish soil. Toll low (~2.8M, IPN midpoint) and high (~3M / "over 90%", USHMM &
  Yad Vashem) both well-sourced. Not disputed as to fact — only scholarly precision.
- **pol-2 German occupation terror against ethnic Poles (1939–45, POL).** USHMM:
  1.8–1.9M non-Jewish Polish civilians killed (AB-Aktion murders of teachers/priests/
  intelligentsia, hostage reprisals, 1.5M deported for forced labour, Warsaw Uprising).
  IPN broader figure ~2.77M ethnic Poles. Tiered POL (national decapitation/subjugation),
  not EXT — Germany did not aim to kill every Pole. disputed:true reflects counting
  boundaries, not a contested event.
- **pol-3 Katyn & Soviet repression (1939–41, POL).** 21,857 executed by NKVD spring
  1940 on Stalin's order — a *documented* count from the 1990 Soviet declassification,
  used as the toll low. High end (150,000 IPN confirmed → ~500,000 Łuczak) folds in
  Gulag/deportation deaths and is an estimate. received_lens "suppressed" because the
  USSR blamed Katyn on Germany for ~50 years.

## Perpetrator ledger ("In their name")

- **pol-4 Jedwabne & summer 1941 pogroms (1941, EXT).** The cleanest Polish-perpetrator
  case. IPN (2002): at least 340 Jews murdered at Jedwabne on 10 July 1941 by ethnic
  Poles — ~40–50 men first, then ~300 burned alive in a barn — Germans present and
  approving but not coercing. Older ~1,600 figure (monument) IPN calls "highly unlikely";
  used only as the disputed high end. Part of a regional wave across 20+ towns (Radziłów
  ~500–800, Wąsosz ~150–250). Tiered EXT: local annihilation of a group by neighbours.
- **pol-5 Postwar expulsion of Germans & Operation Vistula (1945–47, STR).** Agent is the
  *Soviet-installed communist Polish state* — flagged plainly in agent/note/relevance.
  ~4M Germans expelled by 1948; Operation Vistula (1947) forcibly deported ~141,000
  Ukrainians/Lemkos/Boykos. Tiered STR: most deaths from cold/hunger/disease/abuse in
  transit and camps, not a campaign to kill the groups as such.

## What is contested / could NOT be cleanly verified

- **German expulsion death toll is the single most contested figure here.** Estimates
  range from ~60,000 (camp deaths, German Federal Archives 1974) to ~400,000 (Fed.
  Archives total east of Oder–Neisse) to ~2M (Schieder 1958). The 1995–96 joint
  German–Polish commission called the high demographic figures "based on faulty data."
  Crucially, much of the death toll is disease/starvation during *flight before the Red
  Army*, NOT directly at Polish hands — captured in toll.note. I deliberately did NOT
  adopt the 2M figure (expellee-lobby inflated). Toll set 60,000–400,000, disputed:true.
- **Jedwabne high end** (~1,600) is rejected by the IPN; kept only to show the dispute.
- **Per-town pogrom counts** (Radziłów 500–800, Wąsosz 150–250) vary by source.
- **Soviet repression high end** (~500,000, Łuczak) is an extrapolation including
  deportation deaths; the IPN's confirmed figure is ~150,000.
- Łambinowice camp deaths (~1,000–1,500) and the Gęborski barn-fire incident (~48 dead)
  documented by the Central Museum of POWs (CMJW); relayed here via the Gęborski/
  Łambinowice reference pages rather than read off CMJW directly.

## Asymmetry note

The ledgers are deliberately asymmetric and the country `note` says so: Poland suffered
far more than it inflicted (roughly a sixth of its people killed by two occupiers). The
perpetrator ledger is real but smaller, and one of its two entries is the act of a
Soviet-imposed regime. Nothing was invented to balance the two sides.

### Source verification results
- **pol-1 — VERIFIED.** USHMM live page: "the Germans murdered at least 3 million Jewish
  citizens of Poland" (toll.high ✓). Yad Vashem (via search of the cited URL): 3.3M pre-war,
  ~380,000 survivors, six named death camps Chełmno/Bełżec/Sobibór/Treblinka/Majdanek/
  Auschwitz-Birkenau (summary ✓). IPN via WWII-casualties page: 2,700,000–2,900,000 Polish
  Jews (toll.low ✓). All three confirmed. Both toll ends sourced.
- **pol-2 — VERIFIED.** USHMM live page: "between 1.8 and 1.9 million non-Jewish Polish
  civilians" + AB-Aktion + "at least 1.5 million ... deported ... for forced labor"
  (toll.low ✓). IPN via casualties page: 2,770,000 ethnic Poles under German occupation
  (toll.high ✓). Both ends sourced.
- **pol-3 — VERIFIED.** Katyn page: 21,857 executed per 1990 Soviet declassification;
  Politburo order 5 Mar 1940 signed by Stalin + 5 others; categories (POWs, police,
  intelligentsia, border guards) confirmed; decades of Soviet denial confirmed (toll.low,
  summary ✓). Britannica (confirmed via search; the URL 403s to bots but the page exists
  and states ~22,000 on Stalin's orders) (summary ✓). IPN 150,000 + Łuczak ~500,000
  confirmed on casualties page (toll.high ✓). Both ends sourced.
- **pol-4 — VERIFIED (with a tightened caveat).** Wikipedia Jedwabne page: IPN "at least
  340," 1,600 "highly unlikely," ethnic-Pole perpetrators, German presence "tantamount to
  consent ... but did not coerce," ~300 burned in Śleszyński's barn (toll.low, summary ✓).
  ŻIH/Żbikowski article exists with the exact cited title; documents 67 locations and
  regional Polish-perpetrator wave; gives ~1,000 for Jedwabne (summary ✓, and independently
  corroborates the disputed high end). WJC page exists and DOES cite 1,600 — but only inside
  a debated list ("whether 200, or 250, or 1,600"), and it does NOT frame Jedwabne as "one
  of ~20+ towns." Cite/caveat corrected to say exactly that; toll.high stays sourced (1,600
  is on the page; regional scope carried by ŻIH). No falsification; defect was framing only.
- **pol-5 — UNVERIFIED (quarantined).** Operation Vistula side fully verified: ~141,000
  Ukrainians/Lemkos/Boykos forcibly resettled by the Soviet-installed communist Polish
  state/army to break the UPA (summary ✓), Jaworzno imprisonment confirmed. Gęborski page
  confirms his Łambinowice command + the ~48-death barn-fire. BUT two real integrity defects
  on the German-expulsion figures:
  1. **Misattribution.** The "1995–96 joint German–Polish commission judged the old
     demographic figures 'based on faulty data'" was wrong — that commission was the joint
     German–**Czech** commission and its "faulty data" finding was about the ~220,000–270,000
     figure for **Czechoslovakia** (revised to 15,000–30,000), not Poland/Oder–Neisse.
     Corrected and flagged in toll.note + source caveat; this notes file's earlier line
     repeating the claim is hereby superseded.
  2. **Inaccurate Bundesarchiv breakdown.** The "~60,000 in post-war internment camps"
     attributed to the German Federal Archives 1974 study is not what that study reports
     (its components total ~610,000, including 227,000 internment-camp deaths, with "at
     least 400,000" as the stated floor). Corrected in note/cite.
  3. **Unconfirmed attribution.** The "~1,000–1,500 German deaths documented by CMJW" for
     Łambinowice is not on the cited Gęborski page and CMJW is not cited there; flagged for
     re-sourcing to CMJW directly. Jaworzno ~160 and Zawadka Morochowska 56 likewise need
     re-sourcing.
  Event held UNVERIFIED until the German death-toll figures are re-sourced cleanly. No
