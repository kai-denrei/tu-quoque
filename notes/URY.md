# Uruguay (URY) — research log

Built from scratch 2026-06-19. Status: RESEARCHED. 3 events (1 victim, 2 perpetrator).

## Structure / framing decision

Uruguay's ledgers are genuinely asymmetric, and the asymmetry is the entry's whole point:

- **Perpetrator side is heavy and foundational.** Two clear, well-documented state atrocities:
  - **ury-2 Salsipuedes / extermination of the Charrua (1831–32)** — EXT. The newborn republic under President Fructuoso Rivera destroyed the country's original people.
  - **ury-3 Civic-military dictatorship state terror (1973–1985)** — POL. State against its own citizens; world-record per-capita political imprisonment.

- **Victim side is the foreign-perpetrated mirror of the same era.**
  - **ury-1 Operation Condor — Uruguayan exiles hunted abroad (1975–1981)** — POL. Most of Uruguay's ~197 disappeared were seized in Argentina, not at home. Agent is Argentine/Chilean forces (with Uruguayan SID complicity). This is the cleanest genuinely externally-perpetrated atrocity against Uruguayans.

### Events deliberately NOT entered (and why)
- **Guerra Grande (1839–1851 / Great Siege of Montevideo).** Internal Colorado vs Blanco civil war with French/British/Brazilian intervention; not a foreign massacre of Uruguayans. Montevideo itself reportedly suffered relatively little. Not a Uruguay-victim mass-atrocity event.
- **War of the Triple Alliance (1864–1870).** Uruguay was a junior member of the *aggressor* alliance (Brazil/Argentina/Uruguay) against Paraguay; ~5,600 Uruguayan troops, ~3,100 dead. The catastrophe (half of Paraguay's population) is a Paraguay-victim / alliance-perpetrator event, and Uruguay's contribution was minor. Belongs in PRY, not URY. Did not enter Uruguay as perpetrator either, given how small and how drawn-in its role was — flagged in country note instead.
- **Pre-colonial / colonial Charrua demographic decline** is folded into ury-2's toll note rather than given a separate STR entry, because the decisive, documentable event is the 1831 state campaign, and no separate territory-specific colonial death figure exists.

## What is verified / solid
- **Salsipuedes 1831:** date (11 April 1831), agent (Fructuoso Rivera, field command Bernabe Rivera), method (lured to a friendly meeting then attacked), official report ~40 killed + ~300 captured, captives marched ~260 km to Montevideo and enslaved, four taken to Paris and exhibited. By 1840 ~18 Charrua said to survive. Pre-colonial indigenous pop. estimated ≤10,000–20,000; Charrua already in the hundreds by 1831. Multiple sources agree.
- **Dictatorship:** ~197 enforced disappearances, ~202 extrajudicial executions (1968–1985); ~5,000 political prisoners; ~1 in 500 a political prisoner, ~1 in 50 through detention; highest per-capita political-prisoner concentration in the world by early 1976. Coup via Bordaberry's June 1973 dissolution of parliament. Strongly sourced (Lessa / The Conversation gives the precise per-capita figures).
- **Condor:** Uruguayans ~48% of all documented Operation Condor victims (largest national share). Of ~197 disappeared, ~31 inside Uruguay vs ~182 in Argentina. Automotores Orletti (Buenos Aires) was the Condor HQ; ~300 detainees of several nationalities passed through in 1976, ~10% survived; ~27 Uruguayans disappeared in BA Aug–Oct 1976. Uruguayan SID complicit in interrogating/repatriating captured exiles.

## What is contested / could NOT be verified
- **Salsipuedes campaign-wide death toll.** No verified total. The official ~40 understates it but the true magnitude is destruction-of-a-people, not a body count, so **toll.high left null** rather than invented. The 'genocide' label is broadly accepted but is a moral framing. The '18 survivors by 1840' is a traditional account, not a census.
- **Disappeared total is moving and overlapping.** Official ~197 vs INDDHH's 205 cases (2026); domestic vs Condor-abroad split (~31 vs ~182) is approximate and the two ledger entries (ury-1 victim abroad, ury-3 perpetrator total) intentionally OVERLAP on the disappeared — noted in both toll notes that they must not be summed.
- **Dictatorship killed/disappeared combined (~399)** used as toll.high for ury-3; small in absolute terms, deliberately framed as "breadth not body count."
