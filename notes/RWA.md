## Two ledgers
The seed already had both sides, so no new event was added — only sourcing and tier reasoning. Victim ledger: the 1994 genocide against the Tutsi. Perpetrator ledger: RPF reprisal killings in Rwanda (1994) plus the hunt and mass killing of Hutu refugees in Zaire/Congo (1996–97). Rwanda is one of the clearest single-nation demonstrations of the dual lens.

## Event rwa-1 — 1994 genocide against the Tutsi (victim, EXT)
- **Tier EXT**: deliberate destruction of the Tutsi as a group — lists, roadblocks, RTLM incitement, militia weapon distribution. This is intent-driven extermination, not demographic collapse.
- **Low end ~500,000**: Alison Des Forges / HRW, *Leave None to Tell the Story* (1999) — "at least half a million," ~77% of a ~657,000 pre-genocide Tutsi population. Reinforced by the 2020 *Journal of Genocide Research* "Contested Counting" symposium (incl. O. S. McDoom), clustering at 500,000–600,000 Tutsi.
- **High end 1,074,017**: official Government of Rwanda count (includes moderate Hutu and Twa). Flagged disputed — demographers regard >1 million as overstating the pre-genocide Tutsi population. Caveat added: the RPF-led state is an interested party.
- **Contested**: Davenport & Stam's low ~200,000-Tutsi figure exists but is widely disputed (Verpoorten, African Arguments) on a contested census baseline; I did NOT use it as the floor, only noted it.
- **Received lens: memorial** — Kwibuka commemoration is central to state identity.

## Event rwa-2 — RPF reprisals & the Congo hunt for Hutu refugees (perpetrator, WAR)
Two linked episodes, both attributable to Rwandan (RPA) forces:
- **In Rwanda, 1994**: Gersony Report (UNHCR field assessment, suppressed) — 25,000–45,000 killed April–August 1994. Ex-RPF interior minister Seth Sendashonga estimated ~60,000 through 1995. The Gersony Report was never formally finalized; reconstructed from leaked memos/field notes (2010). Marked **primary** with a caveat.
- **In Zaire/Congo, 1996–97**: UN Mapping Report (OHCHR, 2010) — "apparently relentless pursuit and mass killing of Hutu refugees," "several tens of thousands." Acting UN High Commissioner (8 July 1997) said ~200,000 could have been massacred; René Lemarchand, *Forgotten Genocides* (2011), cites 200,000–233,000.
- **Toll range used**: low 25,000 (in-Rwanda floor), high 233,000 (upper Congo estimate). disputed = true.
- **Tier WAR, not EXT**: deliberately. The UN Mapping Report itself frames genocidal intent in Congo as an open question for a court — it lays out factors both for and against. Calling it EXT would overstate what the documentation establishes; calling it nothing would whitewash. WAR (systematic killing of civilians/refugees in armed conflict) is the defensible tag.
- **Received lens: denied** — Rwanda's "genocide ideology"/"divisionism" laws criminalize the "double genocide" framing and have been used against critics (e.g. Victoire Ingabire) who called for prosecuting RPF war crimes; the Gersony Report was actively suppressed.

PASS — no over-claim.
- **Government of Rwanda 1,074,017** [toll.high] — VERIFIED. Real figure (July 2000 census; ~951,018 named; reported via ReliefWeb/Wikipedia). - **Gourevitch (1998)** [summary] — VERIFIED. Real book; narrative account of the genocide's organization. PASS (no url; narrative-only, acceptable).
- **Toll**: low and high separately sourced. disputed=true correct. Davenport & Stam low ~200k correctly excluded as the floor and only mentioned. PASS.
- **Tier re-derived as EXT independently**: deliberate destruction of the Tutsi as such (lists, roadblocks, RTLM incitement, militia weapon distribution) = extermination, intent-driven, not disease/famine collapse. Not an STR case. Agree with EXT.

### Event rwa-2 — RPF reprisals + Congo hunt for Hutu refugees (perpetrator, WAR) → VERIFIED
- **Gersony Report (UNHCR field assessment, 1994)** [toll.low] — VERIFIED. Real; RPF killed est. 25,000–45,000 April–August 1994; UN-suppressed and its existence denied; validity remains disputed. Caveat (never formally finalized; reconstructed) appropriate. PASS.

Real; "apparently relentless pursuit and mass killing of Hutu refugees," "several tens of thousands"; explicitly leaves genocidal intent as an open question for a competent court and notes repatriations as a countervailing factor. - **Lemarchand, *Forgotten Genocides* (2011) + acting UN High Commissioner (8 July 1997)** [toll.high] — VERIFIED. Book real (Univ. of Pennsylvania Press; Reyntjens & Lemarchand chapter "Mass Murder in Eastern Congo, 1996–97"); 200,000–233,000 figure and the 1997 ~200,000 UNHCR statement both confirmed. PASS.

The cited Wikipedia page supports the laws/denial framing **generally** but does **not** name Victoire Ingabire. The specific Ingabire example (16 Jan 2010 Gisozi speech calling for prosecution of RPF crimes; arrested Oct 2010; charged under the 2008 genocide-ideology law) is accurate and corroborated independently (HRW 2012, Amnesty, World Without Genocide). Source–claim mismatch is minor and the underlying fact is true; caveat updated in the JSON to disclose that the URL does not itself name Ingabire. Not a toll source — correctly tagged. PASS.
- **Toll**: low (Gersony) and high (Lemarchand/UNHCR) separately sourced. disputed=true correct. Underlying Congo counts genuinely soft (dispersed/repatriated refugees, destroyed sites) — flagged in toll.note. PASS.
- **Tier re-derived as WAR independently**: systematic killing of unarmed civilians/refugees during and after armed conflict, with genocidal intent left undetermined by the only official mapping exercise. Not a clear destruction-of-group-as-such case; not disease-driven. WAR (war crimes / crimes against humanity scale) is the defensible tag. Agree with WAR — calling it EXT would over-state the documentation.
