## Events

### mex-1 — Mexica (Aztec) human sacrifice — POL, perpetrator, pre-colonial
- **Toll: ~1,000–20,000/yr** (disputed). Low = Caroline Dodds Pennock's plausible-range floor; the contemporary Codex Telleriano-Remensis logs ~4,000 total at the 1487 rededication. High defensible ≈ 20,000/yr (Zumárraga's claim, called "more plausible" by V.D. Hanson). The 250,000/yr figure (Woodrow Borah, a 1%-of-population extrapolation) and the Spanish 80,400-in-four-days claim are marked as inflated/rejected — the latter is logistically impossible (~14 kills/minute round the clock).
- **Archaeology corroborates large scale**: Huey Tzompantli yielded 600+ skulls by 2020 (men, women, children); Offering 48 held ~42 children sacrificed to Tláloc during the 1450s drought (López Luján / Templo Mayor Project; Román Berrelleza found malnutrition markers).
- **Tier POL** not EXT: state cult terror against captives/tribute, not destruction of a group as such.
- **Lens: contested** — Aztec heritage is broadly triumphal in Mexican identity (eagle-on-cactus flag), but sacrifice specifically is minimized/contested.

### mex-2 — Conquest and fall of Tenochtitlan — WAR, victim, colonial
- **Siege toll ~100,000–240,000** indigenous dead (Wikipedia, drawing on Spanish accounts). **Separately**, 1520 smallpox killed ~40% of central Mexico in a year (PBS NewsHour; Mesoamerica-wide 5–8 million across the outbreak).
- **Disease kept analytically separate** per project law: smallpox = demographic collapse / closer to STR, NOT folded into the violent siege toll and NOT EXT (no intent; Europeans did not understand contagion).
- **Restall** (When Montezuma Met Cortés; Seven Myths) anchors the "multi-ethnic coalition war, not Spanish superhumans" point — Cortés had ~20,000 allied warriors at the siege, up to ~200,000 across the campaign.
- **Tier WAR**: siege/storm of a city in war.
- **Lens: flagellated** — "la Conquista" as foundational mestizaje trauma.

### mex-3 — Yaqui deportation & extermination under Porfirio Díaz — EXT, perpetrator, modern
- **Toll ~15,000–60,000 dead**; ~8,000–15,000 deported 1904–1909 (Gov. Rafael Izábal's manhunts). At least two-thirds of deportees dead within their first year on Yucatán henequen estates.
- **Tier EXT** (the one EXT call in this file): the regime targeted the whole Yaqui people — peaceful and rebel alike — for removal/destruction to clear Sonora's river valley. This is deliberate destruction of a defined group, distinct from counterinsurgency.
- **Sources**: John Kenneth Turner, *Barbarous Mexico* (1910 primary muckraking exposé — flagged as advocacy aimed at toppling Díaz); Evelyn Hu-DeHart, "Development and Rural Rebellion: Pacification of the Yaquis in the Late Porfiriato," *HAHR* 54:1 (1974), Duke UP — the scholarly substantiation; Wikipedia "Yaqui Wars" for the numeric ranges.
- **Lens: suppressed** — described in sources as "one of the largest forgotten genocides of modern Mexico."

### mex-4 — Tlatelolco massacre — POL, perpetrator, contemporary
- **Toll: 44 documented (firm floor) to ~300–400** (credible high); official initial claim ~20–28 (some accounts 4). Kate Doyle / National Security Archive (GWU) documented 44 from declassified files and established Presidential Guard snipers staged the pretext. Activist/eyewitness ~300, some claims to 500.
- **Tier POL**: state killing of unarmed protesters to crush a movement before the Olympics.
- **Lens: memorial** — moved from suppression to public memory: 2008 National Day of Mourning, 2024 formal state apology by President Sheinbaum.

## What is contested / soft
- **Aztec sacrifice numbers**: genuinely wide; the high textual figures are colonial propaganda. Archaeology confirms scale but caps the upper end. This is the flagship source-bias example for the atlas.
- **Tenochtitlan siege toll**: high end (240,000) rests partly on Cortés's own letters and Bernal Díaz — motive to magnify. Marked soft.
- **Yaqui deaths**: 15,000–60,000 is a reconstruction; the regime kept no honest count.
- **Tlatelolco**: bodies were removed and the count concealed for decades; 44 is verified minimum, not the likely true total.
