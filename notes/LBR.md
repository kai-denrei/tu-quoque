# Liberia (LBR) — research log

Built from scratch (no seed file). Real web research via WebSearch/WebFetch.

## The dual-lens shape
Liberia is a textbook dual-lens case because the victim/perpetrator line runs through its founding. Settled from 1822 by free/freed African Americans under the American Colonization Society — descendants of the enslaved — who then built a settler caste over the indigenous majority (True Whig Party / Americo-Liberian rule, ~1847–1980). So the same events read opposite from below and above.

## Events written
**Victim ("Done to them")**
- lbr-1 — Fernando Po forced-labour scandal (1920s–1930). Indigenous Liberians coerced into near-slave labour. Toll null (no defensible death count). UNVERIFIED.
- lbr-2 — The civil wars (1989–2003). ~150,000–250,000 killed, ~1/3 displaced. RESEARCHED.

**Perpetrator ("In their name")**
- lbr-3 — The Fernando Po system from the perpetrator side: Americo-Liberian state (King, VP Yancy) selling/coercing its own citizens. Same event as lbr-1, opposite lens — the deliberate convergence. Toll null. UNVERIFIED.
- lbr-4 — Doe regime / AFL: 1985 Nimba reprisals against Gio/Mano (~3,000) + St. Peter's Lutheran Church massacre 29 Jul 1990 (~600; US initial estimate 200–300). UNVERIFIED (tolls disputed).
- lbr-5 — Charles Taylor's NPFL: TRC found it committed the largest share of war abuses; named massacres (Bakedu 300+, Prafar Town, Karnplay church). No defensible NPFL-only aggregate → toll null. UNVERIFIED.

## Tolls and what is contested
- **Civil-war total**: range 150,000–250,000 across both wars (1989–2003). CJA gives "150,000 to 250,000"; the Liberian TRC and much press cite "over 250,000." Set low=150,000, high=250,000, disputed=true. This is an estimate, not a count — record-keeping collapsed.
- **Lutheran Church massacre**: commonly cited ~600; US government initially estimated 200–300; some accounts say up to 2,000 were sheltering. Used 600 as the low of lbr-4's range, with the dispute noted. Independently corroborated by CJA's *Jane W. v. Thomas* litigation (US civil judgment vs. Moses Thomas).
- **1985 Nimba reprisals**: ~3,000 Gio/Mano commonly cited; an estimate from press/HR reporting, precision disputed.
- **Fernando Po**: thousands coerced over the decade, but mortality never quantified — toll left null on BOTH ledger entries. Did NOT invent a number.

### Source integrity — what was checked and found
- **Christy Commission (lbr-1, lbr-3).** Real. The verbatim finding "scarcely distinguishable from slave raiding and slave trading" is confirmed across EBSCO, Liberia Past and Present, and a 1930 League report dated 8 Sept 1930. Commission membership (Cuthbert Christy, ex-president Arthur Barclay, Charles S. Johnson of Fisk) checks out. King and Yancy resignations confirmed; Yancy found to have organised the Fernando Po traffic. EBSCO and Liberia Past and Present pages both live and supporting. Wikipedia 'Persecution against Indigenous Liberians' exists (a stub) and does cite the 1930 Commission report. Postmaster-General Samuel A. Ross title confirmed (he had already died 1929, so was an accused but not surviving official). Tolls correctly null on both ledgers — no defensible death count exists. Status stays UNVERIFIED on both, correctly (null toll).
- **Liberian civil wars (lbr-2).** CJA page live and states verbatim "estimated deaths of 150,000 to 250,000 men, women and children." TRC McMaster PDF resolves (4.2 MB volume) and the "all factions culpable / NPFL largest share / 98 perpetrators" findings are corroborated independently (Carter Center, GlobalSecurity). NPFL share quantified at ~40% of reported violations. **Correction applied:** the note and the CJA cite both claimed "one-third of the population displaced," but the CJA page actually says "over half the country's population." Fixed to match the source on both. Both ledger ends sourced (low=CJA verified, high=TRC verified). Tier WAR correct. Status upgraded RESEARCHED -> VERIFIED.
- **Doe / Lutheran Church massacre (lbr-4).** Monrovia Church massacre Wikipedia page live; ~600 toll, US 200-300 estimate, ~30 AFL soldiers, Krahn perpetrators, Gio/Mano victims all confirmed verbatim. CJA Jane W. v. Thomas litigation page live and independently corroborates the church massacre (Moses Thomas, $84M judgment). **Quarantine flag:** the cited Wikipedia '1985 Liberian coup attempt' page supports only "massacred thousands of civilians in retaliation" — it does NOT contain the specific figure 3,000 used as toll.high. The precise 3,000 is a wider press/HR estimate not pinned to its cited source. toll.high retained as an order-of-magnitude ("thousands") estimate but flagged; event stays UNVERIFIED. Tier POL correct (state killing civilians by ethnicity as collective punishment — not EXT, not WAR).
- **Taylor / NPFL (lbr-5).** TRC McMaster PDF and World Without Genocide both real and supporting. **Correction applied:** the cited International Justice Monitor article of 25 Jan 2010 is REAL and supports the general "widespread and systematic NPFL attacks on civilians" claim (ethnic targeting of Krahn/Mandingo, child soldiers, killing of five American nuns) — but it does NOT name the Bakedu, Prafar Town, or Karnplay massacres, which the old cite falsely attributed to it. Those three massacres are in fact documented in Liberian TRC public-hearing press releases (trcliberia.cc.gatech.edu); Bakedu (300+, 12 July 1990, palaver-hut shooting, survivor Sekou Jabateh) verified at press_releases/154.html, Prafar Town (~50, 1 Aug 1990, Sinoe County) and Never-Die Church Karnplay (Nimba) confirmed via TRC hearing records. IJMonitor cite text rewritten to what it actually supports; a verified TRC press-release source added for the named massacres. Toll correctly null (no NPFL-only aggregate is sourceable). Tier WAR correct. Status stays UNVERIFIED.
