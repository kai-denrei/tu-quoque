# Estonia (EST) — research notes

Built from scratch, 2026-06-19. Status: RESEARCHED.

## What the record shows

Estonia's atrocity history is dominated by being occupied in turn by the USSR (1940–41), Nazi Germany (1941–44), and the USSR again (1944–91). The dual ledger comes out heavily asymmetric, and honestly so.

### Victim ledger (Soviet violence)
- **March 1949, Operation Priboi (est-1)** — the largest single blow. ~20,480–20,722 Estonians deported to Siberia in three days, ~half women, ~30% children. En-route deaths from Estonia were low (~45) but settlement mortality was high; Baltic-wide ~4,123 dead (4.5%) by end-1950. Entered as a deportation toll, not a death count. Tier POL.
- **June 1941 deportation (est-2)** — ~9,254–10,861 deported on 14–17 June; only 4,331 ever returned. Men sent to Gulag camps where mortality was catastrophic (of 3,000+ men, only a few hundred alive by spring 1942). Days before the German invasion — context for later collaboration. Tier POL.

### Perpetrator ledger (the Holocaust)
- **Estonian collaboration in the Holocaust (est-3)** — the hard entry. Estonia's small pre-war Jewish community (~4,500) mostly fled; ~950–1,000 who stayed were murdered by end-1941, and Estonia was declared "Judenfrei." The Nazis then shipped ~10,000 foreign Jews into Estonian camps (Vaivara, Klooga) and killing sites (Kalevi-Liiva). Estonian auxiliaries — Security Police under Ain-Ervin Mere, Omakaitse, police battalions — participated, under Einsatzkommando 1A (Sandberger). Kalevi-Liiva ~1,700–2,000 killed (Sept 1942); Klooga ~2,000–2,500 (Sept 1944). Tier EXT (the act is extermination of a people), most victims not Estonian. Lens: contested.

## What is contested / could not be pinned to one number
- **No single Estonian death figure for either deportation.** Both are entered as deportation counts (low/high) with the death scale described qualitatively, because scholarship gives ranges and category-dependent totals, not one agreed number. This is deliberate per the toll rules.
- **Holocaust toll is category-split and disputed.** ~950–1,000 Estonian Jews is firm; the count of foreign Jews killed on Estonian soil with Estonian-auxiliary participation is approximate (used ~7,500 as a defensible high-end order of magnitude). The Estonian History Commission's ~35,000 figure for all Nazi-occupation deaths includes Soviet POWs and others beyond the Holocaust and was NOT used as the toll.
- **Accountability is itself contested.** The 1961 Soviet Tallinn trial of Mere/Gerrets/Viik was real but also a Cold War propaganda instrument; post-1991 Estonian reckoning with collaboration has been uneven. Noted in the country `note` and the est-3 lens.
- **Kalevi-Liiva / Klooga date and count variants.** Sources differ slightly (Kalevi-Liiva 1,700–2,000; Klooga 2,000–2,500; Klooga date cited as both 19 Aug and 19 Sept 1944). Ranges used to reflect this.
