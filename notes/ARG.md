## What is solid
- **AMIA (1994):** 85 dead is firm and uncontested across WJC, ADL, U.S. State Dept, Wikipedia. Deadliest terror attack in Argentine history. Attribution to Iran (directed) / Hezbollah (carried out) per Argentine court rulings; Nisman found dead Jan 2015. No convictions to date.
- **Embassy (1992):** 29 dead, 242 injured, firm. Mostly Argentine civilians; 4 Israelis. Embassy + neighbouring church/school destroyed. 2024 Argentine court ruled Iran responsible. No convictions.
- **Dirty War documented floor:** CONADEP *Nunca Más* (1984) = 8,961 documented disappearances; 2015 RUVTE state registry = 8,631 (7,018 disappeared + 1,613 killed). Both stress these are floors. ~500 stolen babies (Grandmothers of Plaza de Mayo). Death flights attested.

## What is contested
- **Dirty War toll:** the central dispute. Documented ~8,600–9,000 vs. human-rights movement's **30,000** (Mothers/Grandmothers of Plaza de Mayo, most parties). By 2003 the human-rights secretariat had logged ~13,000. Declassified U.S. doc: Argentine intelligence estimated ~22,000 killed/disappeared by mid-1978. The 8,631↔30,000 range is shown on the entry; it is a live political fault line (a denialist current disputes the 30,000, and the LA Nación source leans into that camp — flagged in caveat). Toll kept as RESEARCHED with a sourced range because both ends are sourced.
- **Conquest of the Desert toll:** "killed" (~1,000, one account ~1,313 in Roca's 1879 sweep) vs. "destroyed as peoples" (15,000+ taken captive/displaced; families broken; captives prevented from reproducing; Teushen and others driven extinct). No reliable single death count for the whole campaign exists → event held **UNVERIFIED** as a death toll; high bound (15,000) is a scale-of-destruction ceiling, not a body count.
- **Genocide characterization (Conquest of the Desert):** contested among Argentine historians and politically charged. Literary scholar Jens Andermann classifies it as genocide; a 2022 Argentine court case seeks formal genocide recognition (Agencia Presentes). Tier set EXT (deliberate destruction of defined groups, incl. preventing reproduction), with tier_reasoning flagging the dispute.

## What could NOT be verified / deliberately omitted
- **Spanish colonial demographic collapse in the Río de la Plata (1530s+):** real (smallpox/measles in non-immune populations, mining/forced labour, Mendoza's failed 1536 Buenos Aires settlement, Jesuit reductions). But NO reliable territory-specific death figure for what became Argentina exists. Deliberately NOT entered as a separate ARG victim event to avoid an invented toll; folded into the country note instead. This is the main asymmetry: Argentina's "done to them" is dominated by foreign-directed modern terror, not a single documented founding-era mass death of its own.
- The Dirty War is filed as **perpetrator** (agent = Argentine state) but is noted as self-inflicted national trauma (Argentines killed by Argentines) — the dual lens collapsing onto one people.
