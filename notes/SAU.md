# Saudi Arabia (SAU) — Research Notes

Built from scratch 2026-06-18 via live web research (WebSearch + WebFetch). No prior seed file existed. Status: RESEARCHED. Two ledgers populated: 2 victim / 2 perpetrator — but **deliberately asymmetric in weight**, and the asymmetry is the finding (see below).

## The asymmetry (key framing point)

Saudi Arabia as a modern state (founded 1932, grown out of the Najd from 1902) has suffered **few mass-atrocity events done TO its own people**. The strongest victim entries are (1) a WWI famine that hit the Hejaz before the Saudi state existed, and (2) the Saudi state killing its own Shia minority. By contrast the perpetrator side is heavy and well-sourced — the Taif massacre during the conquest, and above all the Yemen war (~377,000 dead). I did NOT invent anything to balance the ledgers; I recorded what is real and explained the imbalance in the country `note`.

## Done to them (victim)

### sau-1 — WWI Famine and Siege of Medina in the Hejaz (1916–1919), tier STR
- The Hejaz (now western Saudi Arabia) was hit by Allied naval blockade of the Red Sea, Ottoman army requisitioning, a 1915–16 locust plague, and the 2.5-year siege of Medina. Hajj traffic the economy depended on collapsed (300,000 in 1912 → 60,000 in 1916). Medina (~75,000 people) endured shortage, locusts, disease.
- **TOLL LEFT NULL — this is the principal unverified gap.** The commonly cited "up to 500,000 deaths by 1918" (and a ~300,000 variant) is attached in sources to the **broader Greater Syria / Safarbarlik famine**, NOT the Hejaz specifically. Attributing it to the Hejaz would be an error. No Hejaz-only mortality figure exists in the available record. Event flagged **UNVERIFIED**, toll low/high = null.
- Agent named honestly as multiple: Ottoman army, Allied blockade, Arab Revolt besiegers — no single clean perpetrator.
- Sources: Away from the Western Front (Arab Revolt page — used ONLY for famine mechanism, with explicit caveat that 500k is regional not Hejaz); Wikipedia (Siege of Medina); 1914-1918-Online peer-reviewed encyclopedia (Medina siege, civilian hardship, ~75,000 pop).

### sau-2 — State Killing of the Shia Minority in the Eastern Province / Qatif (1979–1983+), tier POL
- Nov 1979 Qatif Uprising (Muharram Intifada): Shia of Qatif/al-Hasa defied a ban (in place since 1913) on public Ashura processions; the Saudi National Guard crushed them. ~20–24 killed. Cumulative deaths from uprising + later crackdown ~182–219 by 1983.
- This is a **victim event whose agent is the Saudi state itself** — the rare case where a country's own people enter its victim ledger at the hands of its own rulers. Flagged this in `relevance`.
- Toll: low 20 (1979 floor), high 219 (cumulative-by-1983 ceiling). disputed=true (edges blend uprising with later repression; OIR — a Shia opposition group, interested party — claimed 60 members dead). Carnegie/Wehrey independently corroborates "at least two dozen perished."
- Status RESEARCHED.
- Sources: Wikipedia (1979 Qatif Uprising); Toby Matthiesen, IJMES (Cambridge UP), "Rebellion on the Saudi Periphery"; Carnegie/Frederic Wehrey (2013), "The Forgotten Uprising in Eastern Saudi Arabia."

## In their name (perpetrator)

### sau-3 — Taif Massacre and the Saudi Conquest of the Hejaz (1924–1925), tier POL
- In Ibn Saud's conquest of the Hejaz (Second Hejaz–Nejd War, Aug 1924 – Dec 1925), his allied Ikhwan (under Sultan bin Bajad and Khalid bin Luwai) took Taif after Hashemite defenders fled, then massacred ~300–400 residents. The slaughter broke Hashemite morale; Mecca, Medina, Jeddah fell within 16 months.
- Toll: 300–400 at Taif specifically (sources converge; disputed=false). The wider conquest's civilian toll is not well quantified, so I scoped the toll to Taif.
- Agent named plainly: the Ikhwan, fighting for Ibn Saud — no passive voice.
- Status RESEARCHED.
- Sources: Wikipedia (Taif massacre; Saudi conquest of Hejaz).

### sau-4 — Saudi-Led Coalition War in Yemen (2015–present), tier STR
- The strongest perpetrator entry by far. Since 26 March 2015 the Saudi-led coalition (Saudi command + UAE et al.; armed/supported by US, UK, France) has run an air war + blockade in Yemen. Documented strikes on funerals (Oct 2016 Sanaa funeral hall, ~140 killed/600 wounded), markets, hospitals, and the Aug 2018 Dahyan school-bus bombing. War + blockade drove famine and the world's worst cholera outbreak.
- **Toll attribution handled carefully (this is the integrity-critical part):** the 377,000 is the toll of the WHOLE war (all parties, incl. indirect deaths), NOT Saudi-caused deaths alone. I disclosed this in toll.note and the UNDP source caveat.
  - ~150,000 direct combat deaths, all parties, by 2019 (ACLED / Yemen Data Project) → used as **low**.
  - ~377,000 total by end-2021 (UNDP), ~60% (~227,000) indirect, mostly children under five → used as **high**.
  - Coalition airstrikes specifically: ~8,672 civilians (Yemen Data Project) to ~12,907 (LCRD — Houthi-aligned, caveated as interested party). Carried in prose/summary, not the headline low/high.
- disputed=true (total figure and apportionment both contested).
- Status RESEARCHED.
- Sources: Al Jazeera (23 Nov 2021) reporting UNDP; UNDP "Assessing the Impact of War in Yemen" (2021); Wikipedia (Saudi-led intervention in the Yemeni civil war); Yemen Data Project.

## Audit

Adversarial source-integrity + tier re-audit performed 2026-06-18. Premise: assume every citation is fake until proven otherwise. Every source URL was fetched/searched live and every toll cross-checked. Result: country **UNVERIFIED** (2 of 4 events fail VERIFIED).

### Source integrity — what was falsified vs. confirmed
- **MISATTRIBUTED AUTHOR (sau-2) — the one real integrity failure.** The scholarly source for the Qatif uprising was credited to **Toby Matthiesen**. The article at that exact URL/DOI ("Rebellion on the Saudi Periphery: Modernity, Marginalization, and the Shi'a Uprising of 1979," IJMES vol. 38 no. 2, May 2006, pp. 213–233) is in fact by **Toby Craig Jones**. Confirmed against Cambridge Core, Rutgers, Semantic Scholar, and Wikipedia. Matthiesen is a real, separate Saudi-Shia scholar — cited *within* Jones's article — but did not write it. The work is real and supports the summary; only the author was wrong. **Corrected the author in place (no source invented)** and quarantine-noted it. Because the original citation was inaccurate, sau-2 is set **UNVERIFIED** pending one clean re-confirmation pass.
- **All other sources verified real and on-point:**
  - sau-1: Away-from-the-Western-Front (Arab Revolt) — exact "up to 500,000 deaths estimated by 1918" text confirmed, and confirmed to be Syria-wide NOT Hejaz (caveat accurate). Wikipedia "Siege of Medina" — dates, ~12,000 garrison, looting confirmed, NO civilian toll. 1914-1918-Online "Medina, Siege of" — real peer-reviewed article, ~75,000 civilians, shortages/locusts/disease, NO quantified toll. All three independently confirm the *absence* of a Hejaz death figure.
  - sau-3: Wikipedia "Taif massacre" — ~300–400 killed, Ikhwan under Sultan bin Bajad & Khalid bin Luwai, first major standoff of the Second Hashemite–Saudi War — all confirmed.
  - sau-4: Al Jazeera (377,000 / ~60% indirect / ~70% children under five) confirmed. UNDP study direct fetch returned HTTP 403 (anti-bot block, not a dead link); existence + 377,000 figure independently confirmed via web search (UNDP/Pardee, Nov 2021). Wikipedia "Saudi-led intervention" — 8,672 (YDP) / 12,907 (LCRD) / Oct 2016 Sanaa funeral hall (140 killed, 600 wounded) all exact-match confirmed. Yemen Data Project — real, 25,054 coalition air raids, independent monitor.
