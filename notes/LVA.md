# Latvia (LVA) — research notes

Built from scratch 2026-06-19. Status: RESEARCHED (one event UNVERIFIED on toll precision).

## Structure
- **Victim ledger (2):** the Holocaust in Latvia (EXT); Soviet mass deportations (POL).
- **Perpetrator ledger (1):** Latvian collaboration in the Holocaust via the Arājs Kommando / Latvian Auxiliary Police (EXT).

The defining feature of Latvia's ledger is that one event — the Holocaust on Latvian soil — sits on BOTH sides: Latvia's Jews were the victims; a minority of Latvians were among the perpetrators. The country note makes the German-ordered / Latvian-staffed distinction explicit rather than flattening it.

## What is solid
- **Prewar Jewish population ~93,000–94,000.** USHMM gives ~94,000 (1935 census). Consistent across sources.
- **~70,000 Latvian Jews murdered (~three-quarters of the community).** USHMM + Wikipedia "The Holocaust in Latvia" agree. Used 60,000–70,000 range (low accounts for several thousand who fled east before the German advance).
- **Rumbula: ~25,000 shot on 30 Nov + 8 Dec 1941.** USHMM "Riga" page, well-established.
- **Foreign Jews deported into Riga ghetto: ~19,000.** Adds to a ~85,000–90,000 total-murdered-in-Latvia figure (noted, not used as the headline toll).
- **Arājs Kommando ~26,000 killed directly.** Wikipedia + the killing-share scholarship (Plavnieks 2018, Palgrave; ultimately Ezergailis / Angrick & Klein). Unit size ~300–500 in killing phase, up to ~1,500 in anti-partisan phase. Belarus killings are real but uncounted, so the high end is left as "26,000+".
- **Operation Priboi (25 Mar 1949): 41,708 deported from Latvia** (14,173 families); 90,844 Baltic total; 4,123 recorded deaths across all three republics by end-1950. Wikipedia "Operation Priboi" (from opened NKVD/MGB archives).
- **14 June 1941 deportation: ~15,400–16,000 residents**, incl. ~2,400 children under ten; Latvian sources state ~4 in 10 died in exile. LSM (Latvian Public Broadcasting).

## What is contested / could NOT be cleanly verified
- **Soviet-deportation DEATH toll.** This is the weakest number and the reason lva-2 is marked UNVERIFIED. Deportation COUNTS are archival and solid; deaths are estimates. The "four in ten died" figure for 1941 is a Latvian commemorative statement, not an audited register count; the 4,123 Priboi deaths figure is Baltic-wide, not Latvia-specific. I gave a deliberately wide ~4,000–9,000 range and flagged it. A single sourceable Latvia-specific death total was not found.
- **Holocaust toll low bound.** The ~60,000 low is a reasoned floor (subtracting those who escaped east) rather than a directly cited number; ~70,000 is the well-sourced figure. Range + note rather than a false single figure.
- **Arājs Kommando high bound.** Belarus/Russia killings are documented in kind but not in a clean total, so "26,000+" with the caveat instead of an invented higher number.

## Considered and excluded
- **Livonian Crusade (13th c.)** — real conquest and forced Christianisation of the native Baltic tribes (Livonians, Latgalians, Curonians, Semigallians), but no quantifiable death toll meeting the atlas's tier requirements. Folded into the country note, not made an event with a fabricated number.
- **No independent-Latvian-state atrocity against another people** was found; the perpetrator entry is correctly framed as collaboration within Germany's genocide.

- **USHMM 'Riga'** (lva-1 toll.low) — REAL. Confirms "at least 25,000 Jews from the Riga ghetto were shot... 30 November and 8–9 December 1941 by German SS and police units and their Latvian auxiliaries" at Rumbula; ~30,000 sealed in ghetto Oct 1941. Supports its slot. PASS.
- **Wikipedia 'The Holocaust in Latvia'** (lva-1 toll.high) — REAL. ~70,000 killed (~three-quarters of ~93,000), ~19,000 German/Austrian/Czech Jews. Underlying scholarship correctly named (Ezergailis, *The Missing Center* 1996; Press, *The Murder of the Jews in Latvia* 2000). Caveat discloses tertiary-aggregator status. PASS.
- **Wikipedia 'Arajs Kommando'** (lva-3 toll.low) — REAL. Founded July 1941 by Viktors Arājs (contact w/ Stahlecker); ~26,000 killed; ~300–500 in killing phase, up to ~1,500 in anti-partisan phase; Belarus/Russia ops incl. Winterzauber (~4,000). Cites Angrick & Klein 2009, Ezergailis. PASS.

- **LSM 'June 1941 mass deportations remembered across Latvia'** (lva-2 toll.low) — REAL.
