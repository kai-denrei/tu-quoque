# Mozambique (MOZ) — research log

Researched 2026-06-18 from scratch (no prior seed). Web tools (WebSearch/WebFetch) loaded and used throughout. Status: **RESEARCHED** (one event UNVERIFIED on toll grounds — see below).

## Ledger structure

**Done to them (victim) — 3 events**
- **moz-1 Wiriyamu Massacre (1972, colonial, EXT)** — Portuguese 6th Company of Commandos + PIDE/DGS, "Operation Marosca." Toll 150 (Red Cross) to 300 (Expresso). All civilians. Denied at the time, exposed July 1973 by Adrian Hastings; fed into the 1974 collapse of Portugal's Estado Novo. Portugal's PM acknowledged it only in 2022.
- **moz-2 Mozambican Civil War + famine (1977-1992, modern, WAR)** — ~1,000,000 dead (combat + atrocity + war-driven famine, incl. 1984), ~5 million displaced. Gersony Report (1988): refugees attributed ~94% of murders to RENAMO; Mamdani's ~100,000-killed-by-RENAMO is an extrapolation some dispute. Homoine massacre (1987): 424 civilians. RENAMO was built/armed by Rhodesia then apartheid South Africa — so for Mozambicans this is overwhelmingly a victim event.
- **moz-3 Cabo Delgado jihadist insurgency (2017-present, contemporary, WAR)** — Ansar al-Sunna / Islamic State – Mozambique Province. ~2,600-2,760 civilians killed of ~6,250-6,570 total (ACLED-derived); ~700k-850k displaced. Ongoing into 2025-2026.

**In their name (perpetrator) — 2 events**
- **moz-4 FRELIMO re-education camps + socialist repression (1974-1990, modern, POL)** — UNVERIFIED on toll. Machava estimates "over a thousand" inmate deaths and says firm figures cannot be established. 1980: ~10,000 detainees (~3,000 political). Operation Production (1983-88): ~50,000 deported. Public firing-squad executions under Law 2/79. **The "30,000 inmates died" figure on Wikipedia is NOT carried as fact** — it contradicts the leading scholar.
- **moz-5 Mozambican security-force war crimes in Cabo Delgado (2017-2021, contemporary, WAR)** — Amnesty 2021 "What I Saw Is Death": FADM extrajudicial executions, torture, rape, mutilation; Awasse video (woman shot 36 times, lab-verified); men shot into a mass grave at Quissanga. No comprehensive toll → low/high null but status RESEARCHED (the crimes are well-documented case-by-case).

## What is contested / disputed
- **Re-education camp death toll.** The single most important caution. Wikipedia's civil-war article says "30,000 inmates died"; Benedito Luís Machava (the authority on these camps) estimates "over a thousand" and stresses reliable figures are unobtainable. I followed Machava and flagged the higher figure as unverified/disputed. moz-4 toll left null/null, event UNVERIFIED.
- **RENAMO ~100,000 killed (Gersony).** Mamdani cites the Gersony Report for up to 100,000 civilians murdered by RENAMO; critics (incl. contemporary commentary) call it a worst-case extrapolation from ~80 interviews. Flagged in the toll note; not asserted as a hard number.
- **Civil war ~1,000,000 total.** Standard but soft — mixes combat deaths and war-aggravated famine; not a body count. Entered as low=high=1,000,000 with the mixed-estimate caveat.
- **Wiriyamu toll** 150 vs 300 — separately sourced (Red Cross low, Expresso high), disputed=true.
- **Cabo Delgado counts** are ACLED event-data, with inherent undercount; multiple slightly divergent totals (6,257 / 6,418 / 6,570) reconciled in the note.

## What could NOT be verified
- Any precise death count for the FRELIMO re-education camps (deliberately left null; the 30,000 figure rejected).
- Any aggregate count of civilians killed specifically by Mozambican security forces in Cabo Delgado (documented case-by-case only).
- The original Gersony Report document was not read directly — consulted via secondary descriptions (Wikipedia, Heritage Foundation 1988 commentary).
- The Dhada (Civil Wars 2012) and Machava monograph were consulted via abstract/publisher pages, not full text; cited for framing and the "over a thousand" estimate respectively, not for tolls I attributed elsewhere.

## Asymmetry note
Victim ledger (3) is heavier than perpetrator (2), and that is honest, not a gap: Mozambique's worst mass deaths were inflicted ON its people (Portuguese colonialism, a foreign-sponsored insurgency, jihadists). The perpetrator record is real but more about state political repression (camps) and counter-insurgency war crimes than mass killing campaigns by the state. No event, toll, or source was invented to balance the two. The Cabo Delgado pairing (moz-3 victim / moz-5 perpetrator) is the cleanest single dual-lens case: same province, same years, the state both victim of and perpetrator of atrocity.

## Audit

Adversarial source-integrity + tier audit, 2026-06-18 (assume-fake posture; every citation attacked). Country status: **UNVERIFIED** (3 of 5 events VERIFIED; moz-1 and moz-4 UNVERIFIED).

### Per-event verdict

- **moz-1 Wiriyamu — UNVERIFIED (source integrity).** Toll is solid: Red Cross ~150 and Expresso ~300 both independently confirmed (Wikipedia primary, plus secondary corroboration of the 300 figure). Date, perpetrators (Portuguese 6th Co. of Commandos + PIDE/DGS, "Operation Marosca"), all-civilian victims, Hastings exposure (1973), and Costa's 2022 acknowledgement all verified. **Failure found:** the scholarly source was MISATTRIBUTED. "Cutting Heads or Winning Hearts: Late Colonial Portuguese Counterinsurgency and the Wiriyamu Massacre of 1972" (Civil Wars 14:1, 2012) is by **Bruno C. Reis and Pedro A. Oliveira, not Mustafah Dhada** — and that paper is the *skeptical* one disputing whether the massacre as reported happened, i.e. the inverse of the "academic reconstruction" framing it was cited for. Dhada is a real, authoritative Wiriyamu scholar (2016 Martin A. Klein Prize), but his contribution is the *rebuttal* "The Wiriyamu Massacre of 1972: Response to Reis and Oliveira" (Civil Wars 15:4, 2013) plus his monographs. **Fix applied:** citation replaced with Dhada's genuine works (url updated to the 15:4 response); quarantine note added to toll.note; status set UNVERIFIED pending full-text re-verification of the corrected source. (The original tandfonline 12.2012 URL returned 403, but non-existence was disproven another way — the article exists under different authorship.)

- **moz-2 Civil War + famine — VERIFIED.** Wikipedia confirms 30 May 1977–4 Oct 1992, >1,000,000 dead incl. famine, ~5M displaced, RENAMO backed by Rhodesia then South Africa, Homoine 424, Gersony 94%/94%/93%. **Gersony Report independently verified** as a real 43-page 1988 US State Dept report (196 refugee interviews; FOIA/CIA reading-room copy exists); Mamdani's up-to-100,000 estimate and the small-sample dispute (Heritage Foundation 1988 commentary) both real and properly caveated. BlackPast page verified (real, ~1M / ~5M / external sponsorship). The Gersony source's url points at Wikipedia, which the caveat already discloses; the underlying claims check out, so this is acceptable, not fatal.

- **moz-3 Cabo Delgado insurgency — VERIFIED.** Wikipedia confirms 5 Oct 2017 start, total 6,570 incl. 2,759 civilians, Ansar al-Sunna / ISMP. Club of Mozambique ACLED article verified (6,257 total / at least 2,631 civilians, per ACLED's Peter Bofin, Oct 2025). HRW "Five Years On" (24 Nov 2022) verified. Both toll endpoints sourced (low via ACLED/Club of Mozambique, high via Wikipedia). Toll not disputed; not single-source.

- **moz-4 FRELIMO re-education camps — UNVERIFIED (toll, not source).** **All three sources passed integrity checks:** Machava's *The Morality of Revolution* (Ohio University Press, correct author/title/publisher) is real and authoritative; his "**over a thousand** inmate deaths may have occurred, reliable figures cannot be established" estimate is independently confirmed and is exactly what the record claims; his JAH article corroborates ~100,000 detainees passing through the system and cites Jay Ross (the WaPo correspondent) for ~10,000 detainees in 1980. mozambiquehistory.net Punishment dossier verified (Law 2/79 March 1979, public firing-squad executions Apr 1983, starvation deaths in prisons). Washington Post 7 May 1980 article verified as real (the ~10,000-detainee figure traces to its author Jay Ross). The "30,000 inmates died" figure does appear on Wikipedia's civil-war article and the record correctly REJECTS it in favour of Machava — good adversarial discipline. **Why still UNVERIFIED:** the lethal toll has no sourced numeric range (low/high null), so it cannot satisfy the both-ends-sourced requirement; the event remains honestly UNVERIFIED on toll grounds. No invented numbers — correct call retained.

- **moz-5 Security-force war crimes (Cabo Delgado) — VERIFIED.** Amnesty "What I Saw Is Death" (AFR 41/3545/2021, March 2021) verified real, correct index/title, documents all-parties abuses incl. Dyck Advisory Group (caveat accurate). Accompanying 2 March 2021 news release real. Cabo Delgado Wikipedia corroborates the 15 executed (13 Jul 2021) and the ~40-civilian refugee-boat sinking (13 Aug 2020). Null/null toll is law-1-compliant: case-by-case documentation, no comprehensive count, no invented aggregate. Sources all verified → VERIFIED.
