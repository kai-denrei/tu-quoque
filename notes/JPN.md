# JPN — Japan research log

Researched 2026-06-18. Web tools (WebSearch/WebFetch) ran successfully; all figures below trace to real, cited published sources.

## What changed from the seed

The seed had two events: one victim (atomic + firebombing) and one perpetrator bundling Nanjing, Unit 731 and comfort women under a single EXT tier and a single "Nanjing ~40,000-300,000" toll. That bundle hid three different acts with three different tiers and tolls. I split the perpetrator side into three events so each carries an accurate tier and a sourced range, and kept the victim event as one bundle (atomic + Tokyo firebombing) with both components separately sourced.

Both ledgers are present and full: 1 victim event, 3 perpetrator events.

## Events and tolls

- **jpn-1 — Atomic bombings + Tokyo firebombing (victim, WAR).** Tokyo 9-10 March 1945: ~80,000-100,000 (US Strategic Bombing Survey ~88,000; Tokyo police 83,793; Tokyo Fire Dept ~97,000; Rhodes 100,000+). Atomic, end-of-1945: low ~110,000 (1940s US Manhattan Project/Joint Commission), high ~210,000 (1970s Japanese-led International Investigation Team). Combined display ~290,000-400,000. Tier WAR, not EXT: city-bombing as a method of war-fighting, not a campaign to destroy the Japanese as a group. Lens: memorial.

- **jpn-2 — Nanjing Massacre (perpetrator, WAR).** Low ~40,000 (Ikuhiko Hata, ~38,000-42,000); high 300,000 (IMTFE / Chinese government). Mainstream cluster 50,000-300,000; IMTFE found "more than 200,000." Tier WAR, not EXT: mass atrocity against a captured city's population, not a program to exterminate an ethnic group as such. Lens: contested (textbook screening, Tsukurukai denial, plus Japanese historians who documented it).

- **jpn-3 — Unit 731 (perpetrator, WAR).** Pingfang facility deaths ~3,000 (low); field biological warfare "over 200,000" (Sheldon H. Harris); 2002 Changde symposium ~580,000 combined. Display ~3,000 (facility) / ~200,000-300,000+ (field). Tier WAR (field BW shades toward EXT in indiscriminate intent). Lens: suppressed — US immunity deal for Ishii's data; long kept out of public reckoning.

- **jpn-4 — Comfort women (perpetrator, POL).** Toll here is women enslaved, NOT deaths (death counts unrecoverable): low <20,000 (Hata, minimizing), mainstream ~200,000, some 200,000-400,000 (Qiu Peipei). Tier POL: state-organized enslavement/persecution (crime against humanity), not a primarily lethal extermination. Lens: denied — 1993 Kono Statement acknowledged coercion, later officials (Abe) insisted no document proves forcible taking.

## What is contested / could not be cleanly verified

- Every toll is a genuine range with a real source for each end. None had to be set null.
- Nanjing and comfort-women numbers are politically charged: the low ends (Hata) travel with denial of the coerced/atrocity character and are flagged with caveats in the source records, used as the contested low end, not as neutral counts.
- Unit 731's spread (3,000 facility deaths to ~580,000 with field operations) is the widest; no facility survivors are known and the unit destroyed evidence before surrender, so precision is impossible.
- jpn-4 toll unit is "women enslaved" not "deaths" — flagged explicitly in the toll note so it is never read as a death count.

## Tier reasoning note

The seed tiered the whole perpetrator bundle EXT. I downgraded each to WAR/POL deliberately, per the project rule that EXT = destruction of a group *as such*. Nanjing and Unit 731 were mass killing of a conquered/enemy population in war (WAR); the comfort-women system was state-run enslavement (POL). None fit the genocidal-intent test for EXT. The atomic/firebombing event is WAR (mass civilian death as a method of war-fighting), explicitly not EXT and not STR (no disease-driven demographic collapse involved here).
