## Why this is a clean dual-lens case
Cyprus is one of the strongest both-hands files in the atlas because the same conflict ran in both directions:
- Greek Cypriots = victims in 1974 (Turkish invasion: thousands dead, ~140k-190k displaced, 1,510 still missing).
- Greek Cypriot forces = perpetrators against Turkish Cypriots in 1963-64 (hundreds killed, ~25k-30k driven into enclaves) and again in Aug 1974 (EOKA-B massacres of ~210 Turkish Cypriot civilians).
The asymmetry is unusual: by raw deaths/displacement Greek Cypriots suffered more in 1974, but Turkish Cypriots suffered proportionally heavier losses in 1963-64 and were the targets of the worst single-village massacres. The file deliberately holds both.

## Key figures and where they came from
- **1974 deaths:** ~4,500-6,000 Greek Cypriot/Greek (military + civilian); sub-figures 309 Cyprus military, 105 Greece military. Source: Wikipedia "Turkish invasion of Cyprus," cross-referencing Clodfelter and US Library of Congress Country Study.
- **Missing persons:** ICMP/CMP — 2,002 total (1,510 Greek Cypriots, 492 Turkish Cypriots) across the 1960s and 1974; original 1974 list ~1,619. Authoritative, UN-backed. Source: icmp.int.
- **1976 European Commission of Human Rights (Cyprus v. Turkey):** found Turkey responsible for killings, systematic rape (inhuman treatment), and preventing ~190,000 displaced Greek Cypriots from returning. Adopted 10 July 1976, leaked 1977, declassified 1979.
- **1963-64 deaths:** 364 Turkish Cypriots, 174 Greek Cypriots (official records). ~25,000-30,000 Turkish Cypriots displaced into enclaves from 100+ villages.
- **Aug 1974 massacres:** Tochni/Zygi 84 (aged 13-74, executed at Palodia, one survivor Suat Hussein Kafadar); Maratha/Santalaris/Aloda 126 (aged 16 days to 95). Both 14 Aug 1974, both EOKA-B. Tolls well documented, not seriously contested.

## What is contested
- **The whole 1963-64 episode is a propaganda battlefield.** Turkish-state / TRNC sources (Daily Sabah, TRT, Turkiye Today, Hurriyet) frame it as a "genocidal" anti-Turkish campaign ("Kanli Noel," Museum of Barbarism) and push higher counts; Greek Cypriot official historiography long minimized the killings and called it a "Turkish mutiny." Scholars cited within the Wikipedia article (Niyazi Kizilyurek, Olga Demetriou) critique both the Greek Cypriot denialism and the Turkish maximalism. I used the 364/174 official figures and flagged the dispute rather than adopting either side's framing.
- **Specific 1963-64 incidents disputed:** the Nicosia General Hospital deaths — Turkish sources claim 21 patients murdered; a Greek Cypriot investigation found 3, attributing 2 to "a lone psychopath." Not used as a load-bearing number.
- **1974 military vs civilian split** is uncertain; ranges only.
- **Missing-persons numbers** are politicized on both sides but the ICMP/CMP figures are the neutral UN-backed standard and were used as such.

## What could NOT be verified / gaps
- No precise standalone civilian-only death count for 1974; the 4,500-6,000 range bundles military and civilian. Left as a sourced range with a note.
- Britannica was not fetched for Cyprus (Wikipedia + ICMP + the EComHR report covered the load-bearing claims); if a future audit wants a second independent toll for 1974, Clodfelter (Warfare and Armed Conflicts) is the cited scholarly origin and would be the place to go.
- A note source one rung up from Wikipedia for the Aug 1974 massacres would strengthen cyp-3 (currently two reference-tier Wikipedia entries, but the underlying AP reporting and survivor testimony are cited within them and the tolls are uncontested).

- **cyp-1 / Wikipedia "Turkish invasion of Cyprus" (supports toll.high):** VERIFIED. Turkey 1976 (supports summary):** VERIFIED as a genuine document despite the cited host (hri.org / Hellenic Resources Network) being UNREACHABLE at audit time (repeated 60s timeouts). Authenticity + substance independently corroborated by Council of Europe HUDOC (lists "Report Nos. 6780/74; 6950/75, ECommHR, 10 July 1976" — matches adoption date), lawpluralism.unimib.it, Columbia Global Freedom of Expression, and the ICRC casebook: large-scale killings of Greek Cypriot civilians (Art. 2), rape as inhuman treatment (Art. 3), prevention of displaced return (Art. 8). PRECISION FLAG (non-fatal): the 1976 report anchored "~170,000" displaced; the file's "~190,000" is high-end of the contested range (later proceedings established 211,000). Sourced but slightly elevated vs the 1976 document — noted in caveat, not quarantined.
- **cyp-1 / ICMP (supports toll.low):** VERIFIED verbatim — 2,002 missing (1,510 Greek Cypriot, 492 Turkish Cypriot). MAPPING FLAG (non-fatal): this source authoritatively backs the missing-persons figure, not the 4,500 death-toll low; the death-toll low is separately Clodfelter-anchored, so the toll is not single-sourced. Caveat updated to record the correct mapping.
- **cyp-2 / Wikipedia "Bloody Christmas (1963)" (supports toll.high):** VERIFIED — 364 Turkish Cypriots / 174 Greek Cypriots killed; ~25,000 displaced from 104 villages; Kumsal killing of Major Nihat İlhan's family; Ayios Vasilios mass grave of 21; Nikos Sampson / Vassos Lyssarides as paramilitary leaders; scholars Kızılyürek and Demetriou cited. All confirmed.
- **cyp-2 / Wikipedia "Civilian casualties and displacements" (supports summary):** VERIFIED — 364/174 killed, 209/41 missing, ~25,000 displaced, attributed to Pierre Oberling. Toll.low (174, contextual Greek Cypriot dead) confirmed on both Wikipedia sources. Double-anchored.
- **cyp-2 / ICMP (supports toll.note):** VERIFIED — 492 Turkish Cypriot missing.
- **cyp-3 / Wikipedia "Maratha, Santalaris and Aloda massacre" (supports toll.high):** VERIFIED — 126 killed, ages 16 days-95, EOKA-B, men arrested 20 Jul, AP-corroborated mass graves.
- **cyp-3 / Wikipedia "Tochni massacre" (supports toll.low):** VERIFIED — 84 (78 Tochni + 6 Zygi), executed at Palodia, sole survivor Suat Hussein Kafadar, 14 Aug 1974, EOKA-B.
