## Tolls — low and high both sourced

- **Great Leap famine: ~23–55M.** Low end from demographic/census reconstructions (Banister ~30M, lower demographic readings ~23M). High end from archival reconstructions (Dikötter "at least 45M", Yu Xiguang ~55M). Yang Jisheng's 36M sits in between. A PRC-aligned cluster (Sun Jingxian, Yang Songli) argues only 3.5–4M — recorded in the toll note as a denialist outlier, deliberately NOT used as the headline low.
- **Cultural Revolution: ~1.1–2M deaths, ~22–36M persecuted.** Walder & Su Yang (China Quarterly 2003): 750k–1.5M killed in the countryside, ~36M persecuted. Walder's national reconstruction: 1.1–1.6M deaths, 22–30M persecuted. Rummel's ~7.7M flagged as a weak-data outlier.
- **Dzungar genocide: ~420k–480k** (70–80% of ~600,000, per Perdue & Clarke). Wei Yuan's contemporary breakdown (40% smallpox / 30% killed / 20% fled) recorded honestly in the note — significant disease share, but intent-driven EXT tiering stands.
- **Nanjing Massacre: ~100k–300k.** Low from academic estimates (Kasahara). High = Nanjing Tribunal / Memorial Hall 300,000 (also IMTFE 200,000+). Japanese revisionist denial noted as denial, not a credible low.
- **Century / Sino-Japanese War: ~14–20M** for 1937–45 (academic 15–20M; PRC official 20M). Opium Wars themselves only tens of thousands of Chinese dead — their weight is the treaties, not the body count.

## Received lens (China's own memory)

- Great Leap famine — **suppressed** ("Three Years of Natural Disasters / Difficulty"; Tombstone banned; reduced to a few textbook lines).
- Cultural Revolution — **contested** (officially the "ten years of chaos", CCP 1981 resolution concedes catastrophe but limits open discussion).
- Dzungar genocide — **denied** (official "pingding"/unification narrative; depopulation attributed to disease; genocide label avoided).
- Nanjing Massacre — **memorial** (national Memorial Day since 2014; Memorial Hall since 1985).
- Opium Wars / Century — **memorial** (taught as the foundational national wound).

## Contested / caveated

- Famine and Cultural Revolution figures both turn on method (demographic vs archival); ranges shown, outliers flagged in both directions.
- Dzungar EXT tiering is the most consequential judgment call: a large share of deaths were smallpox, but the killing was explicitly ordered to end the Dzungars as a people, so the ACT is EXT on intent. The disease share is stated plainly in the toll note so the tier cannot be read as inflating a death count.
- Nanjing 300,000 is both a defensible tribunal finding and a politically loaded number; both flagged.

- NPR "A Grim Chronicle of China's Great Famine" (2012) confirmed real at the cited URL; about Yang Jisheng / Tombstone; 36M starvation deaths; Yang a former Xinhua journalist (1968–2001); book banned in mainland China — caveat accurate. ### chn-2 Cultural Revolution (POL, perpetrator) — VERIFIED
- Walder & Su Yang, China Quarterly (2003): real. Full title "The Cultural Revolution in the Countryside: Scope, Timing and Human Impact" (JSON uses a fair short form). Figures confirmed exactly: 750k–1.5M killed, ~36M persecuted, from 1,520 county annals. supports toll.low — correct.
- Walder national reconstruction (1.1–1.6M deaths, 22–30M persecuted, ~2,200 city/local annals) confirmed real via the Stanford Report (news.stanford.edu/stories/2019/10). FLAG (minor): source #2's url points to the Wikipedia Cultural Revolution page, which does NOT carry Walder's national figures; the actual source is the Stanford report (which IS the url of source #1). Figure verified regardless — URL mis-pointing, not fabrication. Left as-is; cite names the underlying work honestly.
- Rummel ~7.7M (7,731,000) confirmed as the high outlier; correctly flagged as weak-data. Fairbank ~1M confirmed as the low anchor.
- CCP 1981 "Resolution on Certain Questions in the History of Our Party" / "ten years of chaos / havoc" verdict confirmed on Wikipedia; caveat (ruling-party self-assessment) present and apt.
- Tier: POL re-derived — organised state-directed political repression, deaths from violence not famine/disease. Correct. Both toll ends sourced, disputed:true.

### chn-3 Dzungar genocide (EXT, perpetrator) — VERIFIED
- This is the STR-vs-EXT pressure point. ~40% of deaths were smallpox (Wei Yuan). Tier re-derived: EXT is correct because the ACT carries documented intent to destroy the group as such — not because of the death count. Disease share disclosed honestly in toll.note.
- Perdue (China Marches West, 2005): 70–80% of ~600,000 = 420k–480k — confirmed on Wikipedia. supports toll.high — correct.
- Clarke, Xinjiang and China's Rise in Central Asia (Routledge 2011): real book; the phrase "complete destruction of... the Zunghars as a people" confirmed verbatim on Wikipedia, attributed to Clarke (note: closely echoes Millward 2007; Wikipedia credits Clarke). supports tier — correct.
- Wei Yuan (1794–1857) breakdown (40% smallpox / 30% killed by Manchu bannermen / 20% fled) and ~600,000 in 200,000 households — confirmed; primary kind with conqueror's-side caveat, apt. supports toll.low.
- Qianlong's explicit extermination orders ("Show no mercy", "massacre these crafty Zunghars") confirmed verbatim. Both toll ends sourced, disputed:true.

### chn-4 Nanjing Massacre (WAR, victim) — VERIFIED
- Kasahara, Nankin jiken / 南京事件 (1997): real book; his specific figure 160k–170k sits inside the JSON's "more than 100,000, close to 200,000 or more" paraphrase. supports toll.low — acceptable.
- Nanjing War Crimes Tribunal (1947) 300,000+, inscribed at the Memorial Hall (opened 15 Aug 1985): confirmed. supports toll.high; caveat (charged PRC-memory symbol) apt.
- IMTFE (Tokyo, 1948) "over 200,000": confirmed verbatim in the 4 Nov 1948 judgment ("the first six weeks... was over 200,000"). NOTE: the Wikipedia death-toll page also lists IMTFE's 155,000 buried-bodies subset; the headline judgment figure is genuinely "over 200,000," so the cite stands. supports summary.
- Memorial Day national since 2014 (12th NPC) confirmed.
- Tier: WAR re-derived — civilian-targeting violence inside warfare (post-capture atrocity during invasion), not a standing extermination programme. Correct. Both toll ends sourced, disputed:true.

### chn-5 Opium Wars & Century of Humiliation (WAR, victim) — VERIFIED
- Second Sino-Japanese War (1937–45): Wikipedia gives ~20M dead (mostly civilians) and a 14,000,000–22,000,000 table range (Clodfelter; Mitter 2013 for 12M+ civilians). JSON's 14–20M and PRC-official 20M are consistent and corroborated. Both toll ends sourced.
