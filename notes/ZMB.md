# Zambia (ZMB) — research log

Built from scratch on 2026-06-18. Web tools (WebSearch/WebFetch) loaded and used throughout. Status: RESEARCHED.

## Ledger summary

- **Victim (2 events):** WWI carrier deaths (Mtenga-Tenga); Rhodesian cross-border raids 1978–79 (Operation Gatling).
- **Perpetrator (1 event):** the 1964 Lumpa uprising — the one clear case of the Zambian state killing on a mass scale.

## What I found

### zmb-1 — Mtenga-Tenga / WWI carriers (victim, STR, colonial)
- Northern Rhodesia recruited 312,891 men and women as porters for Britain's East Africa campaign (JSAS 2023, Siegel et al., Mtenga-Tenga article).
- ~20,000 Zambians cited as dead of disease/debilitation (Zambia Tourism heritage site) — used for HIGH end.
- Colonial government's own contemporary estimate was a few hundred (~300) — a self-serving undercount; used for LOW end to show the dispute.
- Campaign-wide carrier deaths: "no fewer than 95,000," ~10% of manpower (1914-1918-Online encyclopedia); National Army Museum cites ~100,000 carriers/camp followers dead on both sides. Deaths from pneumonia, dysentery, malaria, exhaustion, 1918 flu — not combat. STR (disease-driven structural mortality).

### zmb-2 — Rhodesian raids / Operation Gatling (victim, WAR, modern)
- Operation Gatling 19–21 Oct 1978: Rhodesian air force ("Green Leader" Chris Dixon) seized Zambian airspace, bombed Freedom Camp/Westlands near Lusaka; SAS/RLI hit Mkushi and a Great North Road camp.
- Rhodesia claimed 1,500+ "ZIPRA cadres" killed — HIGH end; historians (Moorcraft & McLaughlin) say many were unarmed refugees and the count was inflated.
- ZIPRA figure: 351 killed at Freedom Camp (a refugee camp) — LOW end.
- Raids continued into 1979 (Operation Carpet, 26 June 1979), so cumulative toll exceeds any single op. UN Security Council Resolution 424 (March 1978) condemned earlier attacks.
- Cross-confirmed via WP contemporary coverage (Dec 1978).

### zmb-3 — Lumpa uprising (perpetrator, POL, modern)
- July–Oct 1964, on the eve of independence (24 Oct 1964). Alice Lenshina's Lumpa Church resisted UNIP authority; fortified villages.
- Kaunda (then PM) declared a state of emergency, sent in security forces.
- Toll: range across sources 700 to 1,500+; "about a thousand" / "more than 1,000" common; official often given 800–1,300. ~15,000 fled to Zaire.
- Many killed by government forces; senior UNIP figures reportedly demanded extermination even after Kaunda's no-retaliation declaration. POL.

## What is contested / could NOT be fully verified

- **WWI carrier toll for Northern Rhodesia specifically.** Big spread: colonial ~300 vs. commonly cited ~20,000; the precise national death count was never properly recorded. Bounded as a disputed range. The 312,891-recruited figure and campaign-wide ~95k–100k carrier deaths are solid.
- **JSAS Mtenga-Tenga full text was paywalled** (tandfonline full-text returned 403; BJMH PDF was binary/unreadable via WebFetch). Used the abstract/secondary summary for the recruitment figure and the colonial ~300 estimate. Flagged as caveat on the source.
- **Operation Gatling toll** has partisan provenance at both ends — 1,500 is the Rhodesian (perpetrator) claim; 351 is the ZIPRA (target) figure. Both bounded and caveated.
- **Lumpa toll** disagrees within single sources (Wikipedia itself gives 700, ~1,000, >1,000, up to 1,500). No single authoritative count.

## The asymmetry (explained in the country note, not papered over)

Zambia's perpetrator record is genuinely thin. Searches for state mass killing / ethnic violence returned: Zambia is repeatedly contrasted with Zimbabwe (no Gukurahundi), described as having avoided ethnic mass violence despite risk factors. The post-independence perpetrator record is **repression, not mass death**: detention without trial, torture of detainees after the 1997 coup attempt (peak 104 detainees, some tortured; Amnesty/HRW), lethal police force against opposition/protesters (Amnesty 2021, US State Dept reports), and the army's counterinsurgency vs. Adamson Mushala's guerrillas (a 1973 ambush killed ~100 guerrillas). None of these reach the mass-death scale this atlas tracks, so they are noted in the country `note` rather than entered as fabricated "events" to balance the ledgers. The 1964 Lumpa uprising is the single genuine perpetrator-side mass-killing event.

## Audit

### Source integrity (per source, falsification attempted)

**zmb-1 — Mtenga-Tenga / WWI carriers**
- *Zambia Tourism, "British Empire in Zambia" (toll.high, ~20,000):* VERIFIED. Exact sentence confirmed on the live page — "Some 20,000 Zambia[ns] forcibly recruited as porters for the British forces in East Africa during the First World War perished of disease or debilitation." Supports toll.high. Tourism/heritage provenance correctly caveated.
- *1914-1918-Online, "Carrier Corps" (summary, ~95,000):* VERIFIED. Article exists; exact wording confirmed — "no fewer than 95,000 died, or around 10 percent of the total manpower used"; deaths attributed to disease, exhaustion, inadequate medical care and the 1918 influenza, not combat. Supports summary.
- *JSAS Mtenga-Tenga article (toll.low, 312,891 recruited / ~300 colonial estimate):* WORK VERIFIED, CITATION CORRECTED. The article is real — "African Reactions to the First World War: The Case of the Mtenga-Tenga of Northern Rhodesia (Zambia)," Journal of Southern African Studies vol. 49(4), pp. 553–567 (2023), confirmed via RePEc and JSAS. **But the prior citation misattributed authorship to "B. Siegel et al." The article is single-authored by Mutale T. Mazimba.** Author corrected in the record; the misattribution is documented in the source caveat. The 312,891 recruitment figure is confirmed in the abstract; the ~300 colonial estimate is body-text (full text paywalled, tandfonline 403) and is cited as a colonial undercount, not a true toll.

**zmb-2 — Operation Gatling / Rhodesian raids**
- *Wikipedia, "Operation Gatling" (toll.high, 1,500 / toll.low context):* VERIFIED. Confirms date 19–21 Oct 1978, Rhodesian Security Forces, Rhodesian claim "over 1,500 ZIPRA cadres," ZIPRA "351 boys and girls" at Freedom Camp, "many of whom were in fact unarmed refugees," and Moorcraft & McLaughlin on the impossibility of distinguishing refugees from recruits and the exaggerated count. Both ends carry partisan provenance (perpetrator claim / target figure), correctly bounded and caveated.
- *Washington Post, "Rhodesian Raids on Zambia Fail to Deter Guerrilla Forces" (4 Dec 1978) (summary):* VERIFIED. Title, date and content confirmed via search (live fetch returns 403 anti-bot, not a dead link) — contemporary reporting on the Oct 1978 raids, Kaunda, Nkomo/People's Union (ZIPRA). Used for fact/timing of raids, not for a precise toll.

**zmb-3 — Lumpa uprising**
- *Wikipedia, "Lumpa Church" (toll.high, >1,000 / range 700–1,500):* VERIFIED. Confirms July–Oct 1964, "more than 1,000 people were killed" alongside the 700–1,500 range, state troops sent in by PM Kenneth Kaunda under a state of emergency, ~15,000 fled to Zaire. Internal range disagreement is real and is why the toll is a wide disputed band.
- *Wikipedia, "Alice Lenshina" (summary):* VERIFIED. Confirms climax "July to October in 1964, just before Northern Rhodesia's independence," dead drawn from Lumpa adherents / UNIP members / security forces, "700 lives" intro and "about a thousand" body figures, Kaunda as pre-independence PM.
