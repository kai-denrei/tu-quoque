# Brunei (BRN) — research notes

Built from scratch on 2026-06-19. Two ledgers populated, one event each. Both events carry **null tolls** and **status UNVERIFIED** — the underlying history is real and well attested qualitatively, but no reliable death/enslavement counts exist for either. Country status: UNVERIFIED.

## Victim — brn-1: Japanese occupation (1941–1945), tier WAR
- Imperial Japanese forces invaded Dec 1941 (~8 days after Pearl Harbor), overran Brunei in roughly a week against negligible defence.
- Kempeitai ran policing with near-unlimited power; routine torture. Malays executed as accused "British agents." Forced labour, "banana" currency, famine and epidemic disease so severe people reportedly wore tree bark. Floggings/imprisonment for defiance.
- Allied liberation June 1945 (Australian 9th Division, Brunei Bay landings 10 June) — pre-landing bombardment flattened Brunei Town; mosque levelled, reportedly only a Chinese temple left standing; thousands left homeless.
- **Could NOT verify:** any Brunei-specific civilian death toll. None exists in accessible literature — record is oral/qualitative. Allied military losses (~100+ killed in the landings) are documented but are combatant figures, not the civilian toll. Hence null + UNVERIFIED.

## Perpetrator — brn-2: sultanate slave-raiding & human-tribute system (c.1700s–1928), tier STR
- Centuries-long institution, not a single event → STR (structural).
- Brunei acquired slaves three ways: purchase from pirates/merchants; raids carried out by Bruneians themselves; human tribute extracted from non-Muslim indigenous chiefs (Bidayuh, Dayak, Murut, Bisaya...) who couldn't pay the sultanate's taxes. Enslaved used as servants, field/craft labour, harem concubines.
- Early-19c Brunei described as "a resort for pirates and a market for the slave trade"; British naval suppression of piracy from the 1840s (Keppel et al.) and later oil/rubber wage economy eroded it. Formally abolished by law 1928.
- **Could NOT verify:** any aggregate number enslaved or killed. None survives. Hence null + UNVERIFIED. The mechanism is solidly documented; the magnitude is not.

## Deliberately EXCLUDED (and why) — explained in country note
- **Sandakan–Ranau death marches (1945, ~2,400 Allied POWs killed):** occurred in North Borneo / Sabah, not Brunei; victims were POWs brought from Singapore, not Bruneians. Not a Brunei event.
- **1962 Brunei Revolt (~40 rebels killed):** suppressed chiefly by British/Commonwealth forces (Gurkhas, Royal Marines) against the anti-government TNKU / Parti Rakyat Brunei. Neither a clean Brunei-state-perpetrator nor a clean Bruneian-victim event. Noted as context only.
- **1846 Bombardment of Brunei Town:** British Royal Navy coercion (forced cession of Labuan) but almost no recorded deaths (Bruneian "1 wounded"); not a mass-death event. Context only.
- **Limbang Rebellion 1884 / Limbang annexation 1890:** tax revolt by Bisaya/Lun Bawang against Brunei, then Charles Brooke's annexation of Limbang. Conflict and grievance documented but no sourceable massacre toll; folded into the broader perpetrator picture rather than entered as its own event.

## Audit
Adversarial source-integrity + tier audit, 2026-06-19. Premise: treat every citation as fake until proven otherwise. Every source was independently re-fetched and an attempt made to falsify it.

**Source integrity — all five sources survived falsification (real works, supporting their claims):**
- brn-1 / Wikipedia "Japanese occupation of British Borneo" — REAL. Confirms Dec 1941 invasion (Brunei Town captured 22 Dec), Kempeitai with "virtually unlimited power" using "torture and brutality", abuse of Malays/Chinese/indigenous, and "the shop quarter and many Government buildings were completely destroyed." No BRN civilian toll — existing caveat accurate.
- brn-1 / IAS UBD Working Paper No. 25 — REAL **and now fully verified**. Confirmed title "Memories of World War II: Oral History of Brunei Darussalam (Dec. 1941–June 1945)", authors Frank Dhont, Janet E. Marles & Maslin Jukim, Gadong 2016, listed in the official IAS working-paper series. The prior note that the PDF "could not be machine-extracted" was overcautious: the full 31-page text extracts cleanly and **directly** supports the summary — Kempeitai torture, forced labour (120-mile oil pipeline, airfields), widespread starvation ("you starved"; >3000 Chinese/Javanese died of starvation in Miri), the tree-bark-clothing detail (bark of the Tamaran tree pounded into fabric), indiscriminate torture of those "reported by a spy", and Allied bombing of Bandar. **Corrected** the citation (full title/authors/year) and **rewrote the stale caveat** to reflect that the text is verified and substantiates the summary directly (still null on a death toll).
- brn-1 / amotimes "Brunei Standard" blog — REAL. Supports 3-year occupation, banana currency, flogging/imprisonment of defiers, famine/disease, 10 June 1945 Labuan/Brunei Bay landings. Popular/oral; non-authoritative-for-numbers caveat is correct.
- brn-2 / Wikipedia "Slavery in Brunei" — REAL. Verbatim supports all three acquisition methods (purchase from pirates/merchants; Bruneian slave raids; human tribute from non-Muslim chiefs who failed to pay tax), Bidayuh targeting, uses (agricultural/craft labour, concubinage, household servants), and 1928 legal abolition. No aggregate count — caveat accurate.
- brn-2 / 1911 Britannica "Brunei" (Wikisource) — REAL. Supports "a resort for pirates and a market for the slave trade" and suppression by "Admiral (then Captain) Keppel" in the 1840s. Colonial-bias caveat appropriately disclosed.

**Toll:** Both tolls are null at both ends. Confirmed across all sources that no Brunei-specific civilian death count (brn-1) and no aggregate enslaved/killed figure (brn-2) exists in the literature. This is a genuine evidentiary gap, not a single-source/single-number toll to flag — there is no number to be over-reliant on. The combatant figure in brn-1's note (~110+ killed) was independently verified (Oboe 6 / Battle of North Borneo: 110+ Australians killed; Labuan alone 34 killed, 93 wounded) and is correctly labelled as NOT the civilian toll. The "only a Chinese temple left standing / mosque levelled" liberation detail was cross-confirmed by independent Brunei-history sources.

**Tier (re-derived independently before reading the assigned values):**
- brn-1 = **WAR** — confirmed correct. Military conquest + occupation atrocity + combat destruction of the capital. The famine/disease here is an occupation/war byproduct, not a deliberate disease-as-method extermination, and it is not a campaign to destroy the Bruneian people as such — so neither EXT nor STR; WAR holds.
- brn-2 = **STR** — confirmed correct. A durable, centuries-long institution of coerced bondage and demographic predation (raiding, purchase, war-capture, human tribute), not a single bounded event (not WAR) and not a programme of physical annihilation (not EXT). Structural is right.

**Status outcome:** Both events remain **UNVERIFIED** — not for source failure (all sources are real and supporting) but because the tolls cannot be confirmed at low and high ends (they are null by genuine absence of evidence).
