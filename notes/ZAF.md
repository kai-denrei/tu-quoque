# ZAF — South Africa research log

Built from scratch on 2026-06-18. Both ledgers populated; 2 victim + 2 perpetrator events.

## Victim ("Done to them")

### zaf-1 — South African War concentration camps (1900–1902), STR
- Agent: British Army under Kitchener (scorched-earth + internment).
- Toll: ~27,927 Boer deaths (≈24,000 children under 16; ~1 in 4 inmates). Black African camp deaths far less documented — recorded minimum ~14,154, estimates to ~20,000, ~107,000 interned. Combined ~42,000–48,000.
- Contested element: the Black death count is genuinely uncertain because the British kept poor records of the Black camps. Boer figures are firmer.
- Sources: van Heyningen, History Compass 2009 (paywalled, DOI 10.1111/j.1478-0542.2008.00562.x); Wikipedia "Second Boer War concentration camps"; SAHO white-camps article. Status RESEARCHED.

### zaf-2 — Sharpeville massacre (1960), WAR
- Agent: South African Police (apartheid state).
- Toll: official/police 69 dead, ~180 wounded; revised scholarship ≥91 dead, ≥238–281 wounded.
- Contested element: the 69 figure is the perpetrator's own police count, treated as the low end; Clark & Worger (2023, "Voices of Sharpeville") raised it using hospital-bed witness statements. Used range 69–91.
- Sources: Wikipedia "Sharpeville massacre"; Clark & Worger via The Conversation summary; Britannica. Status RESEARCHED.

## Perpetrator ("In their name")

### zaf-3 — The Mfecane (c. 1810s–1840s), STR — FLAGGED UNVERIFIED
- Agent: Zulu kingdom under Shaka and successors, as ONE driver among several.
- Toll: NOT verifiable. Traditional 1–2 million is widely regarded as inflated colonial/apartheid propaganda ("empty land" myth). Revisionist scholarship (Cobbing debate, from 1988) puts mortality far lower — tens of thousands to hundreds of thousands — and stresses multi-causality (drought, slave/ivory trade, European frontier pressure).
- Decision: low = null (no reliable hard count); high = 2,000,000 recorded ONLY as the disputed traditional ceiling, NOT endorsed. Event status UNVERIFIED. Heavy caveating in toll.note and relevance to avoid laundering the colonial "Black-on-Black savagery" narrative into a settled atrocity figure.
- Sources: Wikipedia "Mfecane" (carries traditional figure as "controversial" + revisionist case); Britannica "Mfecane".

### zaf-4 — Apartheid: state killing at home + destabilisation abroad (1948–1994), POL
- Agent: apartheid SA state (security police, SADF, Vlakplaas death squads).
- Toll: TRC counted ~21,000 political deaths inside SA — ~7,000 (1948–1989) + ~14,000 (1990–1994 transition, inflamed by state "third force"). Headline number = internal deaths only; not all state-attributable (IFP, ANC also killed).
- Regional wars (RENAMO/Mozambique, UNITA/Angola, cross-border raids on front-line states) killed hundreds of thousands but those tolls belong to host conflicts and were DELIBERATELY NOT folded into the headline number — only noted in toll.note (Mozambique ~100,000+ killed / up to ~1M displaced; Angola ~500,000 dead across the conflict). Those host-country figures are illustrative context, not the ZAF event toll.
- Sources: Wikipedia "Truth and Reconciliation Commission (South Africa)"; SAHO "Vlakplaas"; Wikipedia "Apartheid". Status RESEARCHED.

## Could NOT verify / chose to exclude
- Precise Mfecane death toll — no reliable figure exists; left null + UNVERIFIED.
- A single clean state-attributable internal apartheid death toll separate from IFP/ANC violence — TRC's ~21,000 is a documented-deaths aggregate, not a state-only number; flagged in toll.note rather than overstated.
- Soweto uprising (1976) considered as a victim event but folded conceptually under apartheid-state repression; Sharpeville chosen as the iconic, best-documented internal massacre to keep the victim ledger tight.
- Regional-war tolls (Angola/Mozambique) are real but belong to those host countries' ledgers; kept as context only.
