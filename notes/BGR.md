# Bulgaria (BGR) — research notes

Built from scratch on 2026-06-19. Three events: 1 victim, 2 perpetrator.

## Events

### bgr-1 (victim) — Bulgarian Horrors / April Uprising 1876, incl. Batak — WAR
- Ottoman state crushed the 1876 rising using bashi-bazouk irregulars; mass civilian killings, Batak the emblem.
- Toll heavily contested by rival 1876 diplomatic agendas:
  - Baring (British Embassy) ~12,000
  - Schuyler (US consul) ~15,000
  - Later French/Russian consular + Bulgarian estimates: 25,000–40,000, ~30,000 commonly cited
  - Batak alone: 1,200–8,000, ~5,000 most cited
- Range entered 12,000–30,000, disputed=true. Status RESEARCHED.

### bgr-2 (perpetrator) — Deportation of Jews from occupied Thrace/Macedonia/Pirot 1943 — EXT
- Firm, undisputed figure: 11,343 deported to Treblinka (USHMM), almost all murdered.
  - ~7,144 from Vardar Macedonia (Skopje tobacco warehouse) — Yad Vashem
  - ~4,075 from Greek Thrace + E. Macedonia — Wikipedia (Holocaust in Bulgarian-occupied Greece)
  - small numbers from Pirot
- Organized by Belev's Commissariat for Jewish Affairs (KEV) + German adviser Dannecker.
- CRITICAL dual-lens point: same govt did NOT deport Bulgaria's own ~48,000 Jews (Peshev, Church, public protest). "Bulgaria saved its Jews" is true for citizens, false for the 11,343. Status RESEARCHED.

### bgr-3 (perpetrator) — Communist purge + People's Court after 9 Sept 1944 coup — POL
- Firm: People's Court tried ~11,122, condemned ~2,730 to death (Britannica).
- Extrajudicial toll BITTERLY contested: ~5,000 (conservative) up to 18,000 / 26,000 (both from "missing persons" inference) / 30,000.
- Entered as range 2,730 (judicial) to 30,000 (total), disputed=true, status UNVERIFIED because no agreed total exists and figures are politically charged.

## Contested / could not be verified
- 1876 toll: irreducibly disputed; left as a wide sourced range.
- 1944 purge total: no scholarly consensus; high-end figures rest on missing-persons inference, not body counts. Marked UNVERIFIED.
- ustrcr.cz "Crimes Committed by the Communist Regime in Bulgaria" PDF returned corrupted/unreadable on fetch — its specific figures (26,850 Sept–Oct 1944; 640 camp deaths 1945–62; Belene ~15,000 passed through) were seen only in search snippets, NOT independently confirmed, so they were NOT used as the toll source. Britannica + Wikipedia coup article used instead.

## Asymmetry note
Victim ledger anchored on the single most internationally documented case (1876) rather than padded with the many smaller Ottoman-era massacres of Bulgarians. Perpetrator ledger has two solid events. Both ledgers turn on the same Bulgarian nationalist state-building project.
