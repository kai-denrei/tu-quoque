# Eswatini (SWZ) — research log

Built from scratch (no prior seed). Dual-ledger entry: 2 victim events, 2 perpetrator events.

## What was found

### Victim ("Done to them")
1. **Mfecane attacks on the Ngwane/Swazi under Sobhuza I (c.1817–1839)** — STATUS: UNVERIFIED (toll).
   - The proto-Swazi (Ngwane, Dlamini clan) were attacked by Zwide's Ndwandwe c.1817–1820, driven north of the Phongolo/Usutu rivers, and later raided by the Zulu (joint Zulu/British attack noted in 1836). They survived and built the kingdom.
   - No Swazi-specific death toll exists. The region-wide Mfecane figure (1–2 million) is contested (Cobbing/Eldredge) and not apportionable. Toll = null. This is the founding-trauma entry, kept because the displacement is well attested even though the death metric is not.

2. **HIV/AIDS epidemic and demographic collapse (c.1990s–2010s)** — STATUS: RESEARCHED. STR tier.
   - The genuine modern mass-death event done to the Swazi. World's highest adult HIV prevalence (27.2%, 2016). Annual AIDS deaths ~2,400 (1995) rising to ~7,000 (2009), ~0.6% of population/year at peak. WHO (2002): 64% of all deaths in the country were HIV/AIDS.
   - Life expectancy collapse: ~60–61 yrs (1997–2000) → ~32 yrs (2009). One of the steepest peacetime drops on record.
   - Agent framed honestly as the virus (STR — disease-driven collapse), with state delay/stigma (national disaster declared only Feb 1999 by Mswati III) as an aggravating factor, NOT the cause of death. Toll given as annual-death range (2,400–7,000) with the demographic metric in the note.

### Perpetrator ("In their name")
3. **Swazi impi raids for cattle and captives under Mswati II (c.1840–1868)** — STATUS: UNVERIFIED (toll).
   - Swazi armies (reorganised on Zulu age-regiment lines) raided the Pedi, Pulana, Lobedu, Venda, Maseko/Mbayi, Gaza and others across the NE Transvaal, toward Delagoa Bay (Mozambique) and as far as southern Zimbabwe — for cattle and captives, recorded as plunder/slave raiding.
   - No casualty or captive count survives. The one documented set-piece, the Battle of Moholoholo (Dec 1864), was a Swazi DEFEAT (Mswati's brother Zimase annihilated by the MaPulana), so it does not supply a perpetrator death count. Toll = null. STR (sustained predatory raiding system).

4. **June 2021 killing of pro-democracy protesters** — STATUS: RESEARCHED. POL tier. Best-documented atrocity in either ledger.
   - King Mswati III's security forces — army (UEDF) + police (REPS) — fired live ammunition on protesters/bystanders during the pro-democracy uprising (protests from June 2021; protest ban from 25 June; UEDF deployed 1 July).
   - Toll contested: Eswatini Commission on Human Rights = 46 dead (2 children) + 245 gunshot injuries (Oct 2021); Amnesty = 70+; civil society/PUDEMO/activists = 80–100+. Government denied ("mercenaries"). Zero accountability.
   - Folded in: assassination of human rights lawyer Thulani Maseko, shot through his window at home 21 Jan 2023, hours after Mswati III warned pro-democracy campaigners (assassins unidentified; UN/AU experts feared targeted retaliation).

## What is contested
- 2021 massacre death toll: 46 (official commission) vs 70+ (Amnesty) vs 100+ (civil society). Range carried as 46–100, disputed=true.
- Mfecane region-wide toll (1–2 million): contested and not apportionable to the Swazi → null.
- HIV peak annual deaths: ~7,000 (2009) rests on WHO-derived Wikipedia figure; the full UNAIDS historical series / exact peak-year value was not directly visible on the Trading Economics page.

## What could NOT be verified
- Any Swazi-specific Mfecane death count. None exists.
- Casualty/captive numbers for the Mswati II raids. None in sources consulted.
- A single audited cumulative AIDS death total for the epidemic (stated qualitatively as "tens of thousands").
- Colonial land partition (1907–1909) and the 1903–1907 tax revolt are real (two-thirds of land to settlers; forced labour migration; ScienceDirect: "Colonial coercion and the Swazi tax revolt of 1903–1907") but are dispossession/coercion, NOT mass-death events — deliberately NOT given a death toll or a separate ledger entry. No famine or punitive-massacre death figures were found for the colonial period.
