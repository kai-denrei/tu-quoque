# Tunisia (TUN) — research notes

Built from scratch 2026-06-18 with live web research (WebSearch + WebFetch). No seed file existed. Four events: 2 victim, 2 perpetrator.

## What I built

**Victim ("Done to them")**
- **tun-1 — Nazi German occupation & forced labour of Tunisian Jews (1942–1943).** Tunisia was the ONLY Arab/North African country occupied by the Third Reich (Nov 1942 – May 1943). ~32–40 German labour/detention camps; ~5,000 Jewish men conscripted. Deaths from typhus, hunger, beatings, overwork and Allied bombing; isolated deportations to European camps (boxer Victor "Young" Perez via Drancy to Auschwitz, killed Jan 1945). SS Einsatzkommando under Walter Rauff present but Axis driven out before systematic extermination. Tier POL. **UNVERIFIED** — no single agreed death count.
- **tun-2 — French assault on Bizerte / Bizerte Crisis (1961).** France kept its Bizerte naval base after 1956 independence; Tunisia blockaded it July 1961; France replied with paratroopers, jets, armour, offshore cruisers. ~630 Tunisians killed, ~1,555 wounded; ~24 French dead. Tier WAR. RESEARCHED.

**Perpetrator ("In their name")**
- **tun-3 — Slavery run from the Regency/Beylik of Tunis (16th c.–1846).** Two systems: state-licensed Barbary corsairs out of Tunis seizing/ransoming Europeans; trans-Saharan trafficking of Black Africans (~100,000 into Tunisia 1700–1880, per Raëd Bader). Tunis a principal Barbary base. Ahmad I Bey abolished in stages (export ban 1841, Tunis slave market closed 1842, full abolition Jan 1846 — early for the Muslim world). Tier STR. Toll = null by nature (enslavement, not a dated killing). RESEARCHED on scale figures.
- **tun-4 — Lethal repression of the 2010–2011 uprising.** Ben Ali's police/security forces shot protesters. Tier POL. RESEARCHED. Range 129–338, dispute shown.

## What is contested / the disputes shown

- **2011 toll (tun-4):** GENUINE documentary dispute. 2012 Bouderbala preliminary commission = **338 killed**, 2,147 wounded (2,489 cases reviewed, ~66% shot, police responsible ~79% of deaths, ~96% of injuries). 2021 finalised official recognised-martyrs list = ~**129** dead. OHCHR earlier ~147 + ~72 prison deaths. The gap is about recognition/vetting criteria, not about who did the killing. Both endpoints sourced; range 129–338.
- **Holocaust-in-Tunisia toll (tun-1):** loosely reported — Yad Vashem-linked summary ~265 camp deaths; USHMM gives camp/conscription counts but NO death toll; Montreal Holocaust Museum declines a comprehensive toll. Left null + UNVERIFIED because no single corroborated count.
- **Slave-trade scale (tun-3):** underlying estimates diverge hard — Bader ~100,000 Black Africans (1700–1880); Valensi ~7,000 resident slaves/descendants (1861); Ferrière ~167,000 slaves+freed (1848); Austen ~100,000 total. The ~1–1.25M European figure is a **COMBINED** Algiers+Tunis+Tripoli total (chiefly Robert Davis, contested) — explicitly NOT attributed to Tunis alone in the entry.

## What could NOT be verified

- A firm death toll for the Holocaust in Tunisia (held null, UNVERIFIED).
- Any death count for the slavery institution (null by nature — it is captivity/trafficking, not a dated massacre).
- Precise Tunisian casualties from the 1881 French conquest (Wikipedia gives ~200–400 Tunisian dead campaign-wide; Sfax bombardment Tunisian casualties "undetermined"). I did NOT include 1881 as a standalone event — Bizerte (1961, ~630) is the better-documented colonial-violence victim entry, and adding 1881 would have pushed past the 1–3-per-ledger guidance with a weaker toll. Noted here for completeness.

## Asymmetry note

Tunisia's perpetrator footprint is real but bounded: it is dominated by (a) enslavement — a multi-generational pre-colonial institution with no clean death count — and (b) a state shooting its own protesters, not by external conquests or genocides. Tolls here are modest by atlas standards and one resists a death figure entirely. This is stated in the country `note`. No event, toll or source was invented to balance the ledgers.

## Audit

Adversarial source-integrity + tier audit, 2026-06-18 (assume-fake-until-proven posture). Every source re-fetched/re-searched against its claimed content.

**Verdict: country UNVERIFIED** (1 of 4 events UNVERIFIED). 3 events upgraded RESEARCHED → VERIFIED; 1 (tun-1) stays UNVERIFIED.

### Per-event findings

- **tun-1 (Nazi occupation, POL) — UNVERIFIED (kept).** SOURCE-INTEGRITY FAILURE found and corrected. The Yad Vashem source cite asserted "of Tunisia's ~10,000-strong forced-labour pool, ~265 died in the camps" and was tagged `supports: toll.high`. The actual Yad Vashem article says ~5,000 Jews in ~32 camps and that "tens" died at Bizerte/Mateur — NO 10,000 pool, NO 265 figure. The "265" could not be corroborated in ANY source consulted; the only hard count found is the EHRI Portal's "at least 46 Jews are known to have died." The 265 figure was removed as unsubstantiated, the cite rewritten to match the real text (supports → summary), an EHRI reference added (supports → toll.note) for the lone hard count, and a QUARANTINE note added to toll.note. Camp count (~32–40, USHMM/Yad Vashem) and ~5,000 conscription figure are real and confirmed (USHMM + Montreal + Yad Vashem). Toll stays null; event stays UNVERIFIED — correct per law 1 (no agreed independent death count). Tier POL re-derived and upheld: racial-persecution military occupation cut short before extermination; STR-vs-EXT rule satisfied (it is already POL, not mislabelled EXT, and the disease deaths are incidental to forced labour, not the defining mechanism).
- **tun-2 (Bizerte Crisis, WAR) — VERIFIED.** Wikipedia confirms 630 Tunisian / 24 French dead, 1,555 Tunisian wounded, Ferryville shelled (27 killed), 800 paratroopers + jets + tanks/armoured cars + 3 offshore cruisers, three-day July 1961 battle, base abandoned 15 Oct 1963. Encyclopedia.com corroborates the blockade/course/outcome but does NOT itself carry the exact dates or casualties (caveat tightened to say so). Toll is a single point figure (low=high=630) from the Tunisian official balance sheet — flagged as single-source/single-number in the caveat; it is the standard figure of record, no higher sourced count exists. Tier WAR upheld (short sharp inter-state armed conflict).
- **tun-3 (Slavery, STR) — VERIFIED.** All scale figures confirmed against Wikipedia "Slavery in Tunisia": Bader ~100,000 Black Africans (1700–1880), Valensi ~7,000 (1861), Ferrière ~167,000 (1848), Austen ~100,000; staged abolition (export ban 1841, Tunis market closed Aug 1842, full abolition 23 Jan 1846) + reinforcing decree 1890 (Ali III Bey). Barbary ~1–1.25M Europeans confirmed as a COMBINED Algiers+Tunis+Tripoli total attributed to Robert Davis and explicitly contested (Earle, Wright) — correctly NOT pinned on Tunis. Ottoman-era source confirms corsair/captive/ransom authorship and that Tunis was less prominent than Algiers (consistent with the entry). Toll null by nature (enslavement, not a dated killing) — correct, no invented count. Tier STR upheld (multi-generational structural captivity institution).
- **tun-4 (2011 uprising, POL) — VERIFIED.** Egypt Independent confirms Bouderbala 2012: 338 killed, 2,489 cases, 66% shot, police 79% deaths / 96% injuries — BUT the wounded figure is 2,174, not the "2,147" in the JSON (transposed digits); CORRECTED in toll.note and the source cite. Al Arabiya 129/634 (2021 official martyrs list) was 403-blocked to WebFetch but independently confirmed via Asharq Al-Awsat, Middle East Monitor and New Arab. Amnesty MDE 30/011/2011 confirmed real (visited Kasserine/Thala/Regueb/Bizerte/Tunis; Public Order Brigade / BOP named as believed responsible for most killings; corroborates OHCHR ~147 + 72 prison deaths). Both toll endpoints sourced — genuine documentary dispute over recognition criteria, not a single-source toll. Tier POL upheld (state shooting its own protesters).
