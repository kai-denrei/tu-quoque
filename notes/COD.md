## Dual-lens status
Two ledgers present and balanced: one victim event (Congo Free State) and two perpetrator events (Kongo slave trade, Congo Wars). No fabrication needed.

## cod-1 — Congo Free State (1885–1908) — VICTIM — RESEARCHED
- Tier POL: forced-labor extraction regime that killed through hostage-taking, mutilation, starvation and quota terror — atrocity for plunder, not deliberate destruction of a group as such. NOT EXT.
- Toll set to a range 1.2M–10M, marked disputed.
  - Low ~1.2M: Jean-Paul Sanderson's 2020 demographic reconstruction ("most likely", plausible 1–5M).
  - High ~10M: Hochschild, *King Leopold's Ghost* — but this is a population DECLINE (includes disease, collapsed birth rate, flight), not a counted body count. Flagged in the toll note.
- Key caveat captured: no baseline census before 1924, so every figure is an extrapolation; Vansina later distanced himself from the 50%-loss thesis. Spread of estimates (Casement 3M, Forbath 5M+, Ndaywel è Nziem 5–10M) noted via the Wikipedia atrocities survey.
- received_lens: memorial (DRC public memory treats it as foundational trauma).

## cod-2 — Kongo Kingdom & Atlantic slave trade (16th–18th c.) — PERPETRATOR — UNVERIFIED
- Tier POL: Kongo kings/nobles captured, taxed and sold people for profit and power. Documented institutional facts: Afonso I's letters to João III (complaining nobles kidnapped freeborn Kongolese and broke his royal monopoly — not that slaving was wrong); a 1645 household tax payable in one slave/year; ~62,000 sold 1780–1790 during civil war.
- Toll deliberately left null and event marked UNVERIFIED. The harm metric here is enslavement, not a discrete death count. West Central Africa (Kongo–Angola coast + hinterland) was the LARGEST embarkation region of the whole Atlantic trade — ~5.7M of ~12.5M embarked (Slave Voyages database) — but that total spans many polities and centuries and counts embarkations, not deaths. Attributing a specific death toll to Kongo's elite slaving is not sourceable, so per Law 2 it stays null/UNVERIFIED rather than invented.
- received_lens: suppressed (African-elite complicity is under-acknowledged in public memory).

## cod-3 — The Congo Wars (1996–2003) — PERPETRATOR — RESEARCHED
- Tier WAR: armed conflict drawing in nine states + dozens of militias; most deaths from war-induced collapse of health/food systems, plus mass atrocity throughout.
- Toll range 0.9M–5.4M, marked disputed — this is the central numbers fight:
  - High 5.4M: IRC 2008 mortality survey (Aug 1998–Apr 2007); IRC's own stated range is 3M–7.6M; only ~350,000+ deaths were directly violent (1998–2001), the rest disease/malnutrition.
  - Low ~863,000: Human Security Report Project (Human Security Report 2009/2010, Simon Fraser University) recomputed IRC's last three surveys from ~2.83M to under 900,000 after correcting the pre-war baseline mortality and survey-area selection.
- received_lens: contested.

### cod-1 — Congo Free State (VICTIM) → VERIFIED
- Hochschild, *King Leopold's Ghost* (1998), ~10M (toll.high): EXISTS, supports claim. Confirmed via Wikipedia "King Leopold's Ghost" that 10M is a population DECLINE extrapolated from a 1924 census + a 1919 Belgian commission estimate of a halved population (per Jean Stengers), NOT a counted body count. Caveat (popular history, explicit moral case, challenged by demographers) is accurate and discloses bias. Bonus finding: the 10M figure was originally shared by Vansina before his downward revision — caveat could note this but is not wrong.
- Sanderson, 2020 demographic reconstruction, ~1.2M most-likely / 1–5M range (toll.low): EXISTS, supports claim. Confirmed via Wikipedia "Atrocities in the Congo Free State" — "1.2 million the most likely estimate," plausible range "one to five million." Accurate.
- Estimate spread (Casement 3M, Forbath 5M+, Ndaywel è Nziem 5–10M, Vansina revision): all confirmed in the same article (also Gunther 5–8M, Lemkin 75%). "No census before 1924" confirmed verbatim. Summary source supports the claim.
- Both toll ends sourced (✓ not single-source). Tier independently re-derived BEFORE reading assigned: extractive forced-labor regime, killing for plunder, no group-destruction intent → POL. Matches assigned. Not EXT (no intent to destroy a people as such), not STR (disease was downstream of the predatory regime, not unintended structural collapse). NO FLAG.

### cod-3 — Congo Wars (PERPETRATOR) → VERIFIED
- IRC 2008 mortality survey, 5.4M / range 3M–7.6M (toll.high): EXISTS. The 3M–7.6M range confirmed from IRC's own written statement ("could be as low as 3 million or as high as 7.6 million"). "Less than 10 percent of deaths due to violence" corroborates the disease/malnutrition framing and the ~350,000 violent-deaths figure. Caveat discloses IRC's advocacy/fundraising bias and that its methodology was formally challenged. Accurate. (ReliefWeb page returns 403 on direct fetch, but figures corroborated via CBC, VOA, PMC/BMJ, IRC's own statement — NOT single-source.)
- HSRP, Human Security Report 2009/2010 (Simon Fraser U.), recompute to under 900,000 (toll.low): EXISTS. Confirmed via HuffPost (quoting HSRP director Andrew Mack): the ~900,000 figure came from substituting a HIGHER baseline mortality rate into the IRC's "low" estimate of 2.83M — exactly matching the toll.note's "fell from ~2.83M to under 900,000 (~863,000)." Methodological criticism (too-low baseline using the sub-Saharan-Africa average; unrepresentative survey-area selection) confirmed. NBC headline "Review of Congo war halves death toll" corroborates. (Science/AAAS and NBC URLs both 403 on direct fetch; dispute fully corroborated through HuffPost + VOA + CBC search results. Not single-source.)
- ~350,000 direct violent deaths 1998–2001 (summary, Wikipedia "Second Congo War"): confirmed verbatim ("350,000+ violent deaths (1998–2001)"). Belligerents (DRC/Angola/Zimbabwe/Namibia/Chad pro-gov; Rwanda/Uganda/Burundi + rebels) confirmed; "nine African nations + ~25 armed groups" confirmed.
- Both toll ends sourced (✓). Tier re-derived: multi-state armed conflict, most deaths from WAR-INDUCED collapse of health/food systems → WAR. Matches assigned. NOTE on the STR-vs-EXT rule: the disease-driven mortality here is war-induced (active conflict shattering infrastructure), which is armed-conflict excess mortality = WAR, not STR; STR would apply to peacetime structural mortality absent active warfare. NO FLAG.
