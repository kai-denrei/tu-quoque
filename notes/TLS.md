## Ledger shape and the core asymmetry

This is a textbook asymmetric case, and the asymmetry is the honest finding — not a gap to be papered over.

- **Done to them (victim) — 3 events, the overwhelming weight.** The Indonesian occupation (1975-1999) is one of the late twentieth century's largest atrocities relative to population. Two set-piece events sit inside/alongside it: the 1991 Santa Cruz massacre and the 1999 post-referendum scorched-earth campaign.
- **In their name (perpetrator) — 2 events, both internal.** Timor-Leste has been a sovereign state only since 20 May 2002. It has committed NO mass atrocity against another people. The only honest perpetrator entries are Timorese-on-Timorese: the 1975 UDT-FRETILIN civil war and the 2006 security-force crisis. Both are real, both belong in the ledger, and both are an order of magnitude smaller than the victim side. I did NOT invent or inflate anything to balance the columns; the asymmetry is stated plainly in the country `note`.

## What I found (events)

1. **tls-1 — Indonesian occupation 1975-1999 (EXT, victim).** CAVR ('Chega!', 2005) central minimum ~102,800 conflict-related deaths (±12,000): ~18,600 violent killings/disappearances + ~84,000 excess hunger/illness deaths (upper bound ~183,000 for hunger/illness). ~70% of documented violent killings attributed to Indonesian forces. Higher totals: Amnesty/HRW ~200,000 (~a third of pre-invasion pop), Kiernan ~150,000, Defert ~308,000 (1975-81). Cribb argues a lower floor (~100,000 or less). toll.low = 102,800 (CAVR central min); toll.high = 200,000 (widely cited). The statistical core was produced by HRDAG/Benetech for CAVR — used their report as the primary/scholarly anchor.

2. **tls-2 — Santa Cruz / Dili massacre 1991 (POL, victim).** ~250 killed at/after the cemetery (one repeated tally: 271 killed, ~278 wounded, ~270 disappeared); Indonesian govt initially claimed only 19. Some lower estimates (50-100) for the cemetery itself. Filmed by Max Stahl. toll.low 250 / toll.high 271.

3. **tls-3 — 1999 post-referendum scorched-earth (POL, victim).** ~1,400+ civilians killed before/after the 30 Aug vote; ~80% of Dili destroyed; hundreds of thousands deported to West Timor. Militias directed/backed by TNI; the 2008 Commission of Truth and Friendship found Indonesia institutionally responsible. Liquiçá church massacre (April 1999, up to ~100) cited as a representative set-piece. toll.low/high = 1,400/1,500 (narrow band around the standard figure, not a major scholarly dispute).

4. **tls-4 — 1975 UDT-FRETILIN civil war (POL, perpetrator).** CAVR estimate ~1,500-3,000 killed Aug-Sep 1975; killings by both parties' supporters (purges, prisoner killings, revenge murders); leaders reportedly lost control of armed followers. Politically sensitive — sometimes invoked to justify the Indonesian invasion. toll.low/high = 1,500/3,000.

5. **tls-5 — 2006 security-force crisis (POL, perpetrator).** UN Independent Special Commission of Inquiry (Pinheiro/Holtsman/Zacklin) + reporting: ~37 killed in Apr-May fighting, ~150,000 displaced. On 25 May, F-FDTL soldiers shot 8-10 unarmed PNTL police (and wounded ~27) after a ceasefire. Commission found ministers Rogério Lobato (Interior) and Roque Rodrigues (Defence) and defence chief Taur Matan Ruak illegally transferred weapons to civilians; recommended prosecutions. toll.low = 8 (police killed 25 May), toll.high = 37 (broader crisis).

## Contested / uncertain

- **Occupation total death toll** is the big dispute: CAVR central ~102,800 vs. the popularly cited ~200,000 vs. Defert's ~308,000 vs. Cribb's lower floor. Shown as a sourced range with the dispute flagged.
- **Santa Cruz** exact count: witness/HR-group ~250-271 vs. the Indonesian state's 19; range reflects the credible (non-state) estimates.
- **2006**: an unverified claim that loyalist soldiers shot ~60 civilians on 28 April was NEVER substantiated — explicitly EXCLUDED from the toll and noted as such.
