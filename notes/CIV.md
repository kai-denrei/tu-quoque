## The dual-lens shape
CIV's two ledgers collapse onto the same recent decade. The 2010-2011 post-election crisis is BOTH the best-quantified victim event (~3,000 civilians killed) AND the source of the worst perpetrator atrocity (Duekoue, by the pro-Ouattara FRCI). The victim/perpetrator line runs between the rival armies, not between communities. Deeper victim wound = French colonial conquest + forced labour, but its toll is unrecoverable.

## Contested / what diverges
- **Duekoue toll is the sharpest dispute:** UN spokesman/Ouattara govt ~330; ICRC (Red Cross) "at least 800" in Carrefour neighbourhood alone; UN human-rights division 505 in Duekoue / 944 in wider Moyen-Cavally region; Caritas 800-1,000. Range set 330 (low) to 1,000 (high), disputed:true.
- **Duekoue attribution:** UN found mixed responsibility — of one ~330 count, >100 by Gbagbo troops, majority by pro-Ouattara dozos. HRW attributes the bulk of Guere killings to pro-Ouattara forces. Both sides accused; primary agent = pro-Ouattara side.
- **2010-11 overall toll:** ~3,000 is consensus ("at least," so a floor). Early-crisis interim counts (1,000-1,500) reflect only the opening months before full-scale war.
