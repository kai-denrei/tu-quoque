# Papua New Guinea (PNG) — research log

Built from scratch on 2026-06-19. Web tools (WebSearch/WebFetch) loaded and used for real research. Three events: two victim, one perpetrator. Wrapper status UNVERIFIED because both victim-side aggregate tolls could not be sourced as numbers.

## Framing decision

PNG's record is asymmetric and the asymmetry is left honest, per the AUS precedent in this corpus:

- **Victim side** = harms by OUTSIDE powers (Japanese WWII occupation; German colonial labour regime). Both are well-attested as systems of violence but neither has a defensible aggregate death count. Tolls left null/disputed with sourced partial figures kept explicit; events marked UNVERIFIED.
- **Perpetrator side** = one dominant, well-documented case: the Bougainville conflict/blockade (1988–1998), the independent PNG state and PNGDF against a part of its own population. Strong sourcing → RESEARCHED.

No event, toll or source was invented to balance the ledgers.

## Events

### png-1 — Japanese occupation of New Guinea (1942–1945), victim, WAR
- Documented pattern: Japanese troops requisitioned village food at gunpoint, conscripted forced labour, killed resisters. Allied (Australian) side forcibly recruited ~37,000 New Guineans as carriers.
- **Toll: null/null, disputed, UNVERIFIED.** No systematic aggregate count of indigenous war deaths exists.
- Sourced partial figures kept in note: National WWII Museum's "in some parts of the island one in four villagers died" (area-specific, NOT territory-wide); JMVH peer-reviewed survey's ≥2,024 ANGAU indigenous carrier/labourer deaths (~5% of 35,000+, mainly malaria/dysentery), explicitly from incomplete records.
- Tol Plantation massacre (Feb 1942) was investigated and considered but EXCLUDED as a PNG victim event — the ~158 killed were Australian POWs (Lark Force), not PNG indigenous people; it belongs to the AUS victim story, not PNG's.

### png-2 — German colonial forced labour & punitive expeditions (1884–1914), victim, STR
- Documented structure: chartered company then colony; poll tax + compulsory-labour requirement drove men onto copra plantations under indenture; military expeditions 1899–1914 to subdue resisting areas; Sokehs rebellion of labourers crushed 1911 by 4 warships + 745 troops.
- **Toll: null/null, disputed, UNVERIFIED.** No reliable systematic death count.
- COULD NOT VERIFY: secondary summaries (Grokipedia, military-history fandom wiki) cite plantation-labour death rates of ~15% per contract / "over a quarter of recruits died," but these are not anchored in a reliable citable primary tally, so NO number is asserted. The reliable Wikipedia German New Guinea article does NOT carry these mortality figures. Deliberately left out per inviolable law 1.

### png-3 — Bougainville conflict & blockade (1988–1998), perpetrator, WAR
- Root cause: landowner grievances over the Panguna copper mine (>45% of PNG export revenue). PNGDF vs Bougainville Revolutionary Army; PNG government sea/air blockade from 1990 cut food, fuel and — critically — medicine.
- **Toll: 1,000 (low) – 20,000 (high), disputed.** Dispute is over SCOPE, not authorship.
  - LOW (1,000–2,000): conservative combat-death estimate (Wikipedia, summarising more conservative sources).
  - HIGH (12,000–20,000 total; 15,000–20,000 most accepted locally; Australian government assessed 15,000–20,000 "could have died"): includes blockade-driven preventable disease + starvation, the majority of deaths. Pre-war Bougainville population ~160,000.
- Peer-reviewed anchor (Int. J. Mental Health Systems 2016, PMC4776395): 2,023 preventable-disease deaths from incomplete data of just 18 of 23 health centres, Jan 1990–Jul 1991 ALONE; ~67,300 internally displaced into "care centres."
- Amnesty International (ASA 34/01/1997): ≥62 extrajudicial executions + ≥13 disappearances by PNGDF/Resistance Force since Nov 1993; widespread village burning; journalists/monitors barred from the island. Corroborated by US State Dept human-rights reports 1996–1997.

### Source integrity — every source falsification-tested

- **png-1 / National WWII Museum, "Angels and Victims" (Ed Lengel, PhD, pub. 2020-08-09)** — VERIFIED real. Confirmed verbatim: "in some parts of the island during the war, one in four of native villagers would die from starvation, disease, military action, or murder"; "Japanese forces... often simply requisitioned all the food they wanted, murdering some villagers and leaving the rest to starve"; "As many as 37,000 New Guineans were working as forced labor at any given time." Supports the summary claim. Caveat correctly flags the 'one in four' as area-specific, not territory-wide.
- **png-1 / JMVH, Shanks (Vol. 31 No. 3, 2023)** — VERIFIED real. Confirmed: "At least 2024 ANGAU carriers/labourers died during the War, mainly of infectious diseases such as malaria and dysentery, giving an estimated mortality of about 5%"; "At the peak in September 1944 more than 35 000 men were involved." Explicitly notes incomplete records. Supports toll.low (documented minimum, ANGAU only). Peer-reviewed; correctly cited.
- **png-1 / Wikipedia "Japanese occupation of New Guinea"** — VERIFIED real. Confirms occupation chronology + brutality/forced-labour pattern (Hollandia/Lake Sentani airfields by manual labour; killings of non-compliant villagers). No aggregate indigenous toll, as the caveat states. Supports tier. Tertiary — appropriately flagged.
- **png-2 / Wikipedia "German New Guinea"** — VERIFIED real. Confirms 1884 New Guinea Company → colony; "four weeks of labour per person annually and payment of a poll tax in cash"; "military expeditions... from 1899 to 1914"; Sokehs put down by "4 warships with 745 troops" (Jan–Feb 1911). No aggregate death figure. Supports summary.
- **png-2 / Encyclopedia.com "Bismarck Archipelago"** — VERIFIED real. Confirms Gazelle Peninsula Tolai copra plantations from the 1870s; German land appropriation (40% of arable land) and "wars of pacification with the Tolai people in the 1890s." No death toll. Supports tier (plantation/forced-labour context).
- **png-3 / IJMHS 2016, Tierney et al.** — VERIFIED real. Confirms verbatim: "Estimates of the total number of deaths vary from 12,000 to 20,000 from a pre-war population of 160,000"; "Incomplete data from 18 of 23 Health Centres reported 2023 deaths from normally preventable diseases in the period January 1990–July 1991"; "67,300 were internally displaced into care centres"; "blockade of all goods including medical supplies May 1990–September 1994." Supports toll.high. Note: data file says "2,023" preventable-disease deaths; the source says 2023 deaths (i.e., the number 2,023) — consistent.
- **png-3 / Wikipedia "Bougainville conflict"** — VERIFIED real. Confirms dates 1 Dec 1988–20 Apr 1998; Australian-government high estimate "15,000 and 20,000 people could have died"; "lower estimates place the toll at around 1,000–2,000"; Panguna mine (>45% of PNG export revenue); blockade + village burnings. Supports toll.low; high estimate correctly attributed to the Australian government.
- **png-3 / Amnesty International ASA 34/001/1997** — VERIFIED real despite HTTP 403 on amnesty.org, its hosted PDF, and the Refworld mirror. Existence + exact figures confirmed verbatim via search indexing: "Since November 1993 Amnesty International has documented at least 62 cases of extrajudicial execution and at least 13 'disappearances' by the PNG Defence Force (PNGDF) or the government-backed paramilitary Resistance Force"; "Forty-four people... killed or 'disappeared' in 1996 alone." Cross-checked against an Internet Archive mirror, Refworld, and Policy Commons listings. Bias (advocacy NGO; documented minimums) now explicitly disclosed in the caveat. Supports summary. The 403 is anti-bot blocking, not non-existence — caveat updated accordingly.

No fabricated sources found. Every citation resolves to a real, fetchable-or-mirrored work whose content matches the attached claim.
