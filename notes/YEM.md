# Yemen (YEM) — research notes

Built from scratch 2026-06-18 with live web research (WebSearch + WebFetch). No seed file existed.

## Ledger shape
- 1 victim event, 2 perpetrator events.
- The asymmetry is the opposite of what one might expect: Yemen's most famous catastrophe (the 2015–present war) is overwhelmingly a *victim* story, but its largest single perpetrator of direct civilian deaths is **Saudi Arabia / the coalition** — a foreign power — so that perpetrator weight belongs in SAU, not YEM. Yemen's own perpetrator record is the 1986 intra-party bloodbath and the Houthis' wartime conduct.

## Victim ledger
- **yem-1 — The war since 2015 (STR).** Anchored on the **UNDP estimate of ~377,000 total deaths to end-2021**: ~150,000 from direct violence, ~223,000–227,000 from hunger, disease, and healthcare collapse. Low/high set to 150,000 / 377,000 (direct vs all-causes). Save the Children's ~85,000 child-starvation figure (2015–2018) and the 2.5M+ cholera caseload noted in the toll note. Tier is STR because the majority of deaths are famine/disease, not bombs. Status RESEARCHED.
  - Attribution caveat baked into the entry: ~67% of *direct* civilian fatalities attributed to coalition airstrikes (per GCR2P / Yemen Data Project), but the event is framed as harm DONE TO Yemen's people.

## Perpetrator ledger
- **yem-2 — The Events of '86 (POL).** January 1986 South Yemen civil war: ruling Yemeni Socialist Party split into Ali Nasir Muhammad's (az-Zomrah) and Abdul Fattah Ismail's (at-Toghmah) factions; politburo shootout on 13 Jan detonated 11 days of tank/artillery war in Aden. Toll **4,000–10,000** (≈3,800 first day; ~60,000 fled north). Senior dead: Abdul Fattah Ismail, Ali Ahmad Nasir Antar, Saleh Muslih Qassem. Status RESEARCHED.
- **yem-3 — Houthi siege of Taiz + indiscriminate shelling (WAR).** Roads into Taiz closed since 2015; artillery/mortar/rocket/ballistic-missile fire into populated areas in Taiz, Ma'rib, Aden; landmines the UN Panel of Experts called "endemic and systematic." Toll endpoints left **null** — only partial incident counts exist (678 Ma'rib landmine casualties; HRW's 7 Houthi-Saleh attacks killing 139) and they cannot be honestly summed. Status UNVERIFIED.

## What is contested / could NOT be verified
- The war's total (377,000) is a UNDP *statistical model* of excess deaths, not a body count — marked disputed.
- The 1986 toll spans 4,000–10,000; the closed Marxist state and chaos of the fighting prevent a tighter figure.
- The Houthi siege/shelling has **no agreed total death count** — hence null endpoints and UNVERIFIED status. This is an honest null, not a gap to be filled.

## Deliberate exclusion
- The **Egyptian chemical-weapons and bombing campaign in North Yemen (1963–67)** is NOT in Yemen's perpetrator ledger — the perpetrator was Egypt (Nasser's expeditionary force), so it sits in **EGY** (confirmed already present there as egy-3). Yemenis were victims of it; that does not make Yemen the agent.

## Audit

Adversarial source-integrity + tier audit, 2026-06-18. Method: assume every citation fake until proven; attempt to FALSIFY each source against the specific claim it carries; independently re-derive each tier before reading the assigned one; require a source for BOTH ends of every contested toll.

**Country status: UNVERIFIED** (2 of 3 events verified; yem-3 held UNVERIFIED).

### yem-1 — War on Yemen since 2015 (victim, STR) — VERIFIED
- Toll endpoints both sourced and independently confirmed. UNDP "Assessing the Impact of War in Yemen" (Nov 2021): 377,000 total to end-2021, ~154,000/150,000+ direct, ~223,000 indirect (~60% of total) — corroborated by Al Jazeera, France24, ICTJ, ReliefWeb, UNDP PDF. Low (150,000 direct) and high (377,000 all-causes) carry distinct sources. Pass.
- Save the Children's ~85,000 children-under-five figure confirmed as 84,701, April 2015–October 2018, released 21 Nov 2018 (savethechildren.net + NBC/ABC/Euronews). Pass.
- GCR2P page is live and carries the exact "19,200+ civilians killed or maimed by coalition airstrikes, including over 2,300 children." Pass.
- TIER re-derived independently: ~60% of deaths are indirect (famine/disease/healthcare collapse) — this is a structural, conditions-driven die-off → STR. Correctly NOT escalated to EXT (no intent to destroy a group; disease-driven is STR by the STR-vs-EXT rule) and correctly NOT WAR (direct-violence portion alone would read WAR, but the majority cause governs). Tier confirmed STR.
- Status RESEARCHED → VERIFIED.

### yem-2 — Events of '86, South Yemen party bloodbath (perpetrator, POL) — VERIFIED
- Toll 4,000–10,000, ~3,800 first day, 11 days, ~60,000 fled, named YSP factions and victims all confirmed (South Yemeni crisis Wikipedia + MERIP). Both endpoints sourced.
- SOURCE FALSIFICATION of the MERIP cite: "Catastrophe in South Yemen" is a REAL article — author Fred Halliday (the principal Western scholar of South Yemen / PDRY), dated 12 March 1986; opening lines and subject verified on the live page. The cite has been corrected to name Halliday. Pass — no fabricated source.
- TIER re-derived: an intra-party purge that detonated into factional urban warfare in the capital — political killing of rivals plus civilians in the crossfire → POL (with a WAR component in the 11 days of tank/artillery combat). Correctly NOT EXT (no ethnic/religious destruction) and NOT STR (not famine/disease). Tier confirmed POL.
- Status RESEARCHED → VERIFIED.

### yem-3 — Houthi siege of Taiz + indiscriminate shelling (perpetrator, WAR) — UNVERIFIED (quarantined)
- Substance HOLDS and is verifiable: siege of Taiz (dedicated "Siege of Taiz" Wikipedia article exists), Houthi indiscriminate shelling of populated areas in Ma'rib/Taiz/Hodeidah/Mocha, and antipersonnel-landmine use "which may amount to war crimes" — all CONFIRMED on the live HRW World Report 2022 Yemen chapter. The HRW "seven indiscriminate Houthi-Saleh attacks killing 139, incl. ≥8 children" figure CONFIRMED present on the War crimes Wikipedia article.
- TWO load-bearing figures FAILED falsification and are quarantined:
  1. **"678 Ma'rib landmine casualties"** — NOT on the cited War crimes Wikipedia article and NOT found in any independent source. Other figures exist (~9,000 total landmine casualties since 2015 per the Civilian Impact Monitoring Project; 284 in 2020) but no 678 Ma'rib figure. Treated as unverified / possibly fabricated.
  2. **UN Panel of Experts "endemic and systematic"** quote for landmine use — exact paired phrase NOT confirmed on the cited source; Panel reports use "widespread," "indiscriminate," "systematic" but not this quotation. Treated as unverified.
- Source-attribution accuracy CORRECTED: the cited HRW 2022 chapter does NOT contain the "7 attacks/139" figure or the "Taiz roads closed since 2015" claim (those are from other HRW reporting / the War crimes article). Both yem-3 source `cite` strings rewritten to list only what each URL actually supports.
- TIER re-derived: indiscriminate wartime attacks on civilians (siege + shelling + mines) → WAR. Correctly NOT STR (the death mechanism is direct attack/siege, not a country-wide disease die-off) and NOT EXT/POL. Tier confirmed WAR; no change.
- TOLL: null/null endpoints are an HONEST null (no defensible total exists) — correct under law 1, not a fabricated balance.
- Status held UNVERIFIED: two cited fragments failed verification AND there is no defensible toll. Re-verify by sourcing or removing the 678 Ma'rib figure and the "endemic and systematic" quote.
