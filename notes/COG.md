# COG — Republic of the Congo (Congo-Brazzaville) — research log

Built from scratch (no seed). Status: RESEARCHED. 4 events: 3 victim, 1 perpetrator.

## Critical disambiguation
- This is the **Republic of the Congo (Congo-Brazzaville)**, the former **French** colony of Middle Congo / French Equatorial Africa — ISO **COG**.
- NOT the Democratic Republic of the Congo (former Belgian Congo Free State), ISO **COD**.
- The notorious "~10 million dead / half the population" figure belongs to the **Belgian** Congo Free State and was deliberately NOT borrowed for the French Congo. No comparable reconstructed number exists for the French side. This was the main contamination risk and I quarantined it explicitly in both the country note and cog-1.

## What I found

### Victim ledger
1. **cog-1 — French concessionary-company terror (c.1898–1910), POL, UNVERIFIED on toll.**
   Forced rubber/ivory extraction by private concessions; hostage camps, killings. Documented by the 1905 Brazza inquiry (Bangui hostage hut deaths; 1903 Gaud–Toqué dynamite killing of Pakpa). Report suppressed ~100 years, cut from ~1,200 to ~112 pages. No aggregate death toll has ever been reconstructed for the French Congo specifically → low/high null, event UNVERIFIED on the NUMBER only (pattern well attested).

2. **cog-2 — Congo-Océan railroad (1921–1934), STR, RESEARCHED.** Strongest-documented event.
   Built by France + Société de Construction des Batignolles, 511 km through the Mayombe. Forced conscription, transport in chains.
   - J.P. Daughton, *In the Forest of No Joy* (Norton, 2021): **at least ~20,000** dead (French + Congolese archives, company records, survivor interviews). Reported via Stanford Report.
   - Mainstream range ~15,000–23,000; unofficial estimates up to 30,000–60,000.
   - Tolled as range **15,000–60,000** (low/high anchors), central estimate ~20,000. Tier STR (conditions-of-life mass death, not a campaign to destroy a group).

### Perpetrator ledger
3. **cog-3 — "Disappeared of the Beach" (May 1999), POL, RESEARCHED.** The clean perpetrator event.
   Sassou-Nguesso's presidential guard abducted returning refugees at the Brazzaville river port ("the Beach") under a UN humanitarian corridor; presumed executed near the presidential palace.
   - "More than 350" (often **353**) disappeared; ~6,500 refugees were returning from DRC.
   - 2005 Brazzaville trial of 15 officials (incl. Republican Guard cmdr Gen. Blaise Adoua; Army Inspector-Gen. Norbert Dabira) addressed ~85–86 cases → all acquitted; state ordered to compensate relatives of 86 victims.
   - Toll logged **85–353** (low = adjudicated count, high = total disappeared).

### Across the line
4. **cog-4 — 1997–1999 civil war shelling (contemporary), WAR, RESEARCHED.**
   Logged as VICTIM event for the Congolese population, but explicitly flagged as dual-role: indiscriminate shelling of Brazzaville was done by BOTH Sassou-Nguesso's (Angolan-backed) Cobra militia AND Lissouba's government forces + Cocoye/Ninja militias.
   - War-total toll **~13,929–25,050**, 200,000+ displaced (Wikipedia aggregate; no rigorous count exists).
   - Dec 1998 Bacongo/Makélékélé bombardment: at least ~1,000 dead.
