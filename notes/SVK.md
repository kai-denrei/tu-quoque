# Slovakia (SVK) — research log

Built from scratch 2026-06-19. Status: RESEARCHED. 4 events (2 victim, 2 perpetrator).

## Dual-lens thesis
Slovakia is one of the cleanest entangled cases in the atlas. The wartime clerical-fascist
Slovak State (1939–45, President Jozef Tiso) was both the soil of the Holocaust AND its
co-author: it was the **first Axis partner to consent to deporting its own Jews**, and the
**only state that paid Germany to take them** (500 RM/head, >30 million RM total) believing it
was buying resettlement. The same Hlinka Guard that rounded up Jews in 1942 reconstituted as the
POHG emergency divisions in 1944–45 and stood beside German Einsatzkommandos at the Kremnicka and
Nemecka graves. Slovak Jews therefore appear in BOTH ledgers — as the destroyed, and as the
people their own government paid to have destroyed.

## Events
- **svk-1 (victim, EXT):** Holocaust in Slovakia. ~89,000 Jews in 1940 census; ~69,000 killed.
  First wave ~57–58k (Mar–Oct 1942), second wave ~12.6k (USHMM) / ~13.5k (Wikipedia) after the
  Sept 1944 German invasion. Toll band 68,000–69,000.
- **svk-2 (victim, WAR):** Reprisals after the Slovak National Uprising (Sept 1944–Mar 1945).
  ~4,000–5,300 killed in occupation/reprisal; 100+ villages destroyed. Separate ~3,000 insurgents
  died in the fighting; ~30,000 Slovaks deported to camps.
- **svk-3 (perpetrator, EXT):** Slovak State's 1942 deportation of its own Jews. ~57–58k deported,
  nearly all killed. 500 RM/deportee paid to Germany; first/only Axis state to pay. Agents: Tiso,
  PM Tuka, Hlinka Guard, gendarmerie. Deaths overlap svk-1 — entered to attribute STATE AGENCY,
  not as an additional toll.
- **svk-4 (perpetrator, EXT):** Kremnicka (747) + Nemecka (~900) massacres, winter 1944–45.
  ~1,600–1,650 total. Carried out by Hlinka Guard Emergency Divisions (POHG) WITH German
  Einsatzkommando 14 (Einsatzgruppe H). ~half the Kremnicka dead Jewish; also Roma, partisans,
  a few Allied servicemen. Overlaps svk-2 reprisal toll — recorded here because Slovak units took part.

## Double-counting handling
The Holocaust toll appears on both sides (svk-1 victim / svk-3 perpetrator) and the reprisal toll
on both (svk-2 victim / svk-4 perpetrator). In each case the perpetrator entry attributes Slovak
agency for a subset of the same dead; the toll notes explicitly say the figures overlap and are not
additive. This is the intended dual-lens structure, not inflation.

## Contested / uncertain
- 1942 deportation count: sources give 57,000–58,000; Wikipedia states the exact number is
  unknown "due to discrepancies in the sources."
- Total Holocaust dead: USHMM says ">60,000 of >70,000 deported" murdered; the per-country
  consensus figure is ~68,000–69,000 (used as the band).
- Second-wave deportations: USHMM ~12,600; Wikipedia ~13,500.
- Reprisal dead: 4,000 (NWWII Museum low) to 5,300 (NWWII Museum high) / "up to 5,000" (Wikipedia).
- Nemecka toll is an estimate (~900) because the bodies were cremated.
- The ~45,000 Jews in the southern districts ceded to Hungary in 1938 were deported by Hungary in
  1944 — NOT counted here; they belong to Hungary's record.

## Could NOT verify / left out
- A precise single combat-death figure for the uprising itself (one source gives ~3,000 insurgents;
  not pinned to a second source, so noted but not made a toll).
- Roma death toll specific to Slovakia (Slovak Roma persecuted, some killed at Nemecka/Kremnicka,
  but no clean sourced national Roma toll found — folded into svk-4's mixed-victim note rather than
  given its own event with an invented number).

- **svk-3 / Wikipedia 'The Holocaust in Slovakia'** — VERIFIED: ~58,000 deported; "agreed to pay
