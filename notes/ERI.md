# Eritrea (ERI) — research log

Built from scratch on 2026-06-19. Real web research (WebSearch + WebFetch). No seed file existed.

## Shape of the entry
- **Victim ledger (2):** (eri-1) Ethiopian counter-insurgency massacres across the 1961–1991 War of Independence — umbrella event folding in Ona/Besikdira (1970), Asmara (1975) and others; (eri-2) the She'eb massacre (1988) as a discrete, well-known event.
- **Perpetrator ledger (2):** (eri-3) crimes against humanity by the Eritrean state itself per the 2016 UN Commission of Inquiry (enslavement via indefinite national service, torture, disappearance, murder since 1991); (eri-4) the Axum massacre by Eritrean Defence Forces in Ethiopia's Tigray war (Nov 2020).

The dual lens is unusually sharp here: the liberation movement that endured the war's atrocities became, in power, the subject of a UN crimes-against-humanity finding and the author of the Axum massacre — victim and perpetrator within one generation.

## What is solid
- **Axum massacre (eri-4):** strongest-evidenced perpetrator event. Independent, mutually corroborating investigations by Amnesty International, Human Rights Watch and the Ethiopian Human Rights Commission, all naming the EDF (identified by licence plates, uniforms, Tigrinya dialect). Status RESEARCHED.
- **UN CoI 2016 (eri-3):** crimes-against-humanity finding and the 300,000–400,000 "enslaved" estimate (chief investigator Mike Smith) are well attested via OHCHR/UN News and corroborated by HRW and Amnesty. Marked UNVERIFIED only because there is no aggregate DEATH toll — left null with a note.
- **War-of-independence death band (eri-1):** ~60,000–90,000 Eritrean dead is the commonly cited range; war-wide ~100,000–200,000. Wide because no wartime census.
- **She'eb date:** confirmed May 1988 (the massacres-list page had a stray "December 1988" entry; the dedicated She'eb article gives May).

## What is contested / could NOT be verified
- **High-end victim tolls.** The largest figures for Ona/Besikdira ("nearly 1,000") and Asmara 1975 (~3,000 in one day, ~11,000 over six weeks) come substantially from the present Eritrean government's own outlets (Ministry of Information / shabait.com) and diaspora commemoration sites — parties with a clear interest in maximising the toll and casting Ethiopia as sole villain. These are used only as the contested upper bound and flagged with a mandatory caveat; eri-1 and eri-2 are marked UNVERIFIED for the toll metric.
- **Independent corroboration of the lower bands** for the specific 1970/1975 massacres exists via the Wikipedia massacres list (Ona ~800+, Besikdira mosque ~120, Asmara ~3,000), but that too is a tertiary aggregator drawing partly on Eritrean sources.
- **Aggregate death toll of the Eritrean state's post-1991 repression** (deaths in secret detention, in custody, fleeing the country) is genuinely unsourceable — left null in eri-3.
- **Axum top figure (~800, AP)** is the single highest estimate and not confirmed by the rights groups, who give "over 200" / "well over 200." Range kept at 200–800 with the AP figure flagged.

## Audit

### Source-by-source verification
- **eri-1 (WoI massacres).** Wikipedia massacres-list confirmed verbatim: Ona 1970-12-01 "800+", Besikdira 1970-11-30 "120" (mosque), Asmara 1975-02-14 "3000", She'eb "400+". Wikipedia "Eritrean War of Independence" infobox now gives Eritrean combatants 65,000–70,000, Ethiopian ~100,000, **civilians 150,000–280,000**, displaced 440,000–600,000+ — i.e. the entry's "war-wide ~100,000–200,000" is conservative on the civilian side, not inflated. shabait.com "Ona massacre" page exists and gives Ona 800+/Besikdira 220; it does NOT contain the Asmara figures. The Asmara ~11,000-over-six-weeks figure lives at a SECOND shabait article, "The unforgettable massacres" (shabait.com/2020/02/21/...), which the cite named but did not link. **Fix applied:** the second URL is now spelled out in the cite and an audit note records that both interested-state articles were located and carry their claimed figures. Interested-source caveat already present and correct.
- **eri-2 (She'eb).** Wikipedia She'eb article confirmed: 12 May 1988, Derg/Ethiopian army, ~400 in town + at least 100 nearby, killed by tanks and shooting, many elderly/children. Toll traced to an ACADEMIC source — Brenda K. Uekert, *Rivers of Blood: A Comparative Study of Government Massacres* — not an Eritrean-nationalist outlet, which strengthens the figure. **Flag:** single-source event; the lone source anchors toll.high (500), while toll.low (200) is an inference with no independent source. Quarantine note added; kept UNVERIFIED on single-source grounds.
- **eri-3 (UN CoI / state CAH).** UN News story 531522 confirmed: crimes-against-humanity finding, all the listed crime categories, "widespread and systematic" since 1991, Mike Smith named. HRW 2015 "Scathing UN Report" page exists and corroborates. The 300,000–400,000-enslaved estimate is independently confirmed as Smith's. **Correction applied:** the cite called Smith "chief investigator"; he was the COMMISSION CHAIR — fixed in both cite and note. Death metric genuinely null/unsourceable; kept UNVERIFIED for that metric only.
- **eri-4 (Axum).** Strongest event. Amnesty (240+ named victims, "may amount to crime against humanity"), HRW (200+ on 28–29 Nov, 345 total), Wikipedia Axum (EHRC 100+, AP 800) all verified and mutually corroborating; EDF perpetrator confirmed. Both ends of the 200–800 range are independently sourced (HRW/Amnesty floor; AP ceiling, flagged uncorroborated). **Upgraded RESEARCHED → VERIFIED.**
